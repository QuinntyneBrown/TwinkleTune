How it works

From “maybe” to
one more song!

01 FIND MY VOICE

Start with the singer,
not the song.

Twinkle guides a one-minute low-to-high voice game. The comfortable range saves on the device, ready to tune every melody that follows.

  • ✓ Playful, simple prompts
  • ✓ Adjustable any time
  • ✓ No recording leaves the browser
★ That’s your note!A3 · right in your range

02 PICK & SING

Familiar songs,
shifted their way.

A friendly songbook shows which melodies are ready. During singing, syllables glide toward Twinkle while the star follows the voice in real time.

  • ✓ Public-domain childhood favourites
  • ✓ Live, octave-friendly pitch play
  • ✓ Practice tricky bits more slowly
✓ In your key

03 CELEBRATE

Finish feeling
ten feet tall.

Every performance ends with a celebration of bravery and growth. Stars, sparkle totals, and gentle invitations make the next step feel exciting.

  • ✓ Courage-first results
  • ✓ Badges and daily goals
  • ✓ Warm-ups for growing confidence
★★★Brave voice!
