Foundation

Layout

Every child screen is one centred 470px column, on a phone, a tablet or a laptop. Breakpoints, columns and gutters exist only for grown-up pages, marketing and these docs.

Overview

TwinkleTune has two layout systems on purpose.

  • The child column. Every screen a child uses (Welcome, Home, Songs, Sing, Results, Tips, Me, Duet, Find My Voice) is one centred column, at most 470px wide (--layout-screen-max), with the sky filling the rest. Nothing reflows between a phone in her hand and a tablet on a stand, so the one big action is always in the same place.
  • The responsive grid. Grown-up pages on wide screens (family-server managers in the Grown-Ups Corner), the marketing site and this documentation use a 4, 8 or 12 column grid whose columns, gutter and margin change at the md and lg breakpoints.

The frame below is wider than a phone; the screen stays a 470px column in the middle. Side padding is --layout-margin (20px at phone width), top padding --space-7, and the bottom clears the floating nav by --layout-bottomnav-clearance.

  • Dialogs use min(max, 100%), so on the narrowest phones they fill the scrim's padding instead of overflowing.
  • Two-up grids inside the column (.tiles, .profile-grid) stay two-up at every width; they do not become three or four columns on a tablet.

  • Margin is the space from the viewport edge to content (--layout-margin).
  • Gutter is the space between columns (--layout-gutter).
  • Container caps content at --layout-container-max (1280px) and centres it: .container.

  • .grid is repeat(var(--layout-columns), minmax(0, 1fr)) with the gutter as its gap. Card lists use .grid-auto (auto-fill with a minimum width) instead of spanning columns by hand.

Usage

  • Do not add breakpoints to child screens. If a child screen looks empty on a tablet, that is the sky doing its job.
  • Fixed parts (bottom nav, toast host) centre on the viewport and cap at the column or wide-dialog width, so they line up with the column.
  • Use 100dvh rather than 100vh for full-height layouts so mobile browser bars do not clip the action; fixed bottom parts add env(safe-area-inset-bottom).
  • Grown-up pages read the layout tokens, never their own media queries, wherever a token covers the case.
  • Content order in the DOM is reading order at every width; layout never reorders the one big action away from its place.

Behaviour per component at each breakpoint is on Responsive.

Accessibility

  • Reflow (WCAG 1.4.10). Everything works at 320 CSS px without sideways scrolling; only data tables scroll, inside .table-wrap.
  • Zoom. Breakpoints are rem, so a reader with large text gets the narrower layout sooner rather than overlapping columns.
  • Orientation (1.3.4). No screen locks to portrait or landscape; the single column works in both.
  • Landmarks. Each template has one <main>; the bottom nav and side nav are <nav> with a label.
