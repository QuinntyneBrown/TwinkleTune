Foundation

Iconography

TwinkleTune has no icon font. Its icons are emoji, which every device already draws in friendly full colour, plus two hand-drawn SVGs: Twinkle herself and the result star. This page sets their sizes, their meanings and when a screen reader should hear them.

WCAG 2.2 AAEmoji + 2 SVGsStatus: stableNo icon font, no network

Overview

Emoji suit a singing app for 5-to-10-year-olds: they are colourful, instantly recognisable, already on the device, cost no download and need no third-party request (the privacy principle). The trade-off is that each platform draws them slightly differently, so an emoji must never be the only carrier of meaning: a word always travels with it.

  • Emoji are the icon set: bottom navigation, icon buttons, song art, badges, avatars, dialog headers and the warmth at the end of a sentence.
  • Twinkle is an inline SVG (.mascot) painted with tokens, so she follows the theme. She appears on Welcome, Home, Find My Voice and in coaching dialogs.
  • The result star is the same five-point outline (.star-shape), filled gold when earned and pale when still to earn. It is never crossed out.
  • Text glyphs (✦ ★ ☆ ▶ ← ✓ ♪) are typographic marks set in the current font and colour, used for sparkles, star ratings, play, back and "in your key".

An emoji is never the tap target by itself: the button around it supplies the size. Icon buttons are 52px discs (--size-control) whatever the glyph inside.

Twinkle and the star

Both SVGs share one 120-unit star path with a 7-unit rounded stroke. All paint lives in CSS classes that read colour tokens (--color-reward-bg, --color-reward-edge, --color-mascot-face, --color-gentle-bg, --color-mark-idle), so neither SVG contains a colour value and both work in either theme.

  • Twinkle is always aria-hidden="true": her words are in the speech bubble beside her, which is real text.
  • Stars are hidden one by one; the row carries the count (aria-label="1 star earned"). An empty star means "still to earn", never "missed".
  • Do not redraw Twinkle with an emoji (⭐ or 🌟). She has a face, and the face is the brand.

Usage

  • Words first. Every emoji sits beside a word that says the same thing (🏠 Home, 🔥 7 in a row!). The only icon-only controls are back, pause, edit, play and the 🏆 high-score button, and each carries an aria-label.
  • One meaning each. 🔒 means "for grown-ups" or "still to earn", never "forbidden". ⭐ is overloaded in the mocks (Me, difficulty, a sung day, Twinkle's song art); keep each use next to its word so it never has to be decoded.
  • At most one emoji per label, at the end (New song 🎵). The exceptions are fixed pairs that are one icon: 🎤🎤 for Duet.
  • No negative icons. No ❌, ⚠️, 🚫, 👎 or 😢 anywhere a child can see. A nudge uses 💙 or 🐦; a failed server call uses 📡 and a way to keep singing.
  • Colour comes with the emoji. Do not tint emoji with CSS, and never rely on an emoji's colour (gold medal versus bronze) to carry rank: 🥇 🥈 🥉 in family high scores always sit beside the position number.
  • SVG paint lives in CSS. A new illustration follows the Twinkle pattern: classes on shapes, colours from tokens, no fill="#…".

  • Screen readers read unhidden emoji by their Unicode name ("house", "glowing star"), which turns 🏠 Home into "house Home". The mocks omit aria-hidden on the bottom-nav .ico, badge icons and the Me rows; new markup adds it.
  • Emoji carry their own colours and are exempt from text contrast, but an emoji never carries status alone (WCAG 1.4.1), so it does not need to meet 3:1.
  • Glyph controls such as the 🏆 mini button stay at least 24px with 8px between neighbours (D23).
  • In forced-colours mode emoji keep their colour; text glyphs (✦ ★ ▶) take the system text colour and stay visible.

Tokens

Iconography owns no tokens. Emoji sizes come from the type scale on Typography; disc and mascot sizes (--size-control, --size-mascot) from Shape; Twinkle's and the star's paint from Color (--color-reward-*, --color-mascot-face, --color-mark-idle*).
