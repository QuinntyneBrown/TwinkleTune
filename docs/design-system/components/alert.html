Alert

Rounded message boxes that sit in the flow of a screen or dialog and stay until they are no longer true. Twinkle's reassurance notes, the grown-ups' validation box and the family-server messages are all alerts.

Overview

Use an alert for a message that belongs to a place and must stay while it is true: why Twinkle needs her voice range, that the songbook is offline, what a grown-up must fix before a song can be saved. Unlike a toast it does not go away on a timer; unlike a dialog it does not stop anything.

The mocks have two alert boxes, kept with their class names: the why note (.why) on Find My Voice, the offline songbook and the microphone dialog, and the validation box (.errors) in the song editor. The general .alert with tones, titles, actions and dismiss is not in the mocks yet; it is added for the Grown-Ups Corner and the family-server managers, and replaces raw failure text there.

  • A message about one field goes under that field as an inline message. The alert summarises.
  • A child never sees the danger tone. Something that did not work on a child screen is a gentle (pink) alert or a why note that says what still works.

Anatomy

  1. Icon.ico: one emoji at --font-size-2xl, aria-hidden="true". The tone is always also in the words.
  2. Title (optional)strong, Baloo 2 at --font-size-md, on its own line.
  3. Message--text-body-sm in the tone's ink (--alert-fg) on the tone's tint (--alert-bg), 12 × 16px padding, --radius-lg.
  4. Action (optional)One small button, 8px below the message.
  5. Dismiss (optional).alert-dismiss: a 44px round button pushed to the end. Only for alerts that are safe to hide.

Responsive behaviour

  • Alerts fill their column (470px on child screens, the dialog's width in editors) and never sit side by side.
  • The icon stays top-aligned with the first line as the message wraps; the dismiss button stays at the end of the first line.
  • The banner spans the viewport with --layout-margin side padding and centres its content.
  • At 320px the validation box's long lines wrap; each line keeps its bullet.

Theming

.alert reads two component tokens that each tone sets: --alert-bg and --alert-fg, aliasing --color-{info,success,warning,danger}-bg/fg or --color-gentle-bg/fg. .why reads --color-bg-glass and --color-fg-muted; .errors reads --color-danger-bg and --color-danger-fg.

/* A one-off tone without a new class */
.alert.reward-note { --alert-bg: var(--color-reward-bg); --alert-fg: var(--color-reward-fg); }

In the night studio the tints become deep, low-chroma panels and the inks lighten; every tone keeps 4.5:1. The why note's glass stays translucent, so the night sky shows through it.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

An alert present when the screen loads needs no role; it is just content. One that appears later gets role="status" (polite) when it is informative, or role="alert" (APG Alert) only when it is new and blocks the task: the validation box on submit, a failed save. Never put role="alert" on a why note.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabReaches the action and dismiss buttons in DOM order. The box itself is not focusable.
Enter, SpaceActivates the focused action or dismiss.

Focus

When a grown-up submits an invalid form, focus moves to the validation box (give it tabindex="-1") so its list is read first; each line should link to its field. After dismiss, focus moves to the next logical control, never to the top of the page.

Labelling

The emoji is decorative; the words carry the tone (Couldn't reach, Saved). The dismiss button names what it hides: aria-label="Dismiss offline message", not a bare "✕".

Motion

Alerts do not animate. On child screens a why note may join the staggered entrance (.rise), which reduced motion turns off.

Touch

The dismiss button is 44px (--target-comfortable); actions are small buttons (44px). Alerts with actions are grown-up only, so the 52px child target does not apply.

Content

  • Why notes say what Twinkle does for her, warmly: tunes every song to fit YOUR voice.
  • Offline and failure messages lead with what still works: Solo singing still works. Then what comes back and when.
  • Grown-up failures follow one formula: what happened, that nothing is lost, what to do. Couldn't reach the family server. Your song is still here. Check the server is on, then tap Create again.
  • Validation lines name the field or note and the allowed range: Tempo must be between 40 and 220 BPM. Never Invalid input.
  • Never show a raw technical message (Failed to fetch, HTTP codes) anywhere.
