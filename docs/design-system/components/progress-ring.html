Progress ring

Today's sparkle goal as a ring that fills blue around a white centre, one third for each song sung. It sits on the Home goal card and tells a child at a glance how close she is.

WCAG 2.2 AArole="img"Status: stableClass .goal-ring

Overview

The progress ring shows progress toward a small, countable daily goal: three songs today. It is a conic gradient from --color-accent to --color-track, driven by one custom property (--goal, in degrees), with the count in its white centre (1/3). It always sits in the .card.goal beside words that say the same thing (Sing 3 songs — just 2 more to go!).

Use a progress bar for continuous progress (level sparkles, landed notes, song position) and the week streak for days sung. Use the ring only for one goal per screen; it is not a loading spinner (that is the spinner).

Anatomy

  1. Fill--color-accent from 12 o'clock clockwise to --goal (120deg per song).
  2. Track--color-track for the rest of the circle. 64px outer size (--size-art).
  3. CentreA 40px white disc (--size-control-sm, --color-bg-surface) with the count in Baloo 2 black, --font-size-md. The 12px band between centre and edge is the visible ring.

Variants

One variant, shown in its card. The fill amount is the only thing that changes.

When the singer has no voice range yet (Bea), the goal card does not show a ring at all: it becomes a Find my voice link card with a 🎯 icon disc, because the first goal is finding her voice.

Sizes

The ring has one size, 64px (--size-art), matching song art so it lines up with the rest of the screen.

States

The ring is not interactive; its states are its values. --goal takes 0deg, 120deg, 240deg or 360deg.

Responsive behaviour

  • The ring keeps 64px at every width (flex: 0 0 auto); the goal text beside it wraps.
  • The centre count is short (1/3) and never wraps inside the 40px disc. Do not put longer text there.

Theming

Reads --color-accent (fill), --color-track (rest), --color-bg-surface (centre) and --color-fg-default (count). In the night studio the accent lightens to the brand blue and the track darkens, keeping the fill clearly visible.

/* A gold ring for a reward goal */
.goal-ring--reward { background: conic-gradient(var(--color-reward-edge) 0 var(--goal, 0deg), var(--color-track) var(--goal, 0deg) 360deg); }

The gold modifier above is an example of re-theming, not a shipped class.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

role="img" with an aria-label in words (1 of 3 songs done), as in the mocks: the ring changes only between visits, so a static image is honest. If it ever updates live on screen, switch to role="progressbar" with aria-valuemin="0", aria-valuemax="3", aria-valuenow and aria-valuetext="1 of 3 songs done" (APG Meter describes the related static-value pattern).

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabSkips the ring; it is not focusable.

Focus

None on the ring. When the goal card is a link (empty state), the card takes the ring.

Labelling

The label counts what is done, and at the start of the day states the goal instead of a zero (Today's goal: sing 3 songs). The centre text is inside the image and not read separately.

Contrast

Fill against the card
Centre count
Goal line beside the ring

The fill-versus-track edge is lower contrast than 3:1 in places, which is acceptable because the value is always also given as the centre count and the line beside it (WCAG 1.4.11 does not apply to a graphic whose information is present in text). Progress is never shown by colour alone.

Motion

The ring does not animate in the mocks; if a fill animation is added on return to Home, use --duration-progress and drop it under prefers-reduced-motion.

Touch

Not interactive.

Content

  • The centre is a fraction, 1/3, nothing else.
  • The line beside it counts down to the fun (just 2 more to go!) and celebrates the finish (you did it! 🎉).
  • Never imply the goal was missed. A day that ends at 1/3 simply starts fresh tomorrow; nothing says "incomplete".
