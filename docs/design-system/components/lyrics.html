Component · Singing

Lyrics

Three lines of the song under the pitch stage: the line just sung, the line to sing now, and the line coming next. The word to sing right now sits in a pink pill so a young reader can follow along.

Overview

The lyrics card lives under the pitch stage on the Sing screen and is the only place the words of a song appear. It is karaoke for early readers: the current line is big, the word to sing is wrapped in a pink pill, words already sung turn blue, and the lines around it are smaller and softer for context. Every word is a syllable from the song data (syll), so the pill moves in time with the note bars above.

  • Previous line (.lyr-prev): the line just finished, small and subtle. Empty on the first line.
  • Current line (.lyr-now): big Baloo 2, one <span> per syllable.
  • Next line (.lyr-next): what comes after, small and subtle. Empty on the last line and in practice.

Anatomy

  1. Previous line.lyr-prev: Baloo 2 bold, --font-size-md, --color-fg-subtle. Keeps a minimum height so the card never jumps.
  2. Sung words.word-done: --color-fg-accent blue. Always to the left of the current word.
  3. Current word.word-now: --color-gentle-bg pill with --color-gentle-fg text, --radius-md. Shape and colour together mark it.
  4. Upcoming wordsPlain <span>: --color-fg-default at --font-size-2xl.
  5. Next line.lyr-next: same style as the previous line.

Responsive behaviour

  • The card fills the screen column; lines are centred and wrap at word (syllable) boundaries. A current line wraps to two lines at 320px for the longest seed songs; that is allowed, three is not.
  • The card has a minimum height of two song-art units (calc(var(--size-art) * 2)) and each context line a minimum of one track height, so moving from line to line never shifts the screen.
  • The pill uses display: inline-block with 2 × 8px padding, so it never splits across lines.

Theming

Text colours are semantic: --color-fg-default, --color-fg-subtle for context lines, --color-fg-accent for sung words and the gentle pair for the current word. In the night studio the accent lightens and the gentle pill deepens to --palette-pink-dark-* so every pair keeps 4.5:1. The card itself is a card and follows the surface tokens.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A labelled region (<section aria-label="Lyrics">) of three paragraphs. No APG widget pattern applies. The current word gets aria-current="true" (ARIA aria-current) so a screen reader user exploring the line hears which word is now. The card is not a live region: announcing every syllable would drown out the music.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabSkips the card; nothing in it takes focus.
Screen reader reading keysRead previous, current and next lines in order; the current word is announced as "current".

Focus

None. Lyrics are read-only.

Labelling

Syllables are split with spaces (won der) because each is a note. The previous and next lines are whole words, so a listener hears the real line once. Empty context lines stay in the DOM as empty paragraphs to hold the layout; they announce nothing.

Contrast

The current word is marked by a filled pill shape, not by colour alone; sung words are distinguished by position (left of the pill) as well as colour.

Motion

The pill jumps from word to word with no transition: a sliding highlight would lag the beat. Lines swap instantly. Nothing here changes under reduced motion because nothing animates.

Touch

Not a touch target.

Content

  • Words come from the song data only. The current line is the phrase's syllables joined by spaces; context lines are the phrase text as written (Twinkle, twinkle, little star).
  • Before the song starts the current line says Ready? and nothing else.
  • Public-domain lyrics only (seed songs and family songs added through the Grown-Ups Corner).
  • Never show a lyric line with a word struck through, greyed out as "missed", or marked with a cross.
