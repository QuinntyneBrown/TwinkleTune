Song row

One song in the songbook: its picture, its name, how long and how brave it is, whether Twinkle has moved it into her key, and a big blue play button that starts it.

Overview

The song row is how a child picks a song on Pick a Song. It is a white rounded row with a tinted art square, the song title, a meta line and a round blue .play button. The play button is the row's one big action; everything else is information or a small extra.

  • Tuned (default): the meta line ends with the mint ✓ In your key pill (.tuned), whose title says how far Twinkle shifted it (Moved 3 notes higher for you 💙).
  • No voice range yet: the pill becomes a pink link, Find my voice → (.tuned.tuned-cta), that starts Find My Voice. The song is still playable in its standard key.
  • With family extras: when the family server is reachable, .song-actions adds a 🏆 mini button (family high scores) and a 🎤🎤 Duet mini button. Offline, they are simply absent.
  • Sing it again (.again-card): the pink row at the top of the list for the last song sung, with a pink emoji-only play button.

Use a mastered row to show a song she has already sung well, and a table or the song-board rows for scores.

Anatomy

  1. Art64px square (--size-art), --radius-lg, one of four pastel gradients (.art-1 aqua, .art-2 pink, .art-3 sky, .art-4 gold) with the song's emoji. Decorative.
  2. TitleBaloo 2, --font-size-lg, wraps; the row's name (aria-labelledby).
  3. MetaDuration and difficulty in Nunito extra-bold --font-size-xs, --color-fg-muted; wraps.
  4. Key pill✓ In your key in --color-success-fg on --color-success-bg, or the pink Find my voice → link. See pill.
  5. Mini buttons (family server only)🏆 and Duet .mini-btns, at least 24px tall with 8px between them.
  6. Play52px blue circle (--size-control) with the toy lip; presses down like a button.

Art tints rotate through the catalogue (Twinkle aqua, Mary pink, Hot Cross Buns gold, London Bridge aqua, Old MacDonald pink, Row Your Boat sky, Jesus Loves Me sky, Frère Jacques gold). The tint is decoration: the emoji and the title identify the song.

Responsive behaviour

  • The row spans the 470px column. Art and play keep their size; the body column (flex: 1; min-width: 0) takes the rest.
  • The meta line wraps: at 360px the key pill and mini buttons drop to a second meta line. Titles wrap to two lines (Twinkle Twinkle Little Star) and never truncate.
  • The play button stays vertically centred on the row, on the right, at every width, so the thumb always finds it in the same place.

Theming

The row reads --color-bg-surface, --radius-lg and --shadow-1. The play button reads --color-accent, --color-accent-hover, --color-fg-on-accent and --color-accent-edge exactly like the blue button. The key pill reads --color-success-bg / --color-success-fg (mint, D06); the no-range link reads the gentle pair. Art gradients read the decor, gentle and reward tokens and keep their pastel in the night studio.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Each row is an <article> labelled by its title, holding real <button>s (APG Button). The row itself is not clickable: only play starts the song, so a child scrolling with a finger never starts one by accident.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabPer row: key link (no-range only), 🏆, Duet, then play. Then the next row.
Enter, SpaceActivates the focused button; play opens Sing in the ready state.

Focus

Play shows the shared ring as a circle (border-radius: 50%) at 3px offset; mini buttons show it as a pill. Returning from Sing or Results puts focus back on the play button of the song just sung.

Labelling

Every button names the song: aria-label="Sing Twinkle Twinkle Little Star", "High scores for Twinkle Twinkle Little Star", "Duet Twinkle Twinkle Little Star". The shift in the key pill's title is a bonus for mouse users; the visible In your key carries the meaning. Art emoji are aria-hidden.

Motion

Row hover lift and play press over --duration-fast; rows rise in with a stagger. Reduced motion makes both instant.

Touch

Play is 52px (--target-child). Mini buttons are at least 24px tall with 8px spacing (D23); they are extras, never the only way to sing.

Content

  • Titles are the song's public-domain name exactly as the catalogue has it.
  • Difficulty is stars plus a happy word: ⭐ Easy, ⭐⭐ Medium, ⭐⭐⭐ Brave. Never "Hard".
  • Duration as ⏱ 0:29, minutes and seconds.
  • The key pill says what Twinkle did for her (✓ In your key), not what was wrong with the song.
