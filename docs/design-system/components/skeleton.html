Skeleton

Soft pale shapes that stand where family data is about to appear: profile cards, song rows, high-score rows. They show the layout that is coming so nothing jumps when it arrives.

Overview

Not in the mocks yet; added for the family-server screens and the Grown-Ups Corner managers. Those mocks show a sentence while data loads (Looking for the family… ☁️, Loading…); a skeleton is the better choice when the shape of the result is known, because the screen keeps its layout and the real content simply fades in.

Use a skeleton for a list or grid whose layout you know (profiles, the family songbook, high scores, the song and avatar managers). Use a spinner for a wait with no known shape, and never a skeleton for bundled content: seed songs, the singer's own rewards and everything in localStorage render at once.

  • Shapes: .skeleton-text (a line), .skeleton-title (a heading), .skeleton-circle (an avatar), .skeleton-rect (song art or a card).
  • Composed row (.skeleton-row): a white row card holding shapes, matching a song row, manager row or board row.

Anatomy

  1. Row card.skeleton-row: --color-bg-surface, --radius-lg, --shadow-1, 12 × 16px padding: the same box as the row it replaces.
  2. Art block.skeleton-rect: 64px tall (--size-art), --radius-lg. Set its width to match the art.
  3. Title bar.skeleton-title: 24px tall, 60% wide, pill-shaped.
  4. Text bar.skeleton-text: 16px tall, full width. Shorten the last line with an inline width.

Variants

Shapes

Circle (avatar), rect (song art), title and two text lines.

Composed

Who's singing (three profile cards) and the family high-score board (rows with avatar, name and score).

Sizes

Skeleton shapes take the size of the content they stand in for; there is no size scale.

States

Song manager
Rows replace the skeleton in place; the container drops aria-busy. Empty state in the same spot. Alert with what happened and what to do (D19).

Both themes

  • Shimmer: shapes pulse between full and 55% opacity over --duration-pulse. Under reduced motion they hold still at full opacity.
  • Show the skeleton only after 300ms, and for no longer than the API client's health timeout; then show data, cached data or the empty/error state.
  • Render the number of placeholders you expect (the cached count, or three); never fill the screen with dozens of rows.

Responsive behaviour

  • Skeletons live inside the real container (.profile-grid, a list, a dialog body), so they reflow exactly as the loaded content will at every width.
  • Text bars use percentage widths and stretch with the row; art and avatar shapes keep their fixed token sizes.

Theming

Shapes read --color-bg-subtle-hover; the row reads --color-bg-surface and --shadow-1. In the night studio the shapes become a slightly lighter night tone on the rising surface, so they read as placeholders, not holes. Never tint a skeleton with the accent or reward colours: placeholders must look quieter than content.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Shapes are aria-hidden="true"; they mean nothing on their own. The container that will hold the content sets aria-busy="true" (ARIA aria-busy; no APG widget pattern applies) and keeps its accessible name, so a screen reader hears "Singers loading, busy". Add one visually hidden role="status" line if the wait may exceed a second.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabSkips skeletons; no placeholder is focusable. Real controls become reachable when content arrives.

Focus

None. If focus was inside the container (a reload), keep it on the control that triggered the load.

Labelling

Label the container, not the shapes: aria-label="Singers loading" while busy, back to Singers when loaded.

Contrast

Text near a skeleton (row card)
Status text while loading

Skeleton shapes are decorative placeholders and are exempt from the 3:1 graphics rule; they intentionally sit close to the surface so they never compete with real content.

Motion

The shimmer is an opacity pulse, never a sweeping highlight, and stays well under three flashes per second. Reduced motion stops it.

Touch

Not touch targets. A skeleton profile card does not respond to taps; the real card does once it loads.

Content

  • Skeletons carry no words. If a sentence is needed, it is the screen's waiting line (Looking for the family… ☁️) in a status region.
  • Do not put fake names or emoji in placeholders; a child may try to tap "Ada" before she exists.
