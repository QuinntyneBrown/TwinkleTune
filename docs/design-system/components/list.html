Component · Data display

List

Rows for things that repeat: songs she shines at, tips to try, avatars and songs in the grown-ups' managers. Each row is one rounded strip with an emoji, words and, at most, a couple of small actions.

Overview

Use a list when the same kind of thing appears more than twice and a child or grown-up picks from it or reads down it. The mocks have three list rows, each tuned to its screen, and the system adds a generic .list / .list-item pair that follows them for new grown-up screens.

  • Mastered row (.mastered) on Me: Songs I shine at. Emoji, song title in Baloo 2, gold text stars. Read-only.
  • Tip card (.tip-card) on Tips: a whole-row <button> with a tinted icon square (.tip-ico.ti-1…4), title, one-line hint and a minutes pill (.tip-min). Opens the tip dialog.
  • Manager row (.mgr-row) in the Grown-Ups Corner managers: sunken strip with emoji, name (and a small note such as built-in), and 40px edit and delete icon buttons.
  • List item (.list > .list-item) is the generic row, simple or two-line, static or interactive (a.list-item, button.list-item), and grouped in one surface with .list-divided. Not in the mocks yet; added for the Grown-Ups Corner and family-server managers.

Songs to sing have their own richer row, the song row. Use a table when grown-ups compare values across columns, such as family high scores.

Anatomy

  1. Leading visualAn emoji (.em, --font-size-2xl) or a 56px tinted square (.tip-ico, --radius-md). Decorative.
  2. TitleBaloo 2, --font-size-md. Takes the free width (flex: 1; min-width: 0) and wraps.
  3. Secondary line (optional)Nunito bold --font-size-sm in --color-fg-muted.
  4. TrailingA pill, gold stars, or up to two icon buttons. Never more than two actions per row.

Row surfaces: --color-bg-surface, --radius-lg, --shadow-1, 16px padding, 12px apart (child rows); manager rows are --color-bg-surface-sunken, --radius-md, no shadow, 8 × 12px padding, 8px apart, because they sit inside a white dialog.

Responsive behaviour

  • Rows are full column width. The title takes the free space and wraps; the leading visual and trailing pill or buttons keep their size (flex: 0 0 auto).
  • At 320px a tip card's hint wraps to two or three lines; the minutes pill stays on the right, vertically centred.
  • A manager row with a long song title wraps the title, never the icon buttons. Rows never scroll sideways.

Theming

Child rows read --color-bg-surface and --shadow-1; manager rows read --color-bg-surface-sunken; the tip icon tints come from the decor, gentle and reward tokens (--color-decor-aqua, --color-gentle-bg, --color-reward-bg, --color-decor-sky). Mastered stars use --color-reward-edge. In the night studio rows rise above the darker canvas (D08) and the icon tints keep their pastel so tips are recognisable by colour and emoji together.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Mark repeated rows up as a <ul> with an aria-label (Songs I shine at, Songbook), so screen readers announce "list, 4 items". The mocks use bare <div>s; the contract is a list. Interactive rows are a single <button> (opens a dialog) or <a href> (navigates) inside the <li>. This is not an APG listbox: there is no selection model, only buttons and links (APG Button, APG Link).

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves to the next interactive row, or to the next row action (edit, then delete) in a manager row.
Enter, SpaceActivates a tip card or row icon button. Linked rows activate on Enter.

Focus

The shared 3px ring at 3px offset around the whole row for tip cards and linked items; around the 40px circle for row icon buttons. After deleting a manager row, focus moves to the next row's edit button, or to + New when the list is empty.

Labelling

Row icon buttons are named for the action and the item: aria-label="Delete Frère Jacques", never just "Delete". Leading emoji are aria-hidden. Text stars (★★☆) are hidden and paired with a .visually-hidden "2 of 3 stars", because screen readers read them as "black star, black star, white star".

Contrast

Mastered gold stars (--color-reward-edge) are decorative and always paired with the spoken star count, so they are not held to text contrast.

Motion

Interactive rows lift 2px on hover and shrink to 98% on press over --duration-fast; rows enter with the staggered rise. Reduced motion removes both.

Touch

Tip cards are about 88px tall. Manager row icon buttons are 40px with 8px between them, above the 24px minimum with spacing (D23); they live only in grown-up dialogs.

Content

  • Titles are the thing's own name: the song title, the tip's name, the avatar's name (Unicorn).
  • Secondary lines describe the fun, not the rules: Slide your voice low to high — wheee-ooo!
  • Child list headings name what she has, never what she lacks: Songs I shine at, not Songs to improve.
  • Manager notes are plain facts for grown-ups: built-in, family song.
