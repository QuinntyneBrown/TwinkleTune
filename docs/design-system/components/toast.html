Toast

Little pill-shaped messages that pop in at the top of the screen, stay a moment and float away. White says "done", gold says "yay!", and pink gently says what to try next.

WCAG 2.2 AArole="status" hostStatus: stableClass .toast

Overview

Use a toast for a short message that needs no answer and is still true if she misses it: +5 sparkles ✨, 🔥 5 in a row!, Removed 💙, 🏆 New family record!. Toasts never take focus, cannot be tapped and leave on their own, so the singing never stops for them.

  • If she has to choose something, use a dialog.
  • If the message must stay until it is fixed (a failed save in a grown-up editor, a whole form that is invalid), use an alert inside the dialog, and an inline message under the field. A toast may announce it; it is never the only place it lives.
  • Never a red toast. A failure in a child's flow is the same soft pink as a nudge, says what still works, and the screen underneath keeps working.

Anatomy

  1. Host.toast-host: fixed, top centre, 16px from the top, at --z-toast. A column with 8px gaps, at most the viewport less 32px wide. role="status" aria-live="polite". Ignores pointer events.
  2. Emoji (optional, leading or trailing)One emoji that adds warmth: 🏆, ✨, 💙, 📡.
  3. MessageNunito black at --font-size-md on a pill (--radius-full), 12 × 20px padding, --shadow-3.

Sizes

Toasts have one size. The pill grows with its words up to the viewport width less 32px, then wraps.

States

A toast is not interactive, so it has no hover, focus or disabled state. Its states are its life: entering, shown, leaving.

Removed 💙+5 sparkles ✨Reach a little higher! 🐦
  • Entering pops from 40% to full size with a small overshoot (--duration-slow, --ease-spring).
  • Leaving adds .bye: it fades out and floats up 16px over --duration-slow, then is removed from the host.
  • The leaving cell above is fully transparent by design; that is what .bye looks like at rest.

Responsive behaviour

  • Top centre at every width; the host is max-content wide up to the viewport less 32px, so short toasts stay small pills and long nudges wrap onto two lines.
  • On the Sing screen toasts sit above the pitch stage and below the top controls; the 1.6 s streak toast is short so it never covers the lyrics for long.
  • On a phone in landscape the host keeps its 16px top inset; it never overlaps the bottom navigation.

Theming

White reads --color-bg-surface and --color-fg-default; gold a gradient from --color-reward-bg-subtle to --color-reward-bg with --color-reward-fg; pink --color-gentle-bg with --color-gentle-fg. All share --shadow-3. There are no component tokens.

Gold is identical in both themes: sparkles always look like sparkles. In the night studio the white toast becomes the raised night surface and pink deepens to a dusky rose, with its ink lightening to keep 4.5:1.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The host is a polite live region: role="status" aria-live="polite", present in the page from the start and empty, so screen readers announce each toast as it is added (APG Alert covers the assertive cousin; toasts deliberately stay polite so they never interrupt a lyric being read). The toast itself is a plain <span> with no role.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabNever lands on a toast: it has no controls, and focus stays where it was.
EscDoes nothing to toasts. It keeps its meaning for any open dialog.

Focus

Toasts never take or move focus, and they sit at the top of the screen where they cannot cover a focused control in a dialog or the bottom navigation (WCAG 2.4.11).

Labelling

The words are the announcement. Put decorative emoji in <span aria-hidden="true"> when they lead the text, so "🏆 New family record!" is read as "New family record!". Never send a toast that is only an emoji.

Timing

Toasts stay 1.6–2.6 s, shorter than WCAG's usual advice for auto-dismissing messages. That is acceptable only because a toast is never the sole carrier of anything that matters: sparkles are totalled again on Results, a removed song is visibly gone from the list, and a failed grown-up save stays open with an alert. Anything she or a grown-up must act on goes somewhere that stays.

Motion

Pop-in and float-away both use --duration-slow. Under prefers-reduced-motion the durations are zeroed: toasts appear and disappear without movement, and the timers are unchanged.

Touch

The host has pointer-events: none, so a toast never blocks a tap on whatever is beneath it, including the pitch stage while she sings.

Content

  • Five words or fewer when you can: +5 sparkles ✨, Removed 💙. A nudge may be one friendly sentence.
  • Gold says what she earned, with the number: 🔥 5 in a row!, Bea finished — 28 notes! 🎉.
  • Pink says what to try next or what still works, never what went wrong: reach a little higher! 🐦, Can't reach the family server 📡. Never "error", "failed" or "wrong".
  • White confirms with a heart: Song deleted 💙.
  • Never pass a raw browser or server message into a toast.

Code

<!-- once, in the page shell, empty -->
<div class="toast-host" role="status" aria-live="polite"></div>

function toast(text, look = '', ms = 2200) {
  const t = document.createElement('span');
  t.className = `toast ${look}`;          // '', 'toast-gold' or 'toast-pink'
  t.textContent = text;
  host.append(t);
  while (host.children.length > 3) host.firstElementChild.remove();
  setTimeout(() => { t.classList.add('bye'); setTimeout(() => t.remove(), 400); }, ms);
}
toast('🏆 New family record!', 'toast-gold', 2600);
