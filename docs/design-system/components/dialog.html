Dialog

A rounded card that pops in over a softly blurred sky and asks one thing. Child dialogs offer one big action and a quiet way back; grown-up dialogs widen for the managers and editors.

WCAG 2.2 AArole="dialog" + aria-modalStatus: stableClass .overlay .modal

Overview

Use a dialog when the child has to answer something before she can carry on (Can Twinkle hear you sing?), when singing is paused, when something wonderful just happened (Your voice is found! 🎉), or when a grown-up needs a short task out of the child's way (the Grown-Ups Corner, the song and singer editors). A dialog always has a way out that loses nothing.

  • Do not use a dialog for news that needs no answer: a sparkle total or a family record is a toast.
  • Do not use a dialog for a whole screen of content. Find My Voice, Duet Time and the profile picker are pages.
  • Do not stack dialogs. The parent gate replaces itself with the Grown-Ups Corner, and a manager replaces the Corner; there is only ever one card on the scrim.
  • The parent gate is a dialog with a number pad; it guards the Grown-Ups Corner and every destructive action.

Anatomy

  1. Header art (optional).modal-emoji at --font-size-5xl, or Twinkle (.mascot.mascot-sm). Decorative: aria-hidden="true".
  2. Titleh3, Baloo 2 at --font-size-2xl. It is the dialog's accessible name (aria-labelledby).
  3. Bodyp, Nunito bold, --font-size-md, --color-fg-muted, line height 1.5. One or two sentences.
  4. ActionsFull-width buttons, 16px above the first and 12px between. One blue or gold; the rest white or pink.
  5. Way backA quiet link at --font-size-sm: Back to singing, Keep my stuff, Cancel.
  6. Card and overlay.modal: --color-bg-surface, --radius-xl (32px), 28 × 24px padding, --shadow-4. .overlay: fixed scrim in --color-bg-backdrop with a 4px blur, at --z-overlay.

Variants

All variants share the overlay, the card and the modal contract. They differ in header art, the number of actions and width.

For grown-ups only

  • Closing never asks "Are you sure?" unless something would be lost. Cancel in an editor with unsaved typing simply closes; the song manager is not destructive.
  • Child dialogs have no busy, invalid or failed state. Their actions are local and act at once. Only the grown-up dialogs that call the family server use those states.

Responsive behaviour

  • The card is centred at every width, with 20px (--space-5) of scrim around it. It is min(340px, 100%) or min(440px, 100%), so at 320px it fills the width less the scrim padding.
  • It is not a bottom sheet on phones: the app keeps one centred card everywhere, and so does the system.
  • The wide card scrolls inside itself past 84vh. Keep the actions at the end of the content, not pinned, so a scrolled form reads top to bottom.
  • In landscape on a phone, a standard child dialog may scroll too; its actions must never be cut off.

Theming

The overlay reads --color-bg-backdrop; the card reads --color-bg-surface, --shadow-4, --radius-xl, --color-fg-default for the title and --color-fg-muted for body copy. There are no component tokens.

In the night studio the backdrop deepens to a translucent navy and the card rises to the lightest night surface, so it still floats above the page. The blur stays the same in both themes.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

APG Dialog (Modal): role="dialog", aria-modal="true", aria-labelledby pointing at the h3, and aria-describedby at the body paragraph when it carries the question. Everything behind the overlay gets inert. A native <dialog> opened with showModal() satisfies the same contract. The live examples on this page omit aria-modal so the documentation around them stays readable.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabCycles through the dialog's controls only; focus wraps from last to first and never reaches the page behind.
Enter, SpaceActivates the focused button or link.
EscTakes the quiet way back (Back to singing, Keep my stuff, Cancel, Maybe later). In Pause it means Keep singing. It never confirms a destructive action.

Focus

On open, focus moves into the card: to the primary action in child dialogs, to the safe quiet link in confirmations (Keep my stuff), to the first key in the parent gate and to the first field in editors. On close, focus returns to the control that opened the dialog; if that control is gone (a deleted song's row), to the list heading. Tapping the scrim does nothing: dialogs close only through their own choices.

Labelling

Name the dialog with its visible title, not a separate aria-label, so the two cannot drift apart (the mocks label the parent gate "Grown-ups only" while its title says "For grown-ups only"). Decorative header art is aria-hidden="true".

Motion

The card pops in from 40% to full size with a small overshoot (--duration-slow, --ease-spring); the scrim fades over --duration-base. Under prefers-reduced-motion both durations are zeroed, so the dialog simply appears. The backdrop blur is static and stays.

Touch

Full-width buttons at 56px or more; the quiet link sits 16px below the last button and has a 44px hit area. Small buttons (44px) appear only in grown-up dialogs, side by side with 8px between.

Content

  • The title is a question or an exclamation in Twinkle's voice: Can Twinkle hear you sing?, Your voice is found! 🎉, Really start over?. One emoji at most, at the end.
  • The body is one or two short sentences saying why. Grown-up dialogs may say more, but always in plain words.
  • The primary action says what happens: Yes, let's sing! 🎤, To my songs 🎵. Never OK, Confirm or Submit.
  • The way back keeps things: Keep my stuff, Back to singing, Maybe later. Never No on its own.
  • Destructive copy names exactly what goes and what stays: This erases the profile, sparkles, badges and voice range on this device.
