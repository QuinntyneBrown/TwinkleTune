Radio group

One choice from a few, every option in view, inside a fieldset with a legend. Round 24px dots, or whole cards for choices that need a picture. Not in the mocks yet; added for the Grown-Ups Corner and the song editor.

Overview

Use a radio group when a grown-up must pick exactly one of two to five options and seeing them side by side helps: the song's difficulty, the studio theme. Beyond five, use a select. For a single on/off, use a switch or a checkbox.

Not in the mocks yet. Added for the Grown-Ups Corner and the family-server song editor, where Difficulty (three options, each worth explaining) reads better as radios than as the mock's drop-down. On child screens one-of-many is the avatar picker or filter chips, never small dots.

  • Vertical (.radio-group), the default: one option per row, room for a description.
  • Horizontal (.radio-group--row): two or three one-word options.
  • Card (.radio-card on each label): a white card per option that rings blue when chosen, for choices with a big emoji.

Anatomy

  1. LegendThe question, in Baloo 2 bold 16px. It names the group for screen readers.
  2. Dot.radio-dot: 24px circle, 3px --color-border-strong edge. Chosen: blue edge with a 12px --color-accent centre.
  3. Option labelBody text. The whole label row is the target, at least 44px tall.
  4. Description (optional)<small>, --color-fg-muted.

Responsive behaviour

  • Vertical and card groups stack at every width.
  • Horizontal groups wrap (flex-wrap) when the options do not fit; at 320px expect two rows for three options. Switch to vertical if any option needs a description.
  • Cards fill the column; never place cards two-up in a 340px dialog.

Theming

Semantic tokens only: --color-bg-surface dot fill, --color-border-strong edge, --color-accent chosen edge and centre. Cards use --color-bg-surface, --shadow-1 and --shadow-ring-accent, the same ring as the active profile card.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Native radios sharing one name, inside a <fieldset> whose <legend> names the group (APG Radio Group). Add role="radiogroup" to the fieldset only when you need aria-invalid or aria-required on the group.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves into the group (to the chosen option, or the first if none) and out again. The group is one tab stop.
↓ / →Moves to and chooses the next option, wrapping to the first.
↑ / ←Moves to and chooses the previous option, wrapping to the last.
SpaceChooses the focused option if it is not chosen.

Focus

The shared ring is drawn around the dot when the hidden input has :focus-visible. Arrow keys change the choice, so choosing never submits or navigates (WCAG 3.2.2).

Labelling

Each input sits inside its label. The legend is a question or noun (Difficulty); the field error is linked to the group with aria-describedby.

Contrast

Dot edge on a card
Chosen centre on a card
Option label on a card
Description on a card
Focus ring on the sky (cards)

Motion

Edge colour fades over --duration-base; the centre appears without animation. Reduced motion makes the fade instant.

Touch

The dot is 24px; the whole label row (44px minimum) or the whole card is the target. Keep at least 4px between rows (8px for cards).

Content

  • Options are parallel: all adjectives (Easy, Medium, Brave) or all nouns. Order them by meaning.
  • Always start with one chosen, the gentlest or most common. An empty group asks a question nobody needs to answer.
  • Descriptions say what the choice does in plain words: Big leaps and the whole voice range.
  • The hardest level is Brave, never Hard or Expert.
