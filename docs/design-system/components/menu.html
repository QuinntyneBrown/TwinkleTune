Menu

A menu button opens a short list of grown-up actions for one row: edit, duplicate, share a high score, delete. It keeps busy rows tidy in the Grown-Ups Corner and never appears on a child's screen.

Overview

Not in the mocks yet; added for the Grown-Ups Corner and the family-server managers. Today each Songbook row shows ✏️ and 🗑️ row buttons side by side. When a row needs a third or fourth action (Duplicate, Move to family songs, Reset high scores), collapse them behind one ⋯ menu button instead of adding more icons.

  • Use a menu for 3 to 7 actions on one item, for grown-ups only.
  • Keep the two most common actions as visible buttons when there is room; a menu hides things.
  • Do not use a menu to pick a value (use a select or radio group), to navigate the app (use the bottom nav or sidebar nav), or on any child screen: a child gets one big obvious action, not a list of hidden ones.

Anatomy

  1. TriggerAn .icon-btn (⋯) or a .btn btn-sm btn-white with words. Carries aria-haspopup="menu" and aria-expanded.
  2. Heading (optional).menu-heading: overline, --color-fg-subtle. Names the item when the trigger is far from it.
  3. Item.menu-item: 44px tall (--target-comfortable), optional leading emoji, body text, --radius-md hover tint.
  4. Separator.menu-sep: a 1px --color-border-default rule between groups.
  5. Danger item.menu-item--danger in --color-danger-fg, always last, always followed by a confirm dialog (label ends in "…").

The list (.menu-list) is a raised surface: --color-bg-surface-raised, --radius-lg, --shadow-3, 8px padding, layered at --z-dropdown, anchored under the trigger's right edge.

Variants

Open menus are shown here with .menu-list--static, which drops the absolute positioning so the list sits in the page.

Actions with emoji · checkable items with a section heading · a disabled item and a danger item after a separator.

Triggers

The row-size icon button inside a manager row, and a small white button with words when the menu has a clear name.

Sizes

Menus have one size: 44px items, at least 216px wide.

States

Item states are forced with data-state; real menus get them from :hover, :focus-visible (focus moves item to item with the arrow keys) and :active.

Trigger: closed and open

aria-expanded="false" · aria-expanded="true" with focus on the first item. The trigger keeps its hover tint while open.

Both themes

  • Checked adds a ✓ in --color-fg-accent at the end of the item, plus aria-checked="true"; the tick is a shape, not only a colour.
  • Disabled items stay in the list (so the menu does not change shape) in --color-fg-disabled, with aria-disabled="true" so they can still be focused and read. Prefer removing an item that can never apply.
  • There is no busy item. A slow action closes the menu and shows its progress where the result appears (the row, a toast).

Responsive behaviour

  • The list opens below the trigger, aligned to its right edge. If there is no room below, it flips above; if it would leave the viewport sideways, it aligns to the left edge.
  • At least 216px wide (60% of --size-form-max), at most the viewport minus 32px. Long labels wrap; they never truncate.
  • On phones the menu stays a popup, not a bottom sheet: menus are short (seven items at most) and grown-up only.

Theming

The menu reads --color-bg-surface-raised, --shadow-3, --color-bg-subtle and --color-bg-subtle-hover (item hover and press), --color-fg-default, --color-fg-subtle (heading), --color-fg-accent (tick), --color-danger-fg and --color-border-default (separator). In the night studio the raised surface is the lightest navy, so the menu still floats above the cards beneath it (D08).

Accessibility

Role and pattern

APG Menu Button. The trigger is a <button> with aria-haspopup="menu", aria-expanded and aria-controls. The list is role="menu", labelled; each <li> is role="none"; items are role="menuitem" or role="menuitemcheckbox" with aria-checked; separators are role="separator".

Keyboard

KeyAction

↑On the trigger: opens the menu and focuses the last item.
↓ / ↑Moves to the next / previous item, wrapping at the ends. Disabled items are focusable; separators and headings are skipped.

Focus

Focus moves into the list on open (roving: only the focused item is in the tab order) and back to the trigger on close. Items show the shared 3px --color-focus-ring. A pointer click outside closes the menu without moving focus.

Labelling

An icon trigger names the item it acts on: More for Frère Jacques. The list's aria-label repeats that, so a screen reader knows which row's menu is open. Leading emoji are aria-hidden. The trailing "…" on a danger item tells sighted users a confirm follows; the confirm dialog itself says what will be lost.

Motion

The list pops in with the product's popin over --duration-slow on --ease-spring. Reduced motion zeroes the duration: the list simply appears.

Touch

Items are 44px tall (--target-comfortable) and full width, so they are easy to hit on a phone. Row-size triggers are 40px with 8px or more around them.

Content

  • Items are verb phrases in sentence case: Edit song, Change photo, Remove Mum….
  • Order by frequency, with the destructive item last after a separator.
  • An item that opens a dialog ends with "…"; an item that acts at once does not.
  • Use the domain words: Re-do voice range, not Recalibrate; Family high scores, not Leaderboard.
