Pill

Pills are the small rounded labels that say a little more about a song, a tip or a singer: ✓ In your key, 🎯 Your key ✓, 1 min, SINGING NOW ⭐. They inform; they are not buttons.

Overview

A pill is a short status or meta label attached to something else. It never asks to be tapped (with one exception, the pink Find my voice →, below). If tapping it should do something, use a chip or a button.

The mocks grew one class per place; the system keeps those names (page objects depend on them) and adds a generic .pill with tone modifiers for new places.

  • In your key (.tuned): mint, on every song row once she has a voice range. ✓ In your key.
  • Find my voice (.tuned.tuned-cta): the pink link that takes its place when she has no voice range yet. The only interactive pill.
  • Key label (.key-chip): the bigger mint label on Sing, 🎯 Your key ✓, or white Standard key (.standard) when the song is not transposed.
  • Tip minutes (.tip-min): how long a tip takes, 1 min, read.
  • Profile tag (.profile-tag): text-only, blue, capitals: SINGING NOW ⭐ on the active singer's card.
  • Generic (.pill + .pill-info, -success, -reward, -gentle, -danger, .pill-count): new, for grown-up screens such as built-in / family song and nav counts.

Anatomy

  1. Mark (optional, leading)✓, 🎯 or another emoji. A shape that repeats the meaning, so it never rests on colour.
  2. Label12px (--font-size-xs, the floor), black weight. One to three words.
  3. BodyPill (--radius-full), 4 × 8px padding (.tuned), tinted by tone: --color-success-bg here.

Sizes

Pills are text-sized. The key label on Sing is the one larger pill, because it sits alone in the top bar and must be read mid-song.

✓ In your key 2 min 🎯 Your key ✓
Small: 4 × 8px padding (.tuned, .pill) · medium: 8 × 12px (.tip-min) · large: 8 × 16px (.key-chip). All 12px text.

Responsive behaviour

  • Pills do not wrap internally: .pill, .key-chip and .tip-min set white-space: nowrap. .tuned does not yet, so Find my voice → can break in a squeezed cell; give it nowrap when it ships. Inside .song-meta the whole pill moves to the next line at narrow widths.
  • On Sing, the title truncates with an ellipsis before the key label shrinks: the label is part of what Twinkle promised her.
  • The profile tag sits under the name on its own line on every width.

Theming

The generic pill reads two component tokens, --pill-bg and --pill-fg, which each tone overrides with a semantic pair (--color-success-bg / --color-success-fg and so on). The product pills read the same semantic pairs directly: mint for .tuned and .key-chip, --color-gentle-bg / --color-gentle-fg for the call to action, --color-bg-subtle / --color-fg-accent for tip minutes, and --color-fg-accent alone for the profile tag.

/* A new tone without touching .pill */
.pill-duet { --pill-bg: var(--color-reward-bg-subtle); --pill-fg: var(--color-reward-fg); }

In the night studio the tints deepen and their text lightens, keeping every pair at 4.5:1 or more.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

No APG pattern: a static pill is plain text in a <span> and needs no role. It is not a live region; a pill that changes while she watches (Standard key → Your key) is announced by the toast or dialog that explains the change (Song tuned for you). The call-to-action pill is a real <a href> (APG Link).

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabSkips static pills. Stops on Find my voice →.
EnterFollows Find my voice → to Find My Voice.

Focus

Only the call-to-action pill takes focus: the shared 3px --color-focus-ring at a 3px offset.

Labelling

The mocks explain the shift only in a title ("Moved 3 notes higher for you 💙"), which touch users and most screen readers never get. Put that sentence where everyone can reach it: as visually hidden text next to the pill, in a tooltip that also opens on focus, or in the Song tuned for you dialog. Counts carry an aria-label that says what is counted (8 songs).

Motion

None. Pills do not animate; the screen around them does.

Touch

Static pills are not targets. Find my voice → is small (about 24px tall) and therefore never the only way in: Welcome leads straight into Find My Voice, the Home goal card links to it while she has no range, and the Grown-Ups Corner has a full Re-do button.

Content

  • Say what is good about the thing: In your key, Family best. A pill never reports a shortfall (no Too high, Not tuned).
  • Use Standard key, a neutral fact, when a song is not transposed, never Not your key.
  • Tip minutes are a number plus min, or read for a tip with no timer.
  • The profile tag is the one all-caps label; nothing else shouts.
  • Keep to 12px. Smaller text was raised to the floor during extraction (D10).
