Voice ladder

Find My Voice's ladder of twelve rungs, the big microphone and the little equaliser. The singer climbs the ladder with her voice; the lowest and highest comfy notes she holds become her voice range.

Overview

The voice ladder is Find My Voice, the one-minute game that captures a singer's voice range. She sings her lowest comfy note and holds it, then her highest. Each rung is a step of pitch, bottom (🌊 low) to top (⭐ high); her avatar climbs to the rung she is singing, and a held note is captured in gold with its name (Low B3 ✓). From the range, every song gets a shift that fits her voice.

It has three parts that always appear together on the Find My Voice screen:

  • Ladder card (.ladder-card): twelve .rungs in .ladder, plus .ladder-side with ⭐ HIGH · LOW 🌊.
  • Microphone (.mic): a 92px blue disc that pulses while listening.
  • Equaliser (.eq): seven bars beside the listen message that dance while sound is heard.

The step dots above it are the stepper; Twinkle's instructions above that are the coach bubble.

Anatomy

  1. Ladder.ladder: flex-direction: column-reverse, so rung 0 (lowest) is at the bottom; 8px between rungs.
  2. RungA 32px pill (--size-rung). Idle --color-mark-idle; .lit aqua-to-sky once passed; captured rungs carry a label in 12px black caps.
  3. Now rung and singer dot.rung.now: blue gradient with a pulsing ring; .singer-dot shows her avatar in a 40px disc at the right end.
  4. Side scale.ladder-side: ⭐ at the top, 🌊 at the bottom, vertical "HIGH · LOW" between. Decorative (aria-hidden).
  5. Microphone.mic: 92px (--size-mic) disc with --gradient-hero, 8px surface border, accent glow. .live pulses.
  6. Listen message and equaliserBaloo 2 message (a polite live region) over .eq: seven 8px bars, brand blue and sky alternating, that bounce while .live.

  • Holding: the message counts up in words and percent (Keep holding it… 62% ✨) for the same hold time on every device. The percentage is the progress; there is no separate bar.
  • Too quiet or wobbly is not a state the child sees as a problem: the dot simply stays where it is and Twinkle's bubble encourages (a big cozy “laaa”).
  • Microphone blocked leaves the ladder idle and opens the mic-denied dialog for a grown-up; the singer can always choose Later and sing in the standard key.
  • Done opens the Voice found dialog with both captured notes (You sing from B3 up to E5.).

Responsive behaviour

  • The ladder card fills the screen column; rungs stretch (flex: 1) and the side scale keeps its width. Twelve 32px rungs and eleven 8px gaps make a 472px ladder at every width, so the game looks the same on phone and tablet.
  • On short phones (under 700px tall) the screen scrolls; the microphone row sits directly under the ladder so both stay in view while she sings.
  • The listen row keeps the microphone at 92px and lets the message wrap; the equaliser keeps its 40px height.

Theming

Rungs read --color-mark-idle, the decor gradient (--color-decor-aqua → --color-decor-sky) when lit, --gradient-rung-now when now and the reward gradient when captured. The microphone uses --gradient-hero and --shadow-glow-accent; the equaliser alternates --color-accent-graphic and --color-decor-sky. In the night studio idle rungs darken to --palette-night-650 while the captured gold stays the same.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The ladder is a labelled picture of progress: <section aria-label="Your vocal range ladder">, rungs as generic elements, the side scale aria-hidden. The microphone is role="img" with an aria-label that says whether it is listening. The listen message is the accessible channel: give it role="status" (ARIA status role; no APG widget pattern applies) and update it at most once a second, rounding the hold percentage to tens. The only control is the screen's one button (I'm ready — listen! 🎤), a native button.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabBack (←), Later, then the main button. The ladder, mic and equaliser are skipped.
Enter, SpaceOn the main button: starts listening. The button is removed while listening; focus moves to the listen message container (tabindex="-1") so it is not lost.

Focus

No ladder part is focusable. When listening ends (Voice found dialog), focus moves into the dialog; when it closes, focus returns to the main button.

Labelling

Captured rungs carry their note in text (Low B3 ✓), so the range is never in colour alone. The singer dot's avatar is decorative inside the rung; the message says what is happening (Listening for your high note… ⭐).

Motion

The now rung and the live microphone pulse a ring over --duration-pulse; equaliser bars bounce over --duration-progress with staggered delays. Under prefers-reduced-motion the pulses and bounce run once and stop; the message and the singer dot still update, so no information is lost.

Touch

Not touch targets. The microphone looks pressable but is not: the screen's button below is the one action, at 68px.

Content

  • Listen messages say what Twinkle is doing, warmly: Press the button when you're ready!, Listening for your low note… 🌊, Keep holding it… 62% ✨, Listening for your high note… ⭐.
  • Captured labels: Low B3 ✓, High E5 ✓. Note names appear only here and in the Voice found dialog; grown-ups see them, children see the ladder.
  • Use comfy, never correct: the range is what feels good, not a test.
  • Never say too low, out of range or failed. If nothing is heard, Twinkle invites another "laaa".
