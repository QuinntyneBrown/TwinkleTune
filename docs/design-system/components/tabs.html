Tabs

Tabs switch between panels of different content in the same place: a song's family high scores this week and of all time, or the Grown-Ups Corner's Songs and Avatars. Pill tabs look like chips; the underline style is for denser grown-up pages.

Overview

Not in the mocks yet as real tabs; added for the Grown-Ups Corner, family-server managers and marketing. The mocks' difficulty and tip-category rows use tab roles but filter one list, so they are documented as filter chips (D18). Use tabs only when each choice reveals its own panel.

  • Pill tabs (.tabs) match the chip look and the toy style. Use them on any screen a child might see, such as the high-score board's This week and All time.
  • Underline tabs (.tabs--underline) are quieter, for grown-up dialogs and the marketing site where tabs sit under a heading.
  • Two to five tabs. More than five, or labels that need a sentence, means separate pages in the sidebar nav.
  • Do not use tabs for steps in a sequence (use the stepper) or for app sections (use the bottom nav).

Anatomy

  1. Tablist.tabs: a flex row, 8px gaps, scrolls sideways when it overflows. role="tablist", labelled.
  2. Selected tabAccent body, --color-fg-on-accent label, 3px lip (--lip-small). aria-selected="true".
  3. Count (optional).tab-count: overline type in a small white pill, --color-fg-accent.
  4. Unselected tabGlass body (--color-bg-glass), --color-fg-muted, 44px tall (--target-comfortable).

The panel below the list is a plain region with role="tabpanel"; it carries no styling of its own.

Sizes

Medium 44px (--target-comfortable), 16px body text · small (.tab-sm) 24px minimum, 14px text, grown-up dialogs only.

States

Hover and focus are forced with data-state; selection and disabled use aria-selected and aria-disabled.

Pill
Underline

Both themes

  • Selected in the pill style is a fill and a lip; in the underline style it is a 3px bar and accent text. Each is a change of shape, not only colour.
  • Disabled uses aria-disabled="true" (still focusable and announced) in --color-fg-disabled. Prefer leaving a tab out over disabling it.
  • Loading belongs to the panel, not the tab: the panel shows a skeleton while family data arrives.

Responsive behaviour

  • The tablist never wraps. When tabs overflow (a 360px phone with long labels), the list scrolls sideways with the scrollbar hidden and the selected tab is scrolled into view.
  • Labels never truncate. If three tabs do not fit at 360px, shorten the words or use the small size in grown-up dialogs.
  • The underline rule spans the full width of its container at every breakpoint.

Theming

Pill tabs read the same tokens as chips: --color-bg-glass, --color-fg-muted, --color-bg-surface (hover) and the accent trio when selected. Underline tabs read --color-border-default for the rule, --color-accent for the bar and --color-fg-accent for the selected label. Counts read --color-bg-surface and --color-fg-accent. The night studio needs no special case.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

APG Tabs with automatic activation: moving focus to a tab shows its panel, because panels are local and instant. The list is role="tablist" with an aria-label; each tab is a <button role="tab"> with aria-selected and aria-controls; each panel is role="tabpanel", aria-labelledby its tab, and tabindex="0" when it has no focusable content. Use manual activation (Enter to show) only if a panel loads from the family server.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabEnters the tablist on the selected tab; the next Tab goes into the panel.
← / →Previous / next tab, wrapping at the ends; shows its panel.
Home / EndFirst / last tab.
Enter, SpaceShows the focused tab's panel (needed only with manual activation).

Focus

Roving tabindex: the selected tab has tabindex="0", the others -1. Focus shows the shared 3px --color-focus-ring at a 3px offset; the list's padding keeps the ring from being clipped by its scroll container.

Labelling

The tab's visible words are its name. A count is read as part of the name ("Songs 8"), so make the words carry it: Songs with a count reads fine; a bare number does not. The tablist's aria-label names what is being switched (Family high scores).

Motion

Panels swap instantly. The tab's background change is the only transition, and it is zeroed under reduced motion.

Touch

Medium tabs are 44px tall with 8px between them (pill) or 16px (underline). Small tabs are grown-up only.

Content

  • One or two words, nouns rather than verbs: This week, All time, Songs, Avatars.
  • Use the same order everywhere the same tabs appear; the first tab is the most used and is selected by default.
  • A leading emoji may help in grown-up managers (🎵 Songs, 😊 Avatars) and matches the button that opened them.
  • Never label a tab with a judgement: All time, not Best and Worst.
