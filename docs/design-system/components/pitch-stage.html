Component · Singing

Pitch stage

The playfield on the Sing screen. Note bars scroll from right to left, the singer's star rises and falls with her voice, and every landed note turns gold. Nothing on the stage ever marks a note as missed.

Overview

The pitch stage is the heart of the Sing screen and appears nowhere else. It turns pitch detection into a game a five-year-old understands: sing to move your star, catch the bars. Each bar is one note of the song, already shifted to the singer's voice range; its height on the stage is its pitch and its width is its length. The bar under the star's line is the note to sing now. A landed note (matched, octave-folded, on enough of the frames in which she was heard) turns gold and earns ten sparkles.

The stage is drawn with Canvas or WebGPU in the app (src/rendering/); the HTML parts documented here are the overlay layer and the contract the mocks and e2e tests share. Pair it with lyrics below and the coach live row under that.

  • Solo is the default.
  • Duet adds the buddy chip (.opponent-chip) in the top-left corner.
  • No-mic mode keeps the bars scrolling but leaves the star resting and quiet, shows no streak, and changes the stage label to SING ALONG AND HAVE FUN 🎶.
  • Practice plays only the tricky part, more slowly; the stage looks the same.

Anatomy

  1. Stage.stage: 300px tall (--size-stage), --radius-xl, --shadow-2, surface-to-sunken gradient with faint staff lines every 48px in --color-decor-line. touch-action: none.
  2. Note bar.note: a 32px pill (--size-rung) holding the syllable. Idle --color-decor-pale; .now adds a blue ring; .hit turns gold with a glow. Inside .note-track, which scrolls left.
  3. Singer's star.singer: a 64px disc (--size-art) with her avatar, fixed at 21% from the left; only its top moves. Blue ring at rest, gold ring .on-note, 75% opacity .quiet.
  4. Cheer.feedback-pop: Baloo 2 at --font-size-2xl in --color-fg-accent, pops in just above the star and floats away in one second.
  5. Streak chip.streak-chip: gold pill with a small lip, top right. Hidden (opacity 0) until .show.
  6. Stage label.stage-label: 12px black caps, letter-spaced, --color-fg-subtle, bottom left. Tells her what the stage wants.

Two more overlays complete the set: .countdown covers the whole stage before singing starts, and .opponent-chip sits top left in a duet. A decorative canvas.reactive-scene (aria-hidden) fills the stage behind everything.

  • Quiet (.singer.quiet) means she is not being heard: before the start, between phrases, and always in No-mic mode. The star dims to 75%; it never shakes or turns another colour. Silence is not a wrong note.
  • On note swaps the blue ring for gold while her pitch matches the current bar, so she sees "yes" before the bar even lands.
  • Streak shows after three landed notes in a row and fades out (not with a "lost" message) when the run ends. Every fifth note in a run also pops a gold toast.

Responsive behaviour

  • The stage spans the screen column (up to 470px) at a fixed 300px height. Pitch maps to height, so the vertical scale is identical on every device and the same song looks the same on a phone and a tablet.
  • The star is pinned at 21% of the width; bars are positioned from that line in rem, so a wider stage shows more upcoming notes rather than stretching them.
  • Overlay chips are absolutely placed in the corners with 16px insets; in a duet the buddy chip (left) and streak chip (right) both fit at 320px because their copy is capped (an emoji, a name up to 8 letters, two numbers).
  • In landscape on a phone the screen still scrolls vertically; the stage never goes full-screen, so the lyrics stay one short scroll away.

Theming

The stage background is --color-bg-surface → --color-bg-surface-sunken with --color-decor-line staff lines; idle bars are --color-decor-pale, landed bars the reward gradient, and the now-ring --color-accent-graphic. In the night studio the surfaces darken and rise above the canvas, the staff lines become --palette-night-650, and the gold of a landed note stays exactly the same, so success always looks like success. The Canvas/WebGPU renderer reads the same custom properties at start-up (getComputedStyle), never hex values.

/* The renderer resolves tokens once per theme change */
const css = getComputedStyle(stageEl);
const hit = css.getPropertyValue('--color-reward-bg');

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A labelled region: <section class="stage" aria-label="Pitch game — match the notes">. The singer's star is role="img" aria-label="You"; bars are text spans whose syllables duplicate the lyrics, so the stage adds no information a screen reader user would miss. No APG widget pattern applies: the stage takes no input except the voice. The real-time feed for assistive tech is the lyrics card and the status of the countdown, not every bar.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabReady state: moves to Tap to start ▶. While singing, the stage has nothing focusable; focus stays on the ⏸ Pause button.
Enter, SpaceOn Tap to start: begins the countdown. On ⏸: opens the Pause dialog.
EscRecommended: opens the Pause dialog, the same as ⏸, so a keyboard user can always stop the music.

Focus

When the countdown starts, focus moves from the removed start button to the ⏸ Pause button (never to <body>). After Pause → Keep singing, focus returns to ⏸.

Labelling

The countdown layer is aria-live="assertive" for "3, 2, 1, Go!" only; it is emptied (hidden) once singing starts. Cheers and the streak chip are not live: they would interrupt the singing every second. The buddy chip carries its words in text (🐱 Bea ✨80 🔥4); give it aria-label="Bea: 80 sparkles, 4 in a row" when shown.

Landed is never colour alone: a landed bar also gains a glow, the cheer names it in words, and the sparkle pill counts up.

Motion

The track scrolls continuously and the star follows pitch; both are the game itself and keep running under prefers-reduced-motion, but the decorative parts stop: cheers appear and disappear without the pop and float, the streak chip fades instantly, the reactive scene holds still and drifting notes in the sky stop. The only flashing is none: no element changes brightness more than three times a second.

Touch

The stage sets touch-action: none so a stray swipe while singing never scrolls the page. The only touch target on it is Tap to start, an extra-large 68px button.

Content

  • Cheers are two words at most plus one emoji, all positive and varied: Perfect! ✨, Great! 🌟, Yes! 💙, Sparkly! ✨, Wow! 🎉 (the five in CHEERS, src/screens/sing.ts). There is no cheer for a note that did not land.
  • Streak copy: 🔥 N in a row!. Never Streak lost or Combo broken.
  • Stage label in caps, under 30 characters, telling her what to do: SING TO MOVE YOUR STAR ↑↓, or in No-mic mode SING ALONG AND HAVE FUN 🎶.
  • Bar text is the syllable from the song data (syll), never a note name: children read words, not C4.
