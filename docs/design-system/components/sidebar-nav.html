Sidebar nav

The sidebar nav lists the sections of a wide grown-up page, grouped and collapsible: Settings, Songbook, Singers, Avatars. The current section sits on a soft sky gradient, never on colour alone.

Overview

Not in the mocks yet; added for the Grown-Ups Corner on tablets and desktops, and for the marketing and documentation sites. In the mocks the Grown-Ups Corner is one dialog with a row per area (mic timing, voice range, family content, singers). When it grows into a full page on a wide screen, the sidebar nav lists those areas so a grown-up can move between them without going back each time.

  • Use it on grown-up pages at the lg breakpoint and wider, with four or more sections.
  • Children never see it. The app's own sections are the four in the bottom nav (Home, Songs, Tips, Me), at every width.
  • For two or three views of the same thing, use tabs. For where you are in a deep page, add a breadcrumb.

Anatomy

  1. Group heading.sidenav-group > summary: overline, uppercase, --color-fg-subtle, a native <details> toggle, at least 24px tall.
  2. Link.sidenav-link: 44px tall (--target-comfortable), pill, --text-label in --color-fg-muted, optional leading emoji.
  3. Current link--gradient-cool body, --color-fg-default text, aria-current="page".
  4. Count (optional)A .pill-count pushed to the end of the link.

The nav is a column 256px wide (--layout-sidebar-width) with 4px between links.

  • Current uses the sky gradient (--gradient-cool) and full-strength text, plus aria-current="page". The text change from muted to default, and the filled pill shape, mean it reads without colour (D25).
  • Hover fills with --color-bg-subtle and darkens the text.
  • Expanded / collapsed is the group's <details open>; the browser exposes it as expanded or collapsed.
  • No disabled links: a section that is not available (the family server is offline) stays in the list and its page explains, in grown-up words, what happened and what to do.

Responsive behaviour

  • From lg (1024px) up, the nav is a sticky 256px column beside the content and scrolls on its own if it is taller than the viewport.
  • Below lg it moves above the content as a collapsed <details> titled with the current section (Songbook ▾); opening it shows the same list full width.
  • On phones the Grown-Ups Corner stays the single dialog the mocks show; the sidebar nav is a wide-screen enhancement, not a replacement.
  • Labels wrap inside a link rather than truncating; counts stay pinned to the end.

Theming

The sidebar reads --color-fg-muted, --color-fg-default, --color-bg-subtle (hover), --gradient-cool (current) and --color-fg-subtle (group headings). Counts use .pill-count's accent pair. In the night studio the cool gradient shifts to deep blues and the current text lightens, keeping the pair readable.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A labelled <nav> landmark of links (APG navigation landmark); collapsible groups are native <details>/<summary>, which follow the APG Disclosure pattern without extra ARIA. The current link has aria-current="page". Do not add role="menu": this is navigation, and Tab moves through it.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves through group headings and links in order. Links inside a closed group are skipped.
Enter, SpaceOn a group heading: opens or closes the group.
EnterOn a link: goes to that section.

Focus

The shared 3px --color-focus-ring at a 3px offset around the link pill. After navigation, focus moves to the new section's <h1> (or the skip link target), not left on the sidebar.

Labelling

The nav's aria-label names the area (Grown-Ups Corner), distinct from the app's Main navigation. Leading emoji are aria-hidden. Counts carry an aria-label that says what is counted (8 songs).

Motion

Groups open and close instantly (native <details>). Hover background changes are instant.

Touch

Links are 44px tall and full width, with 4px between them; group headings are at least 24px.

Content

  • Section names are short nouns, the same words the page title uses: Songbook, Singers, Avatars, Family high scores.
  • Group by where the data lives, because that is what grown-ups need to know: This device (sparkles, badges, mic timing) and Family server (profiles, songs, photos, high scores).
  • Use the domain words. Singers, not Users or Accounts; Start over, not Factory reset.
