Chip

Chips are round, glassy toggles that narrow a list: All, Easy, Medium, Brave on Songs, and the tip categories on Tips. The chosen chip turns toy-button blue with a small lip.

Overview

A chip filters what is already on the screen. Tapping 😤 Brave on Songs hides the easy songs; tapping 🌬️ Breathing on Tips shows only breathing tips. Nothing is saved and nothing is lost: tap ⭐ All and everything is back. The class is used 60 times across 14 mocks.

  • Use chips for a single row of 3 to 6 filters over one list, where one choice at a time is active.
  • Use tabs instead when each choice shows a different panel of content, not a filtered version of the same list.
  • Use a pill for a label that is not tappable (✓ In your key, 1 min). A chip is always a button.
  • The 🔥 streak chip on Sing and the opponent chip in a duet share the word but not the job: they are status labels and live with the pitch stage.

Anatomy

  1. Emoji (optional, leading)A face or picture that lets a pre-reader pick the right chip: 😊 Easy, 🙂 Medium, 😤 Brave.
  2. LabelNunito extra-bold 16px (--font-size-md), one or two words, never wraps.
  3. BodyPill, 44px tall (--target-comfortable), 8 × 16px padding, translucent --color-bg-glass so the sky shows through.
  4. Selected--color-accent with --color-fg-on-accent and a 3px lip (--lip-small) in --color-accent-edge.

Variants

There is one chip. Its two looks are unselected (glass) and selected (blue); the row it sits in decides the rest.

Song difficulty (Songs, filtered to Brave) and tip categories (Tips). The tips row scrolls sideways on a phone.
HTML
<div class="chip-row" role="group" aria-label="Song difficulty">
  <button class="chip" type="button" data-filter="0" aria-pressed="true">⭐ All</button>
  <button class="chip" type="button" data-filter="1" aria-pressed="false">😊 Easy</button>
  <button class="chip" type="button" data-filter="2" aria-pressed="false">🙂 Medium</button>
  <button class="chip" type="button" data-filter="3" aria-pressed="false">😤 Brave</button>
</div>

Sizes

Chips have one size, 44px tall; use tabs-sm for a denser grown-up filter.

States

Pointer and focus states are forced with data-state; selection uses the real aria-pressed.

Both themes

  • Selected is carried by the blue fill, the lip and aria-pressed="true"; the label stays the same so the chip never jumps in width.
  • Hover firms the glass to solid --color-bg-surface and darkens the label to --color-fg-default. The selected chip keeps its blue.
  • Pressed nudges the chip down 2px, a smaller version of the toy-button press.
  • Disabled exists for completeness (a family server category with no songs while offline). On child screens hide an empty filter instead; a chip that does nothing is confusing.
  • No results is not a chip state. If a filter leaves the list empty, the list shows its empty state and the chip stays selected so she can see why (Songs, no results).

Responsive behaviour

  • .chip-row never wraps: on a narrow phone it scrolls sideways with the scrollbar hidden, and the last chip is cut by the screen edge as a hint that there is more.
  • Chips keep white-space: nowrap; labels never truncate or wrap inside a chip.
  • The row has 2px side padding and 12px below, so focus rings and the selected lip are not clipped by the scroll container.
  • When a filter is applied by script, scroll the selected chip into view.

Theming

The chip reads --color-bg-glass, --color-fg-muted, --color-bg-surface and --color-fg-default (hover), and the accent trio --color-accent, --color-fg-on-accent, --color-accent-edge when selected, the same as the blue toy button. In the night studio the glass becomes a translucent navy and the selected chip lightens to brand blue with a deep navy label, exactly as the button does.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Each chip is a toggle button (APG Button, toggle variant): <button type="button" aria-pressed>. The row is role="group" with an aria-label that names the filter (Song difficulty). Selecting one chip un-presses the others.

The mocks mark the row role="tablist" and each chip role="tab", but there is no tab panel, so a screen reader promises something that is not there (D18). Filter chips with aria-pressed are the recommended fix. If a row really switches between panels, use Tabs with aria-controls and a role="tabpanel". Keep the data-filter and data-cat hooks either way; the page objects use them.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves through the chips one by one, then on to the list.
Enter, SpaceSelects the focused chip and filters the list.

Focus

The shared 3px --color-focus-ring at a 3px offset, around the pill. Focus stays on the chip after the list re-filters; it is never moved into the list.

Labelling

The visible words are the name; leading emoji are read but harmless ("smiling face Easy"). After a filter, announce the result politely through the page's status region: "3 Brave songs".

Motion

Background changes over --duration-base, the press over --duration-fast. Under reduced motion both are instant.

Touch

44px tall with 8px between chips. That is below the 52px child target, accepted because chips are wide (at least 72px) and a mis-tap only changes a filter, which is undone with one more tap.

Content

  • One or two words, Title Case only where the mock does (Brave Voice, Show Time are names).
  • The first chip is always All (with ⭐ on Songs) and is selected by default.
  • Difficulty is framed as courage, never as failure risk: Easy, Medium, Brave. Never Hard.
  • A leading emoji should be a face or object a pre-reader can match, and the same emoji used on the song row (⭐ Easy).
