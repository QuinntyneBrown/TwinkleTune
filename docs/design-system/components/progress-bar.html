Progress bar

Rounded tracks with a gradient fill: how far through the song she is, how close the next level is, and how many notes she landed. The number is always also said in words.

WCAG 2.2 AArole="progressbar"Status: stableClass .track / .fill

Overview

Use a progress bar to show a known amount of a whole: song position, level progress, notes landed. Every bar sits next to words that state the same value (You landed 18 of 24 notes! 🎯, 140 ✨ to Level 4!), so the bar is a picture of the sentence, never the only source. For today's sparkle goal use the progress ring; for an unknown wait use a spinner or the indeterminate bar below.

  • Blue (.fill) is plain progress: song position on the Sing screen, level progress on Me.
  • Gold (.fill-gold) is a reward: the landed-notes score on Results.
  • Pink (.fill-pink) is gentle progress, such as practising the tricky part. It is in the kit but no mock uses it yet.

A bar never represents failure. There is no red fill and no "low score" colour: 44% landed is the same gold as 95%.

Anatomy

  1. Label row (optional).progress-label: name on the left, value in words on the right, --text-label. Me uses its own .xp-row; Results puts the sentence in the card heading.
  2. Fill.fill: full height, --radius-full, width set inline as a percentage. Blue --gradient-fill, gold or pink gradients.
  3. Track.track: --color-track, --radius-full, 16px tall by default, clips the fill.
  4. Fill endThe blue gradient ends in --color-border-strong so the leading edge stands out from the track (D22).

Variants

Blue (Ada's level, 53%) · gold (18 of 24 notes landed) · pink (tricky part practice).

<div class="track" role="progressbar" aria-label="Level progress" aria-valuemin="0" aria-valuemax="100" aria-valuenow="53"><div class="fill" style="width:53%"></div></div>

Both themes

  • Empty shows the bare track, as for Bea at Level 1 (300 ✨ to Level 2!). An empty bar is a start line, not a zero score.
  • Animating in (.fill-animated) grows from 0 when a screen opens (Me, Results). The song-position bar on the Sing screen updates continuously and never uses it.

Responsive behaviour

  • The track fills its container's width; height never changes with width. The fill is a percentage, so it scales with the track.
  • In the Sing header the small track sits under the song title and shrinks with it (min-width: 0) when the key chips take space.
  • Label rows wrap: the value moves under the name rather than truncating.

Theming

The track is --color-track; fills are --gradient-fill (--color-accent-graphic → --color-border-strong), the reward gradient and --gradient-fill-gentle. Override per instance with those tokens only. In the night studio the track sinks to --palette-night-900 and the fills keep their hue, so gold progress still reads as a reward.

/* A gentle bar inside a reward card */
.score-card .track .fill { background: var(--gradient-fill-gentle); }

Accessibility

Role and pattern

role="progressbar" on the .track with aria-valuemin, aria-valuemax, aria-valuenow, and aria-valuetext when a count reads better than a percentage (18 of 24 notes). See the ARIA progressbar role; the APG has no separate widget pattern for it (the related APG Meter is for values that are not progress). Give it an aria-label or aria-labelledby the visible heading. Indeterminate bars drop aria-valuenow and set aria-busy="true". The mocks' bars have no role; the contract adds it.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabSkips the bar; it is not focusable.
Screen reader reading keysAnnounce "Notes landed, progress bar, 18 of 24 notes".

Focus

None. Never make a progress bar interactive; use a slider for an adjustable value.

Labelling

The song-position bar on the Sing screen is labelled "Song progress" and updates aria-valuenow at most once a second; it is not a live region. Results and Me bars are labelled by their sentence (aria-labelledby the heading).

Contrast

Fill end vs track (decorative)
Label text beside the track
Value text on the card

The bar itself is a graphic that duplicates the words next to it, so WCAG 1.4.11 does not require 3:1 between fill and track; the fill end was still darkened to --color-border-strong so the edge is easy to see (D22).

Motion

.fill-animated grows over --duration-progress with --ease-standard; the indeterminate fill sweeps over --duration-pulse. Under reduced motion the fill appears at its value and the indeterminate sweep runs once, then rests.

Touch

Not a touch target.

Content

  • Say the value in words next to the bar, framed as what she has: You landed 18 of 24 notes! 🎯, 140 ✨ to Level 4!, ✨ 760 sparkles collected.
  • Never show the gap as a loss (6 notes missed, 25% wrong).
  • Use counts children understand ("18 of 24") over percentages; percentages are for grown-up screens and the tuner.
