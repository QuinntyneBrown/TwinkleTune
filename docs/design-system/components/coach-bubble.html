Coach bubble

Twinkle, the star coach, and her speech bubble. She explains every screen in one warm sentence, cheers while the singer sings, and is the only voice that ever tells a child what to do next.

Overview

Use the coach bubble wherever a child needs guidance in words: what this screen is for, what to do next, or a cheer. It replaces instructions, helper text and error copy on child screens. Twinkle speaks in the first person, in one or two short sentences, and only ever encourages. For a passing event (a new badge, a family record) use a toast; for grown-up instructions use plain text or an alert.

  • Twinkle (svg.mascot) is a gold five-point star with a face, drawn inline so its paint reads tokens (.twinkle-body, .twinkle-eye, .twinkle-cheek, .twinkle-smile). She floats gently.
  • The bubble (.bubble) is a white rounded card in bold Nunito. .bubble-left adds the tail pointing back at Twinkle.
  • The row places the two side by side. Each screen has its own row class, kept from the mocks because e2e page objects rely on them.

Anatomy

  1. TwinkleInline SVG, aria-hidden="true". Body --color-reward-bg with a --color-reward-edge stroke, face --color-mascot-face, cheeks --color-gentle-bg. Floats on --duration-ambient.
  2. Tail.bubble-left::after: a 16px square rotated 45°, the bubble's colour, pointing at Twinkle.
  3. Bubble--color-bg-surface, --radius-lg, --shadow-1, 16px padding, bold Nunito at --font-size-md, line height 1.45.
  4. Emphasis<strong> for the one phrase that matters (tune every song just for you). At most one per bubble.

Variants

The bubble is the same everywhere; the row around it changes with the screen. Pick the row by screen, not by taste.

Sizes

Twinkle has three sizes; the bubble has one. Beside a bubble she is always small, so the words get the room.

.mascot-sm 72px (--size-avatar-pick) beside bubbles · .mascot 110px (--size-mascot) in dialogs such as Voice found · .mascot-lg 150px (--size-mascot-lg) on Welcome and error pages.

On the Sing screen the live row shrinks Twinkle further to --size-avatar and the bubble text to --font-size-sm, so the row never pushes the lyrics off a phone screen.

Responsive behaviour

  • Twinkle keeps her size (flex: 0 0 auto); the bubble takes the rest of the row (flex: 1) and wraps its text. The row never stacks: the tail always points sideways at Twinkle.
  • The bubble's longest line in the mocks (Find My Voice) wraps to four lines at 320px. That is the ceiling: if a line needs five, cut words.
  • .hello-bubble caps the bubble at --layout-dialog-max so the welcome line does not stretch across a tablet.
  • In the live row the sparkle pill keeps white-space: nowrap, so on a narrow phone the bubble shrinks, not the score.

Theming

Twinkle's paint lives in CSS, not in the SVG, so she follows the theme: .twinkle-body reads --color-reward-bg / --color-reward-edge, .twinkle-eye and .twinkle-smile read --color-mascot-face, and .twinkle-cheek reads --color-gentle-bg. Gold is the same in both studios, and her face stays dark navy so she reads at 4.5:1 or more in both. The bubble is --color-bg-surface, which rises above the night canvas in dark mode (D08).

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Twinkle is decoration: aria-hidden="true" on the SVG. The bubble is plain text in reading order. When its words change while the screen stays (Find My Voice steps, the listen message), make the bubble a polite live region, role="status" (ARIA status role; no APG widget pattern applies). On the Sing screen the coach line is not live: speech would talk over the singing, and the lyrics carry the real-time information.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabSkips the coach; nothing in it takes focus. Focus goes to the screen's one action, which the bubble describes.
Screen reader reading keysRead the bubble as a paragraph in DOM order, before the action it introduces.

Focus

None. Never put a button inside the bubble; the action belongs below it as the screen's one big button.

Labelling

The bubble needs no label; its text is its content. Emoji at the end of a line (💙 ✨ 🎈) are read aloud by most screen readers; keep at most one so the sentence stays short when spoken. Twinkle needs no alt text because the bubble already carries her words.

Motion

Twinkle floats up and down 12px over --duration-ambient, forever. Under prefers-reduced-motion the float stops after one cycle and she stays still. A changed bubble swaps its text without animating, so a live region is never announced twice.

Touch

Not a touch target. A tap on Twinkle does nothing; do not add easter eggs that a child might hunt for instead of singing.

Content

  • First person, present tense, one idea: I'll listen to your voice so I can tune every song just for you.
  • One or two sentences, ending in a question or an exclamation when it invites action (Ready?, Let's make today sparkle!).
  • Name the good thing first, then the next step: Your notes sparkled! The “My fair lady!” part was tricky — want to practice just that bit? "Tricky" is the strongest word allowed, and it always comes with an offer.
  • Never wrong, missed, try harder, oops or a score that reads as failure. Silence is not a mistake: during a quiet stretch Twinkle says Big breath — here comes the next part!
