Tile

Big, whole-surface tap targets that take a child somewhere. The blue Sing a Song hero tile is the one big obvious action on Home; the smaller pink and white tiles sit beneath it in pairs.

Overview

A tile is a card that is itself a link. The whole surface is the tap target, so a five-year-old can hit it anywhere and land where the picture promised. Tiles are for navigation between the main places of the app; they never perform an action on the spot (use a button for that).

  • Hero tile (.hero-tile) is the screen's one big obvious action: Sing a Song on Home, on the blue --gradient-hero with a glow, a white Let's go! ▶ pill, a tilted 🎤 and twinkling sparks. One per screen, and only on Home.
  • Pink tile (.tile.tile-pink) is the gentle destination: Warm-Ups.
  • White tile (.tile.tile-white) is the calm destination: My Songs.

Small tiles always come in a pair inside .tiles, a two-column grid, so the row never looks lopsided. Use a card when the surface shows information and holds its own controls, and a list for more than two destinations.

Anatomy

  1. TitleBaloo 2, --font-size-4xl, white on the hero gradient. Two or three words; it is most of the link's name.
  2. Promise lineNunito extra-bold, --font-size-md. Why it is worth tapping.
  3. Go pillA white pill with blue text (--color-fg-accent) and the toy lip. It looks like a button but is part of the one link.
  4. Big emoji🎤 at --font-size-8xl, tilted and wiggling, clipped by the tile. Decorative.
  5. SparksThree ✦ that twinkle on --duration-ambient. Decorative.

A small tile has three parts: an emoji (.ico, --font-size-4xl), a Baloo 2 title (strong, --font-size-lg) and a short hint (span, --font-size-xs, muted), on a --radius-lg surface with --shadow-1.

Sizes

Size is the variant: the hero tile is full width with 28 × 24px padding and a 32px radius; small tiles are half the column with 20 × 16px padding and a 24px radius. There are no size modifiers, and a small tile is never stretched to full width (use a card link instead).

Responsive behaviour

  • Tiles live in the 470px child column, so the pair stays two-up from 320px phones to tablets. At 320px each small tile is about 132px wide; titles wrap to two lines rather than shrink.
  • The hero tile's 🎤 is clipped by overflow: hidden and positioned from the bottom-right, so it never pushes the text; the text column keeps clear of it because the promise line is short.
  • Never put three small tiles in a row. A third destination becomes a list row or moves to the bottom nav.

Theming

The hero tile reads --gradient-hero, --color-fg-on-accent, --shadow-glow-accent and, for the go pill, --color-bg-surface, --color-fg-accent and --color-accent-edge. Small tiles read --color-gentle-bg / --color-gentle-fg (pink) and --color-bg-surface / --color-fg-muted (white). In the night studio the hero gradient deepens and its title stays white; the pink tile keeps its pastel so Warm-Ups looks the same at bedtime.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Each tile is one native <a href> (APG Link). The Let's go! pill is a <span> inside the link, not a nested button: interactive elements must never nest.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves between tiles in reading order: hero, then pink, then white.
EnterFollows the tile's link.

Focus

The shared 3px --color-focus-ring ring at 3px offset, outside the tile, so it shows against the sky rather than on the blue gradient.

Labelling

The link's name is its visible text in order: Sing a Song, Every song in YOUR key, Let's go!. Emoji (🎤, 🎈, 💖, ✦) are wrapped in aria-hidden="true" so the name is not padded with "microphone, sparkle, sparkle". Do not add an aria-label that hides the promise line.

Contrast

Hero title on the hero blue
Go pill text
Pink tile text
White tile hint
Focus ring on the sky

The hero gradient is measured at its darkest stop (--color-accent); the white title sits on the upper-left of the tile where that stop lies.

Motion

Hover lifts 2px and press shrinks to 97–98% over --duration-fast; the 🎤 wiggles and the sparks twinkle on --duration-ambient. Under prefers-reduced-motion the ambient loops stop and the press is instant.

Touch

The hero tile is about 200px tall and each small tile about 130px, far above the 52px child target, with a 16px gap between them.

Content

  • Titles are the place's name in two words or fewer, title case as in the mocks: Sing a Song, Warm-Ups, My Songs.
  • Hints describe the reward, not the task: 2-minute voice games, 4 songs mastered. Never a count of zero.
  • One emoji per tile, chosen to read without words for a child who cannot read yet.
