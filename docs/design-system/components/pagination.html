Pagination

Pagination splits a long family list into pages of round, toy-like page buttons: the family high-score history, a big songbook, the photo library. The current page is blue with a small lip.

Overview

Not in the mocks yet; added for the family-server managers. Today every list fits on one screen: eight songs, three singers, eight avatars. Pagination is for when a family list outgrows that, such as a songbook of 60 songs or a year of family high scores.

  • Use it on grown-up lists longer than about 25 rows, where a reader wants to jump to a known place.
  • Child lists never paginate: Songs, Tips and badges stay one scrolling list, because "page 2" is meaningless to a 5-year-old and hiding songs behind it breaks "one big obvious action".
  • For a short list that might grow a little, prefer a single Show more button at the end.

Anatomy

  1. Previous / nextAn arrow in a page button, labelled Previous page / Next page.
  2. Page button.pagination-link: 44px round-ended pill (--target-comfortable), white, --shadow-1, label type.
  3. Current pageAccent body, --color-fg-on-accent, 3px lip; aria-current="page".
  4. Gap.pagination-gap: "…" in --color-fg-subtle, hidden from assistive technology.

  • Current is blue with a lip and aria-current="page", and the page number is also given in text near the list (Page 5 of 12), so it never rests on colour.
  • Disabled applies only to previous on the first page and next on the last: the link loses its href, gets aria-disabled="true", flattens and greys out. It stays in place so the row does not shift.
  • Loading belongs to the list: keep the pagination, set aria-busy="true" on the list and show skeleton rows.

Responsive behaviour

  • From the sm breakpoint up, show first, last, current and one neighbour each side (at most 9 items).
  • Below sm, drop the neighbours: previous, current, a gap, last, next (at most 5 items, about 260px).
  • The list wraps (flex-wrap) rather than scrolling if it still does not fit; buttons never shrink below 44px.
  • Centre the pagination under its list on phones; align it to the list's end on wide screens.

Theming

Page buttons read --color-bg-surface, --color-fg-default, --shadow-1 and --color-bg-subtle (hover); the current page reads the accent trio; disabled reads --color-fg-disabled on --color-bg-subtle. In the night studio the white buttons become raised navy and the current page lightens to brand blue with a navy number, matching the toy button.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

No dedicated APG pattern; it is a navigation landmark (<nav>) labelled with what it pages (Pagination, family high scores), containing a list of links. The current page link has aria-current="page". When pages load in place without a URL change, use buttons instead of links and move focus to the list's heading after loading.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves through the page links. Disabled previous/next (no href) are skipped; gaps are not focusable.
EnterGoes to that page.

Focus

The shared 3px --color-focus-ring at a 3px offset. After a page change, focus goes to the top of the new results (the list heading), not back to the pagination.

Labelling

Number links are labelled Page 5; arrows Previous page / Next page. Gaps are aria-hidden. Announce the new position through the page's status region: "Page 5 of 12, family high scores".

Motion

None on the control. If the list scrolls to its top after a page change, the scroll is instant under reduced motion.

Touch

44px buttons with 8px between them (--target-comfortable). Grown-up lists only.

Content

  • Numbers for lists without a natural order; Newer / Older for time-ordered lists such as photo uploads.
  • Always give the position in words near the list: Page 2 of 4 or Songs 26–50 of 60.
  • Never a page-size picker on family lists; choose a sensible size (25) and keep it.
