Component · Data display

Week streak

Seven round day discs that show which days this week the singer sang. A sung day gets a gold star; a day not sung yet is a soft dot, never a cross.

Overview

The week streak sits under ⭐ This week on the Me screen. It celebrates the day streak (consecutive days sung) without ever counting a gap against the singer. Use it only for the current week, Monday to Sunday, for the active singer. Streak data stays on the device (localStorage), so it renders offline and never waits for the family server.

  • Done (.day.done) is a gold disc with ⭐: she sang that day.
  • Today (.day.today) gets the blue ring. Before she sings it shows 🎤 as an invitation; once she sings it is also .done and shows ⭐.
  • Idle (plain .day) is a pale disc with a dot: a day not sung, or a day still to come. Past and future idle days look the same on purpose, so a missed day is never singled out.

For the running count inside a song (🔥 7 in a row!) use the streak chip on the pitch stage; for today's sparkle goal use the progress ring.

Anatomy

  1. Week card.card.week: white card, --radius-xl, --shadow-2, days spread with justify-content: space-between, 16 × 20px padding.
  2. Day disc.day i: a 40px circle (--size-control-sm) holding one emoji at --font-size-lg.
  3. Weekday letterNunito black, 12px (--font-size-xs), --color-fg-muted. Hidden from assistive tech; the full day name is in visually hidden text.
  4. Today ring--shadow-ring-accent plus the pale-to-aqua gradient, and aria-current="date" so "today" is never carried by the ring alone.
  5. Idle dot--color-mark-idle disc with a middle dot in --color-fg-subtle. Calm, not empty-looking, and never a cross.

  • Loading does not exist: streak data is local and synchronous, so the week renders with the screen.
  • New week: on Monday every disc returns to idle and Monday is today. The day streak count lives in the 3-Day Streak badge and the home streak chip, not in this card, so the reset never reads as a loss.
  • No error state: if storage is unavailable (private mode), show the week with today invited (🎤) and every other day idle.

Responsive behaviour

  • The card fills the 470px screen column (--layout-screen-max); the seven discs spread evenly with space-between, so they breathe on a tablet and tighten on a phone.
  • The discs keep their 40px size at every width; seven discs plus the 20px side padding need 320px, so below 360px the card's side padding drops to --space-3 rather than shrinking the discs.
  • Never wrap the week onto two rows and never scroll it: a week is always read as one line.

Theming

The discs read semantic tokens only: --color-mark-idle and --color-fg-subtle for idle, the reward pair (--color-reward-bg-subtle → --color-reward-bg, label --color-reward-fg) for done, and --shadow-ring-accent with the decor gradient for today. In the night studio the idle disc becomes --palette-night-650 on a rising surface, and gold stays gold: a sung day looks the same in both themes.

/* A seasonal week: swap the done gradient, keep the semantics */
.week.holiday .day.done i { background: var(--gradient-fill-gentle); color: var(--color-gentle-fg); }

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A static list: <ul aria-label="This week's singing"> with one <li class="day"> per day. No APG widget pattern applies because nothing is interactive; it follows the APG structural roles guidance for lists. The mocks use <section> and <div>; the list keeps the class names and adds the count ("list, 7 items") that a screen reader needs.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabSkips the week entirely; no day takes focus.
Screen reader list keys (L / I in browse mode)Jump to the week, then read day by day: "Monday, sang … Thursday, today, sang … Friday".

Focus

None: the component holds no focusable element. Do not make days tappable; a streak is something to look at, not a control.

Labelling

The emoji and the weekday letter are aria-hidden; each day carries visually hidden text with the full day name and, when true, "today" and "sang". Today also gets aria-current="date", so "today" is never carried by the blue ring alone (D25). Idle days say only the day name: never "missed".

Contrast

Weekday letter on the card
Idle dot on the idle disc's card
Star mark on gold
Star mark on the pale end of gold

Motion

The card rises in with the screen (.rise, --duration-deliberate). Days do not animate individually. Reduced motion zeroes the entrance.

Touch

Not a touch target. Because nothing responds to a tap, avoid any hover or pressed styling that would suggest otherwise.

Content

  • Weekday letters are single capitals, Monday first: M T W T F S S. Hidden text spells the day in full.
  • Done is always ⭐; today-not-sung is always 🎤 (an invitation); idle is always a middle dot ·. Never ✗, ❌, 😢 or an empty red ring.
  • The section heading is ⭐ This week. Never Missed days, Streak lost or a percentage.
