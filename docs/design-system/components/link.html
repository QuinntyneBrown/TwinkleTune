Link

Quiet links are the soft second choice under a big button: Home, Later, Close, Keep my stuff. A dotted underline says "you can tap me" without competing with the toy button above.

Overview

A link is the calm way out. Every dialog and most child screens have one big toy button; the quiet link sits beneath it for the choice that is fine but not the point: Home 🏠 under the results, Later in the Find My Voice top bar, Close under a manager, Keep my stuff under a reset. It is used 55 times across the mocks.

  • Quiet link (.link-quiet) is extra-bold muted text with a dotted underline. It can be an <a href> (it goes somewhere) or a <button> (it closes a dialog), and looks the same either way.
  • Inline link is a plain <a> inside running text in grown-up copy, coloured --color-fg-link and underlined. It is new in the system, for the Grown-Ups Corner and marketing pages; child screens do not put links inside sentences.

Do not use a link for the main action, for anything destructive, or as a substitute for a button a child must find. If the screen would be stuck without it, it is a button.

Anatomy

  1. LabelNunito extra-bold, inherits the size around it (14px in dialogs via .modal .link-quiet). Colour --color-fg-muted.
  2. Emoji (optional, trailing)One, at the end, inside the link. Leading emoji are used only on Welcome (🔒 Grown-Ups Corner) where they tell two links apart.
  3. UnderlineA 1px dotted bottom border in --color-border-strong; turns solid and blue on hover. The hit area is at least 24px tall (--target-min).

Later
Later
  • Hover turns the text --color-fg-link-hover and the dotted line solid. The change is in shape as well as colour, so it reads without colour vision.
  • Pressed darkens to --color-fg-default for the moment of the tap.
  • Disabled exists only for the button form, during a grown-up save, so Close cannot be tapped mid-write. An <a> is never disabled; remove it instead.
  • There is no visited style: the app is a set of screens, not documents.

Responsive behaviour

  • Quiet links centre under the button stack (.result-ctas > .link-quiet) at every width.
  • On Welcome the two links sit on one line (.grownups, 16px apart) and wrap onto two lines below about 340px; they never truncate.
  • In the top bar (.topbar .link-quiet.spacer) the link replaces the right-hand spacer and shrinks to 14px so the title stays centred.

Theming

The quiet link reads --color-fg-muted, --color-fg-link-hover, --color-fg-default and --color-border-strong; the inline link reads --color-fg-link and --color-fg-link-hover. In the night studio muted text lifts to a pale blue-grey and the dotted line to a mid navy, both keeping their ratios. No component tokens are needed.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

<a href> when it navigates (APG Link), <button type="button"> when it acts in place, such as closing a dialog (APG Button). The look is shared; the element follows the behaviour, so a screen reader announces the truth.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to and from the link, after the buttons above it.
EnterFollows an <a> or activates a button-link.
SpaceActivates the <button> form only.

Focus

The shared 3px --color-focus-ring outline at a 3px offset, drawn around the text box.

Labelling

The visible words are the name, and they make sense out of context: Back to songs, not Back, when the screen has more than one way back. Leading emoji on Welcome are read aloud; that is acceptable because 🔒 and 👨‍👧‍👧 add meaning there.

Motion

None. Colour and underline style change instantly.

Touch

The link is at least 24px tall (--target-min) and always has 16px or more of space from the button above it, which meets WCAG 2.5.8. It is never the only way to do something a child needs.

Content

  • Gentle, short, and never a dead end: Maybe later, Back to singing, Keep my stuff. Each one tells the child she is safe.
  • Never Cancel, Skip or No thanks: they sound like giving up. Later says the door stays open.
  • For a destructive question, the link names what is kept (Keep my stuff), the button names what is lost (Yes, delete / Keep it).
  • Inline grown-up links describe the destination (How the family server works), never click here.
