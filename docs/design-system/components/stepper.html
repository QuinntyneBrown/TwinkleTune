Stepper

Three little dots at the top of Find My Voice. Blue for the steps she has done, a bigger glowing gold dot for the step she is on, and plain dots for what is still to come.

Overview

The stepper shows how far along a short, fixed sequence she is. TwinkleTune has one: Find My Voice, which takes three steps (get ready, sing your lowest note, sing your highest note) before the Your voice is found! 🎉 dialog. The dots tell her the end is near; Twinkle's coach bubble tells her what to do now.

  • Use it only for a linear sequence of two to five steps on one screen, where each step replaces the last.
  • The dots are not buttons. She cannot jump ahead or back by tapping them; the screen's actions move her on, and the top bar's back button or Later leaves.
  • For progress through a song, use a progress bar; for a sparkle goal, a progress ring.

Anatomy

  1. Done dot.step-dot.done: filled --color-accent.
  2. Now dot.step-dot.now: --color-reward-bg gold, scaled to 130%, with the reward ring --shadow-ring-reward.
  3. To-come dot.step-dot: --color-bg-surface with --shadow-1, 14px (--size-dot).
  4. Row and label.steps: centred, 8px gaps, 20px below. role="img" with an aria-label that says the step in words.

Variants

One variant: a row of three dots on Find My Voice. Longer sequences add dots; they never add numbers, labels or connecting lines.

Sizes

The stepper has one size: 14px dots, with the current one growing to about 18px.

States

Each dot has three states, set by class; the row has one state per step of the sequence. Dots are not interactive, so there is no hover, focus or disabled.

Both themes

  • Now is told by size and the glowing ring as well as gold, so it is distinguishable without colour.
  • There is no "failed" or "skipped" dot. If she does not land a note, the step simply stays current and Twinkle encourages her to try again.
  • When the last step completes, the row is not updated to all-blue; the Voice found dialog opens over it.

Responsive behaviour

  • Centred under the top bar at every width; the row is about 60px wide and never wraps.
  • Dots scale with the root font size, so at 200% zoom they are 28px.
  • Leave 20px (--space-5) below the row so the grown now dot never touches Twinkle's coach bubble.

Theming

Reads --color-bg-surface and --shadow-1 (to come), --color-accent (done), --color-reward-bg and --shadow-ring-reward (now). No component tokens. Gold stays gold in the night studio; the to-come dots become night surfaces, still lifted by the shadow, and done turns the lighter brand blue.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

There is no APG widget for a non-interactive step indicator. The row is a single image of the progress, role="img", whose aria-label says the step in words (Step 2 of 3: your lowest note); its dots are presentational. Update the label whenever the step changes. If steps ever become navigable, switch to an <ol> of links with aria-current="step".

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabSkips the stepper; it is not interactive. Focus moves from the top bar to the screen's main action.
Screen reader browse keysRead the stepper as one image with its step label.

Focus

The stepper never takes focus. When a step changes, focus stays on the action she used; announce the new instruction through Twinkle's coach bubble (a polite live region; the mocks' bubble is not one yet) rather than the stepper label.

Labelling

The mocks put aria-label="Progress" on a plain <div>. A generic element's aria-label is not announced, and "Progress" would not say which step anyway. The contract gives the row role="img" and a label with the step number, total and name.

Contrast

The dots are supplementary: the step is always also in words (the label and Twinkle's bubble), so the pale to-come dot on the sky is exempt as decoration. The done and now dots still meet 3:1 so they read as marks.

Motion

The dots change state without animation; the row only joins the screen's staggered entrance (.rise), which reduced motion removes. The now dot's size is static, not a pulse.

Touch

Not a target. Because the dots look small and tappable, nothing must happen when they are tapped; never give them pointer cursors or hover effects.

Content

  • No visible words on the stepper itself. The step's instruction is Twinkle's: Sing your lowest comfy note — a big cozy “laaa” — and hold it!.
  • The hidden label follows one pattern: Step N of M: name, with the name in a child's words (your lowest note, not low range capture).

function setStep(row, i, names) {
  [...row.children].forEach((d, n) => { d.classList.toggle('done', n < i); d.classList.toggle('now', n === i); });
  row.setAttribute('aria-label', `Step ${i + 1} of ${names.length}: ${names[i]}`);
}
