Component · Navigation

Bottom nav

A floating white pill at the bottom of the four top-level screens, with a big emoji and one word for each: Home, Songs, Tips and Me. The screen she is on glows in a soft aqua bubble.

WCAG 2.2 AA<nav aria-label> + aria-current="page"Status: stableClass .bottomnav

Overview

The bottom nav moves between the four places a child returns to again and again: Home 🏠, Songs 🎵, Tips 💡 and Me ⭐. It floats over the sky at the bottom of those screens, within easy reach of a thumb.

  • Show it only on Home, Pick a Song, Twinkle's Tips and My Star Journey. Every other screen is a step inside a task (Sing, Results, Find My Voice, Duet Time, Who's singing?, Welcome) and uses .screen--nonav with a top bar back button instead, so she cannot wander off mid-song.
  • Always the same four items in the same order. Never add a fifth, never hide one, never reorder by use.
  • It is not for actions. The one big action of the screen stays in the screen.

Anatomy

  1. Icon.ico: one emoji at --font-size-2xl, decorative (aria-hidden="true").
  2. Current itemaria-current="page" (and .active): aqua-to-pale bubble, full-strength ink.
  3. LabelOne word, Nunito extra-bold at --font-size-xs (12px), --color-fg-muted. The link's name.
  4. Pill.bottomnav: --color-bg-surface, --radius-full, 8px padding, --shadow-3; fixed 16px above the bottom edge, centred, at --z-sticky; min(440px, 100vw − 32px) wide.

Variants

One variant. The only thing that changes from screen to screen is which item is current.

🏠Home 🎵Songs 💡Tips ⭐Me
On Home. The pill floats over the sky; the screen above reserves 128px (--layout-bottomnav-clearance) so nothing hides behind it.

Sizes

The bottom nav has one size. Each item is at least 52px tall and shares the pill's width equally.

States

Hover and focus are forced here with data-state; current uses the real attribute, aria-current="page".

Both themes

  • Current is shown by the bubble and by full-strength ink against the muted labels, and announced by aria-current="page"; it does not rely on the aqua tint alone.
  • Tapping the current item does nothing new: it reloads nothing and keeps scroll position.
  • No disabled or badge state. Every place is always open, and there is no unread count to chase.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 472px the pill is the viewport width less 32px; above it, 440px centred. It never becomes a side rail on tablets or desktops: the child app is one column everywhere.
  • Items share the width with justify-content: space-around; at 320px each item is about 70px wide, enough for its one-word label.
  • Screens with the nav keep --layout-bottomnav-clearance (128px) of bottom padding so the last card scrolls clear of the pill.
  • On devices with a home indicator, add the safe-area inset to the 16px bottom offset.

Theming

The pill reads --color-bg-surface and --shadow-3; labels --color-fg-muted, hover --color-bg-subtle, current --color-decor-aqua to --color-decor-pale with --color-fg-default. No component tokens. In the night studio the pill becomes a raised night surface and the current bubble a lighter navy gradient, with light ink at 4.5:1 or better.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <nav aria-label="Main navigation"> landmark of plain links, with aria-current="page" on the current one (APG Landmark Regions). It is not a tab list: each item loads a different screen.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves through Home, Songs, Tips, Me after the screen's content (the nav comes last in the DOM).
EnterGoes to that screen.

Focus

The shared 3px --color-focus-ring at a 3px offset around the item's rounded bubble. Because the nav floats over content, a focused control near the bottom of the screen must scroll clear of it; the clearance padding guarantees that (WCAG 2.4.11).

Labelling

The word is the name; the emoji is aria-hidden="true", so a screen reader says "Songs, link" rather than "musical note Songs, link". The landmark label is Main navigation.

Contrast

Label on the pill
Hovered label
Current label on the aqua bubble

Motion

Only the hover background fades, over --duration-base; reduced motion makes it instant. Screen changes use the view transition, which reduced motion also removes.

Touch

Each item is at least 52px tall (8px padding, 24px emoji, 12px label) and roughly 70–100px wide, with 4px between bubbles: comfortably above the child target.

Content

  • One word each, exactly: Home, Songs, Tips, Me.
  • Emoji, exactly: 🏠, 🎵, 💡, ⭐. They match the screens' titles (Pick a Song 🎵, Twinkle's Tips 💡).
  • No counts, dots or "new" badges.
