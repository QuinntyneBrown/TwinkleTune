Spinner

A small round loading ring that always comes with words beside it. It appears only while the family server answers, never in front of solo singing.

Overview

Not in the mocks yet; added for the family-server screens (Who's singing, the song board, the Grown-Ups Corner managers). Today those mocks show a plain sentence while they wait (Looking for the family… ☁️, Loading…); the spinner gives that sentence a visible heartbeat without changing its words.

Use a spinner for a short wait of unknown length, roughly 300ms to 5s, when nothing of the result's shape is known. Prefer a skeleton when you know the layout that is coming (a profile grid, a list of rows), and a progress bar when you know how much is left. Solo play never waits for the network, so a spinner never stands between a child and a song.

  • Loading line (.loading-line): spinner plus a sentence. The default.
  • In a button: a busy button (aria-busy="true") draws its own spinner; see Button states.
  • Alone, large (.spinner-lg): only centred in an empty dialog body, still with words under it.

Anatomy

  1. RingA 3px circle (--border-width-thick) in --color-track.
  2. ArcThe top quarter in --color-accent, turning once per --duration-progress.
  3. WordsBold Nunito in --color-fg-muted, 12px to the right. Say what is being fetched, warmly. Required.

  • Show the spinner only after 300ms; most LAN answers arrive sooner, and a flash of spinner is worse than none.
  • The API client probes /health with a short timeout, so a spinner never runs for long: after the timeout the screen falls back to cached or bundled data.

Responsive behaviour

  • The spinner keeps its size at every width. The loading line is inline-flex: its words wrap beside the ring, and the ring stays top-aligned with the first line.
  • Centre a loading line where the content will appear (the profile grid area, the board list), so nothing jumps sideways when the content arrives.

Theming

The ring reads --color-track and the arc --color-accent; the words read --color-fg-muted. In the night studio the track sinks and the arc lightens to brand blue, so the arc stays visible on dark surfaces. Override with tokens only.

/* A gold spinner inside a reward card */
.score-card .spinner { border-color: var(--color-reward-bg-subtle); border-top-color: var(--color-reward-edge); }

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The ring is decoration (aria-hidden="true"). The loading line is the message: role="status" (ARIA status role, a polite live region; no APG widget pattern applies). The region the spinner stands in for sets aria-busy="true" until its content arrives, then the status line is removed.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabSkips the spinner. Focus stays where it was (for a busy button, on the button).

Focus

A spinner never takes focus. When loaded content replaces it, do not move focus unless the user asked for that content (opening a dialog moves focus into it as usual).

Labelling

The visible words are the accessible message; never use a spinner with only aria-label="Loading". A busy button keeps its label in the DOM so it is still announced (Save, busy).

Motion

One full turn per --duration-progress, linear. Under prefers-reduced-motion the turn runs once and stops, leaving a still ring with its arc; the words carry the meaning.

Touch

Not a touch target. A busy button ignores taps until it finishes.

Content

  • Say what is happening, in the screen's voice: Looking for the family… ☁️, Fetching family high scores…, Saving Frère Jacques….
  • End with an ellipsis (…), one character, not three dots.
  • Never Please wait, Loading data or a percentage you do not know.
