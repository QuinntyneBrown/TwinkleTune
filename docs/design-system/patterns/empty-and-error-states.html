Pattern

Empty and error states

An empty section says what will appear there and how to earn it. A failure says what still works and offers one way to keep singing. Neither ever sounds like a problem the child caused or has to solve.

Overview

TwinkleTune is offline first: solo play runs entirely on the device, and every family-server feature is a progressive enhancement. So most "errors" are really reduced states. When the family server is unreachable, the bundled songbook and the profile on this device carry on, and the screen says so in a friendly line.

The family of states and the parts that show them:

  • Empty: nothing here yet. A dashed card (.new-songs, or the system's .empty-state) or, inside a dialog, a .quiet-note.
  • No results: a filter left nothing. The mocks show only the gift note; the system adds a compact empty state that offers the way back.
  • Offline / reduced: the family server is unreachable but the screen still works. A .why banner above the list; server-only buttons are simply not offered.
  • Failed: a whole screen or dialog needs the server. A dashed card with 📡, a plain sentence and one button back to solo singing; in a dialog, a .quiet-note.
  • No-mic mode: no microphone. She sings for fun; nothing is scored.

Structure

Formula for every empty or failed state, in this order: emoji (what kind of thing: 🎤 singing, 🎁 new songs, 📡 the server) → one or two plain sentences (what is happening, then what still works or how to fill it) → at most one action that keeps her singing.

No-mic mode

When the browser's permission prompt is declined, mic-ask offers Yes, let's sing! 🎤, Sing just for fun 🎶 and Back to songs. When the microphone is blocked for the app, mic-denied says Twinkle can't hear you 💙 and asks her to fetch a grown-up. In no-mic mode the stage label reads SING ALONG AND HAVE FUN 🎶, sparkles stay at 0, and Results celebrates the sing-along (What a fun sing-along, Ada! 🎉, You sang the whole song!) with no stars, marks, badges or high scores.

Responsive behaviour

  • Empty and failed cards fill the column width (up to 470px) and centre their text at every size; the emoji sits above the words.
  • .empty-state--compact puts the emoji beside the words; below 360px it is still one row because the copy is one sentence.
  • The .why banner keeps its emoji on the left and wraps its sentence; it never truncates.
  • A failed state replaces only the region that failed. The top bar, Twinkle and the bottom navigation stay, so she can always go elsewhere.

Accessibility

  • Empty and failed messages are plain text in the reading order where the content would be, not toasts, so they are found by reading the page and remain until the state changes.
  • When a list region switches from loading to empty or failed, it drops aria-busy; a polite live region on the region announces the new line once.
  • Decorative emoji (🎁, 📡, 🎤 as icons) are aria-hidden="true"; the sentence carries the meaning.
  • Locked badges are named with their goal (Sing three days in a row), not "locked" or "failed", and are not shown by greyness alone: the 🔒 replaces the badge emoji.
  • The one action is a real link or button with a verb label (Keep singing solo ▶), reachable by Tab.
