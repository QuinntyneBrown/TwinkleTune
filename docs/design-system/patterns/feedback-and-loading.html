Pattern

Feedback and loading

Twinkle reacts to everything a singer does, but always with the lightest touch that fits the moment: a cheer beside her star, a line in Twinkle's bubble, a toast at the top. Loading is just as gentle: a quiet line, never a wall of spinners.

Overview

Feedback in TwinkleTune has one job: tell a child what went well and what to try next. There is no negative channel. A note that slips by simply stays blue, silence is not a wrong note, and a failed server call never reaches her as an error.

The channels, from closest to the action to furthest away:

  1. On the stage: a landed note turns gold (.note.hit), her star glows gold (.singer.on-note), and a cheer pops beside it (.feedback-pop: Perfect! ✨, Yes! 💙). See pitch stage.
  2. The streak chip: from three landed notes in a row, 🔥 7 in a row! shows in the stage's corner.
  3. Twinkle's bubble: a sentence of encouragement or a hint, under the stage and on most screens. See coach bubble.
  4. Toasts: short events at the top of the screen (sparkles, every fifth streak, a duet partner finishing, a removal). See notifications.
  5. Inline results: the score card, stars and the gold .badge-toast on Results.

Loading

Most screens render straight from the device and never load. Only family-server data waits, and on the home LAN it normally answers at once, so loading is as small as it can be.

Responsive behaviour

  • The stage is a fixed 300px tall at every width; notes scroll at 90px per beat and the "now" line stays 21% from the left, so cheers always appear in the same place relative to her star.
  • The live row keeps the bubble and sparkle pill side by side; the bubble wraps to more lines rather than the pill dropping below.
  • Toasts are centred at the top with 24px clear on each side; long toasts wrap inside their pill instead of widening past the column.
  • Loading lines are centred in the space the content will fill, so the layout does not jump when rows arrive.

Accessibility

Announcing

The toast host is a polite live region (role="status" aria-live="polite", D16), so toasts are read without moving focus. Cheers and pill colours are decorative and are not announced note by note: a screen reader would talk over the music. The streak and the sparkle total are text and can be read on demand; Results states everything in words (You landed 18 of 24 notes!).

Loading

A list region that is loading gets aria-busy="true" and the quiet line as its only content. Spinners and skeletons are aria-hidden; the words beside them carry the meaning. A busy button keeps its accessible name while its label is visually hidden.

Not by colour alone

A landed note turns gold and raises a worded cheer; the streak chip says the number; the sparkle pill shows a count. Nothing relies on gold versus blue to be understood (WCAG 1.4.1).

Motion

Cheers float up and fade over --duration-progress; toasts pop in over --duration-slow; spinners turn and skeletons pulse. Under prefers-reduced-motion cheers appear and disappear without travel, toasts fade, spinners stop as a ring and skeletons hold still. Nothing flashes more than three times a second.

Content

  • Cheers are one or two words plus one emoji: Perfect! ✨, Yes! 💙. They are praise for what she did, never a grade.
  • Twinkle's lines are one sentence, second person, present tense: You're doing wonderfully! 💙, Your songs are tuned and ready. Let's make today sparkle! ✨
  • Loading copy is short and calm: Loading… for grown-up lists, a Twinkle-style line for children (Looking for the family… ☁️). Never Please wait.
  • Counts are numerals: 🔥 7 in a row!, +5 sparkles ✨, 18 of 24 notes.
