Pattern

Forms

TwinkleTune has very few forms, and most of them belong to grown-ups. A child types her name and a room code; a grown-up edits songs, avatars and singers on the family server. Both keep what was typed, check on submit, and never make a child feel she did something wrong.

Overview

Forms fall into two families with different rules, because their readers are different people.

  • Child forms: the Welcome name card, the singer editor reached from Who's singing?, and the duet room code. One or two big fields (.name-input, 2xl Baloo type), an avatar picker, one blue button. They prefer a sensible default to an error: an empty Welcome name is saved as Superstar.
  • Grown-up forms: the song editor and the avatar editor, behind the parent gate in the Grown-Ups Corner. Smaller .field controls with .field-label, side-by-side pairs, a JSON textarea, a Preview and a Create button. These may say plainly what needs fixing.

Every form here lives in a dialog or a centred card; there are no full-page forms. Building blocks: text field, select, form field, form layout, inline message, alert and button.

Structure

Order inside a form dialog, top to bottom: emoji or title (h3, the dialog's name) → validation box (grown-ups, only after a failed submit) → fields in reading order → the one primary action (or a .field-actions pair for Preview + Create) → a quiet link to leave without saving (Cancel).

  • Labels sit above the control (.field-label, Baloo 2 bold, 16px), never as placeholders. A placeholder may show an example (Tiger, ABCD).
  • Pairs: short related fields share a .field-pair (Emoji + Tempo, Difficulty + Art style). Long fields (Title, the phrases JSON) take the full width.
  • Hints for unusual input go in the label or a .field-hint: Phrases & notes (JSON — midi 60 = middle C, times in beats).
  • Actions: one blue primary. Preview is white beside it in .field-actions. Destructive actions (Remove singer 🗑️) are pink and open a confirm dialog; they are never the form's submit.

Validation timing

  • Validate on submit, never on each keystroke or on blur. A child who pauses mid-name should not be interrupted.
  • After a failed submit, re-check a field as it changes and remove its error the moment it is fixed. Never add new errors before the next submit.
  • The device checks the same rules the server enforces (title, tempo 40–220, notes within 48–84, durations positive, no overlaps, span within 16 semitones). The server still has the final word; its messages arrive in the same box.

Responsive behaviour

  • Form dialogs are centred at every width: .modal up to 340px, .modal-wide up to 440px, scrolling inside at 84% of the viewport height so the actions never leave the screen.
  • .field-pair keeps two columns down to 320px; the fields inside are short by design. A pair whose labels would wrap to three lines becomes two full-width fields.
  • When the on-screen keyboard opens, the focused field scrolls into view inside the dialog; nothing is positioned against the bottom of the viewport.
  • Inputs are at least 16px so mobile browsers do not zoom on focus; child inputs are 24px.

Accessibility

Labelling

Every control has a <label for> (the mocks are a step ahead of the app here). The room code field is named by its visible heading (Type the room code 🔑) through aria-labelledby, or aria-label="Room code". The photo input is named Photo. The avatar row is a role="group" named by Pick an avatar, each button named and pressed with aria-label + aria-pressed (D17).

Errors

Invalid fields get aria-invalid="true" and aria-describedby pointing at their message. After a failed grown-up submit, focus moves to the .form-summary (tabindex="-1"), whose links move focus to each field. Error text is never carried by colour alone: .field-error starts with 💙 and says the rule in words (WCAG 3.3.1, 3.3.3, 1.4.1).

Motion and touch

Dialogs pop in over --duration-slow; the parent gate's wrong-answer wiggle is the only motion tied to input and stops under reduced motion. Child inputs are 68px tall; grown-up fields 44px; avatar buttons 64px with 16px between them.

Content

  • Child messages are Twinkle talking: short, warm, about what to do. Every singer needs a name! 💙, Codes have 4 letters!. Never Invalid, Required or Error.
  • Grown-up messages name the rule: Tempo must be between 40 and 220 BPM., Note 3: midi 96 is outside 48–84 (C3–C6). Point at the item by its position or name.
  • Failures say what happened and what to do, never the raw transport message. Failed to fetch becomes The family server didn't answer. Check it is switched on, then try again. (D19)
  • Buttons say the outcome: Add singer ⭐, Save changes, Create ✨, Join the duet! 🎤, Add ✨. The leave link is Cancel, or Back on the duet card.
