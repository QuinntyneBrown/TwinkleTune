Pattern

Notifications

TwinkleTune's only notifications are toasts: small pills that pop in at the top, say one happy or helpful thing, and float away. White confirms, gold celebrates, pink nudges. There is no red toast.

Overview

A toast tells a singer that something just happened, without stopping her. Toasts never take focus, cannot be tapped and leave on their own. There are no push notifications, no notification centre and no unread badges: privacy first, and nothing to nag a child.

Three looks, defined by the toast component:

  • White (.toast, the info state): a neutral confirmation. Removed 💙, Song deleted 💙, Avatar removed 💙.
  • Gold (.toast-gold, success): good news. +5 sparkles ✨, 🔥 5 in a row!, a captured note, a saved song, a singer arriving, 🏆 New family record!
  • Pink (.toast-pink, warning and danger): a gentle nudge that says what to try next, and the same soft pink when something did not work. That sounds close to your low note — reach a little higher! 🐦, Can't reach the family server 📡.

When to use

Use a toast forUse something else for
Confirming an action that already happened (removed, deleted, saved)A choice or a decision: a dialog
Celebrating a moment mid-flow (sparkles, every fifth streak note, a duet partner finishing)A result she should be able to read at leisure: inline on Results (.badge-toast, score card)
A short nudge about what to try next (Find My Voice, room code length)Ongoing guidance: Twinkle's bubble
A family-server call that failed while the screen keeps workingA screen or dialog that cannot work without the server: an empty or failed state in place
—Grown-up errors that need reading or acting on: an inline alert in the dialog, which stays

Structure

One host per app, fixed at the top centre above everything (--z-toast), holding a column of pills 8px apart, newest at the bottom. Each pill is one line of 16px black-weight Nunito with at most one emoji.

🐱 Bea ✨80 🔥4 🔥 5 in a row! SING TO MOVE YOUR STAR ↑↓
🔥 5 in a row! Bea finished — 28 notes! 🎉
Two gold toasts stacked during a duet, newest at the bottom. The host is a polite live region; pills ignore pointer events.

Responsive behaviour

  • The host is centred, 16px from the top (plus the safe-area inset), and at most 100vw − 40px wide.
  • A pill is as wide as its words. A long nudge wraps to a second line inside the pill and keeps its rounded ends; keep copy short enough that this is rare at 360px.
  • The position is the same on every screen and at every width, so she learns where good news appears.

Accessibility

Live region (D16)

The host carries role="status" and aria-live="polite" from page load, so each pill added to it is read once, after the current speech, without moving focus. The mocks' host is not a live region; the system adds it. Emoji are part of the text; a pill that is only an emoji is not allowed.

Timing (WCAG 2.2.1)

Toasts disappear on their own, so they may never be the only place important information lives and may never contain a control. Anything she must read or act on stays on screen: a dialog, an inline alert, or the Results page. Grown-up failures that need a next step get an inline .alert in the open dialog as well as, or instead of, the pink toast.

Not by colour alone

Gold, white and pink set the mood, but the words carry the meaning: +5 sparkles ✨ and Can't reach the family server 📡 make sense in greyscale.

Motion

Pop in with --ease-spring over --duration-slow; leave by fading up 16px. Under reduced motion they appear and fade without scale or travel.

Content

  • One short line; one emoji at most, at the end (or a leading 🔥 / 🏆 that names the kind of news).
  • White confirmations end with 💙 to keep even a removal warm: Removed 💙.
  • Pink nudges say what to try, not what went wrong: reach a little higher! 🐦, Codes have 4 letters!.
  • Failures name what could not be reached and stop there: Can't reach the family server 📡, Could not delete right now. Never Error, never the browser's message (D19).
