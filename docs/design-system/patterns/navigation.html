Pattern

Navigation and page structure

Four places to go, one obvious thing to do on each, and a back arrow that always goes somewhere predictable. A five-year-old should never wonder where she is or how to get back to singing.

Overview

TwinkleTune is a single centred column (470px at most) on every device. Navigation has three layers:

  • The bottom nav: a floating white pill with 🏠 Home, 🎵 Songs, 💡 Tips and ⭐ Me. It appears only on those four hub screens.
  • The top bar: a round ← back icon button, a centred title and a balancing spacer. Every screen below a hub has one; Sing replaces back with ⏸ Pause.
  • The one big action: each screen has a single obvious next step (a hero tile, an extra-large button, a play button per song), so most journeys are a chain of big taps rather than menu choices.

Grown-up areas open as dialogs from ⚙️ on Me, behind the parent gate, so they never appear in the child's navigation. Wide grown-up pages may use a sidebar nav and breadcrumb; neither is in the mocks yet.

The singing flow

  1. Home: tap the Sing a Song tile.
  2. Songs: tap ▶ on a song (or Sing it again!). The first time a shifted song is opened this visit, Tuned just for YOU! explains the shift.
  3. Sing, ready → countdown → singing. ⏸ opens Pause: Keep singing ▶, Start over 🔁, Pick another song 🎵.
  4. Results: Practice the tricky part 🎯 (gold, when offered), Sing again 🔁, New song 🎵, or Home 🏠.

Each step has exactly one primary action and at most two calm alternatives. The flow never dead-ends: every screen leads back to singing in one tap.

Back behaviour

  • ← goes to a fixed parent, not browser history: Songs, Profiles and Tuner → Home; Duet → Songs. A child who arrived from a deep link still lands somewhere familiar.
  • Sing has no back arrow. ⏸ pauses the music and opens the Pause dialog; leaving the song is a choice in that dialog. In a duet the choice is Leave the duet 👋, which closes the room for both singers.
  • Results has no top bar. Its buttons are the way on, and Home 🏠 is the quiet way out.
  • Until a profile exists, every route goes to Welcome; until a voice range exists, Home invites Find my voice but does not force it.

Responsive behaviour

  • The column is 470px at most at every width; on a tablet or laptop it simply sits in the middle of the sky. Nothing moves to a sidebar for children.
  • The bottom nav is min(440px, 100vw − 40px) wide and floats 16px above the bottom (plus the safe-area inset on phones with a home indicator). Hub screens keep 128px of bottom padding so content never hides beneath it.
  • Top bar titles are 32px Baloo 2; a title that would wrap at 320px is shortened in the copy, never truncated (the Sing title is the exception: long song names ellipsise beside the progress bar).
  • Grown-up pages wider than 1024px may add a sidebar nav; the child app never does.

Accessibility

Landmarks and headings

Each screen has a skip link to <main id="main">, one <h1> (the top-bar title, the greeting, or a visually hidden Singing Twinkle Twinkle Little Star on Sing) and a <nav aria-label="Main navigation"> on hubs. The current item carries aria-current="page" as well as the tinted pill, so it is not shown by colour alone.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabSkip link → back button → screen content in reading order → bottom nav items.
EnterFollows a nav item, the back link or a tile.
Space / Enter on ⏸Pauses and opens the Pause dialog; focus moves to Keep singing.

On a route change focus moves to the new screen's <h1> (programmatically focusable with tabindex="-1"), and the document title changes, so screen-reader users hear where they arrived (WCAG 2.4.2, 2.4.3).

Labelling and touch

Icon-only controls are named: Go back, Pause, Switch singer, More tips, Grown-ups corner. Nav items show their word under the emoji. Back, pause and nav targets are 52px or more; the tip-of-the-day arrow grows to 44px (D23).

Motion

Screens enter with a short rise and an 80ms stagger per block (.rise .d1–.d8); route changes fade out and in over --duration-base. Reduced motion removes both.

Content

  • Nav labels are one word: Home, Songs, Tips, Me. They never change with state.
  • Screen titles are what she is doing, with one emoji: Pick a Song 🎵, Who's singing? 🎤, Duet Time 🎤🎤. Me is personal: Ada's Star Journey.
  • The big action is a happy verb phrase: Let's go! ▶, Let's Sing! 🎤, Join the duet! 🎤, Practice the tricky part 🎯.
  • The way out is named for where it goes: Home 🏠, Back to songs, Back to singing. Never just Back when a destination fits.
