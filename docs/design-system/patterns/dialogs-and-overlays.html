Pattern

Dialogs and overlays

A dialog stops the music and asks one simple question. TwinkleTune uses them for moments that need a choice (pause, the microphone, a voice found, a confirm) and for the grown-ups' tools behind the parent gate. One at a time, always centred, always with a safe way out.

Overview

Every dialog is the same dialog component: a blurred sky scrim (.overlay) and a white rounded card (.modal, 340px; .modal-wide, 440px for managers and editors) that pops in. The pattern is about which moments deserve one, what goes inside, and how they chain.

When to use a dialog

  • Use one when the screen behind must stop and wait: pausing a song, a permission she must answer, a destructive action, or a moment worth celebrating that ends in a choice (To my songs 🎵 or Home).
  • Use one for grown-up tools, so they never become part of the child's navigation; they open from ⚙️ on Me through the gate.
  • Don't use one to confirm something happened: that is a toast (Removed 💙).
  • Don't use one for something she can undo or that costs nothing: Start over during a song needs no confirm.
  • Don't use one for errors. A failed server call becomes a quiet line inside the dialog or a pink toast; see empty and error states.

Structure

Inside, top to bottom: optional big emoji (.modal-emoji: 🫧 pause, 🔒 gate, 🫣 confirm, 🎯 tuned) → h3 title, which names the dialog → one or two sentences → full-width buttons, primary first → a quiet link for the safe way out.

Chaining and stacking

  • One dialog at a time. Opening a manager from the Grown-Ups Corner closes the corner behind it; a confirm opened from a manager replaces the manager and returns to it on Keep it or after Yes, delete.
  • Toasts float above dialogs (--z-toast over --z-overlay), so Every singer needs a name! 💙 can appear over the singer editor without closing it.
  • The page behind is inert (inert on the screen, as in the mocks), so neither pointer nor Tab reaches it.
  • Gate first. The parent gate stands in front of the Grown-Ups Corner and of removing a singer; it opens as its own dialog and, when answered, is replaced by the dialog it guarded.

Responsive behaviour

  • Dialogs are centred cards at every width (not bottom sheets): min(340px, 100%), or min(440px, 100%) wide, with 20px of scrim around them on the smallest phones.
  • Tall dialogs (managers, the song editor, the Grown-Ups Corner on a short phone) scroll inside at 84% of the viewport height; the title scrolls with the content and the quiet link is always reachable at the end.
  • Buttons fill the card width and stack; they never sit side by side except the editor's Preview + Create pair.
  • Setting rows wrap their buttons under the label when the card is narrower than its contents.

Accessibility

Contract (D15)

role="dialog" with aria-modal="true", named by its h3 through aria-labelledby (the mocks use only aria-label). Focus moves into the dialog on open (to the primary button, or the first field in an editor), is trapped inside while open, and returns to the control that opened it on close. See the APG modal dialog pattern.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabCycles through the dialog's controls only.
Enter / SpaceActivates the focused button or answer key.
EscTakes the safe way out (see the table above); never a destructive choice.

Parent gate

The gate is a speed bump for children, not security, and must not block grown-ups who use assistive technology: the question is real text (aria-describedby), the three answers are ordinary buttons with their numbers as names, and a wrong answer is announced by nothing more than the wiggle, so it is safe to try again. Under reduced motion the wiggle is replaced by a brief outline on the key.

Motion and touch

The scrim fades over --duration-base and the card pops in with --ease-spring over --duration-slow; reduced motion makes both instant. Dialog buttons are 56px tall; gate keys 64px with 8px between them; manager row buttons 44px.

Content

  • Child dialogs ask or celebrate in Twinkle's voice: Taking a breather?, Can Twinkle hear you sing?, Your voice is found! 🎉, Tuned just for YOU!. The body reassures: Your song is waiting right where you left it.
  • Confirms are questions naming the thing: Delete “Hot Cross Buns”?, Remove Bea?, Really start over?. The body says exactly what goes: This erases the profile, sparkles, badges and voice range on this device.
  • Confirm buttons repeat the verb: Yes, delete, Yes, remove, Yes, erase everything. The safe link keeps: Keep it, Keep them, Keep my stuff. Never OK / Cancel.
  • Privacy is stated where data is managed: the Grown-Ups Corner says what stays on the device and what lives on the home server.
