# Duet Multiplayer — L2 Detailed Requirements

**Subsystem code:** DM
**Specification level:** L2 (detailed / verifiable)
**Baseline:** v1.0 · **Status:** Approved

## 1. Purpose & reading guide

Verifiable requirements for family duets. Sources:
`backend/src/TwinkleTune.Application/Duet/RoomService.cs` (with `RoomServiceTests`),
`backend/src/TwinkleTune.Api/Hubs/DuetHub.cs` (with `DuetFlowTests`),
`frontend/src/api/duet.ts`, `frontend/src/screens/duet.ts`, and duet paths in
`frontend/src/screens/sing.ts` and `results.ts`.

## 2. Detailed requirements

### DM-L2-1 — Room creation with unambiguous code
- **Traces to:** DM-L1-1, DM-L1-2 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `RoomService.Create`; `NewCode` (alphabet `ABCDEFGHJKMNPQRSTUVWXYZ`, 4 letters); `DuetHub.CreateRoom`
- **Requirement:** Creating a room shall generate a unique 4‑letter code drawn from an alphabet that
  excludes the ambiguous letters I, L, and O, add the creator as the first player, and return the room
  state.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a singer creates a room
> **WHEN** the room is created
> **THEN** a 4‑letter code containing no I/L/O is returned and the creator is its sole player.

---

### DM-L2-2 — Joining a room and join validation
- **Traces to:** DM-L1-2 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `RoomService.Join` (trim/upper; not‑found and full errors); `duet.ts` (client 4‑letter guard; error mapping)
- **Requirement:** A second singer shall join by code (trimmed and upper‑cased); an unknown code shall
  be rejected ("that room code wasn't found"), and a full room shall be rejected ("already has two
  singers"). The client shall require a 4‑character code before attempting to join.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a valid open room code entered in lower case with spaces
> **WHEN** a singer joins
> **THEN** the code is normalised and the join succeeds, and the other player is notified.

> **GIVEN** an unknown code
> **THEN** the join is rejected with a "check the letters" message.

> **GIVEN** a code fewer than 4 characters
> **THEN** the client shows "Codes have 4 letters!" and does not attempt to join.

---

### DM-L2-3 — Two‑player cap
- **Traces to:** DM-L1-2 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `RoomService` (`MaxPlayers = 2`); `RoomServiceTests`
- **Requirement:** A room shall hold at most two players; a third join shall be rejected.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a room already holding two singers
> **WHEN** a third attempts to join
> **THEN** the join is rejected with "already has two singers".

---

### DM-L2-4 — Synchronised start requires two players
- **Traces to:** DM-L1-1, DM-L1-4 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `RoomService.StartSong` (needs 2 players; `StartAtUtc = now + 3s`; resets summaries); `DuetHub.StartSong` → `SongStarted(songId, startAtUtc)`
- **Requirement:** Starting a song shall require two players; on start the server shall set a start time
  ≈3 seconds in the future, clear any prior summaries, and broadcast `SongStarted` with the song id and
  start time to both clients. A start attempted with fewer than two players shall be refused.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a room with two players
> **WHEN** one starts a song
> **THEN** both clients receive `SongStarted` with the song id and a start time ~3 s ahead.

> **GIVEN** a room with one player
> **WHEN** a start is attempted
> **THEN** it is refused with "You need two singers in the room to start!".

---

### DM-L2-5 — Clock‑skew absorption
- **Traces to:** DM-L1-4 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** ADR‑0001/0002 (server `startAtUtc` + 4‑beat count‑in; per‑phrase comparison); `player.ts` count‑in
- **Requirement:** The synchronised start shall combine the server‑issued future start time with the
  gameplay count‑in so that small clock differences between devices do not desynchronise the shared
  start; result comparison shall be per‑phrase rather than instantaneous.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** two devices with a small clock offset receive `SongStarted`
> **WHEN** each begins the duet
> **THEN** the future start time plus the count‑in keeps the shared start aligned within the count‑in window, and results are compared per phrase.

---

### DM-L2-6 — Score‑tick relay
- **Traces to:** DM-L1-3, DM-L1-5 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** `DuetHub.ScoreTick` → `OpponentTick` to others; `duet.ts`/`sing.ts` (`sendTick`, throttle one per finalised note)
- **Requirement:** A client shall emit at most one score tick per finalised note carrying
  `{ landed, streak, sparkles, noteIdx }`; the hub shall relay each tick to the other player only; tick
  send failures shall be swallowed.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** two singers in an active duet
> **WHEN** one finalises a note and emits a tick
> **THEN** only the other singer receives an `OpponentTick` with the current landed/streak/sparkles/index.

---

### DM-L2-7 — Finish relay and combined result
- **Traces to:** DM-L1-5, DM-L1-6 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `DuetHub.FinishSong` → `OpponentFinished`; `DuetResult { Players, CombinedSparkles = sum }` when both done; `DuetFlowTests`
- **Requirement:** When a singer finishes, the hub shall notify the other; when both have finished, the
  hub shall emit a combined `DuetResult` to the room containing both players' summaries and the sum of
  their sparkles.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** both singers finish the song
> **WHEN** the second finish is processed
> **THEN** both clients receive a `DuetResult` whose combined sparkles equal the sum of the two summaries' sparkles.

---

### DM-L2-8 — Presence: join, leave, disconnect, closure
- **Traces to:** DM-L1-8 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** `DuetHub` (`PlayerJoined`, `OnDisconnectedAsync`/`LeaveRoom` → `PlayerLeft` or room closed); `RoomService.Leave` (removes player; closes room when empty)
- **Requirement:** The system shall announce a player joining, announce a player leaving (whether by
  explicit leave or disconnect), and close a room once it is empty.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a two‑player room
> **WHEN** one player disconnects
> **THEN** the other is notified the player left, and the room persists for the remaining player.

> **GIVEN** the last player leaves
> **THEN** the room is closed and removed.

---

### DM-L2-9 — Idle room expiry
- **Traces to:** DM-L1-8 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** `RoomService` (`IdleExpiry = 30 min`; `CleanupExpired`)
- **Requirement:** Rooms idle for longer than 30 minutes shall be removed, releasing their players'
  connection mappings.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a room with no activity for over 30 minutes
> **WHEN** a subsequent create/join triggers cleanup
> **THEN** the stale room is removed.

---

### DM-L2-10 — In‑memory, non‑persistent rooms
- **Traces to:** DM-L1-8 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `RoomService` (`ConcurrentDictionary`, not persisted); ADR‑0002
- **Requirement:** Room state shall be held only in memory and shall not survive a server restart.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** active rooms
> **WHEN** the server restarts
> **THEN** no rooms remain (a duet that outlives a restart is not a supported scenario).

---

### DM-L2-11 — SignalR client wrapper and session lifecycle
- **Traces to:** DM-L1-7, DM-L1-8 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** `duet.ts` (`DuetClient` with `withAutomaticReconnect`; `duetSession` get/set/end; `leave` invokes `LeaveRoom` then stops)
- **Requirement:** The client shall wrap the hub connection with automatic reconnection, expose typed
  create/join/start/tick/finish and event subscriptions, and maintain a duet session across the
  lobby → sing → results navigation, ending it (leaving the room and stopping the connection) when done.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a live duet session
> **WHEN** the duet ends or the user leaves
> **THEN** the client leaves the room and stops the connection, and the session is cleared.

---

### DM-L2-12 — Lobby flow
- **Traces to:** DM-L1-1 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** `duet.ts` (choice → create/join; room‑code display per character; waiting placeholder; song select with preferred pre‑selected; start)
- **Requirement:** The lobby shall let a singer make a room or join with a code, display the room code
  in large per‑character form, show both players (with a waiting placeholder when alone), and — once
  both are present — offer a song picker (pre‑selecting the song passed in) and a start action.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a singer creates a room
> **WHEN** the lobby renders
> **THEN** the 4‑letter code is shown character by character and a "waiting" placeholder stands in for the absent second singer.

> **GIVEN** both singers are present and a song was pre‑selected via the entry link
> **THEN** that song is pre‑selected in the picker and a start action is available.

---

### DM-L2-13 — Duet gating and graceful fallback
- **Traces to:** DM-L1-7 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `duet.ts` (no `singerId` → "Pick my profile"; `!online` → "Sing solo instead")
- **Requirement:** Duets shall require a server‑linked profile and a reachable server; a local‑only
  profile shall be routed to profile setup, and an unreachable server shall be routed to solo play,
  each with a friendly message rather than an error.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a local‑only profile (no singer id)
> **WHEN** the duet screen opens
> **THEN** it explains duets need a family profile and offers to pick one.

> **GIVEN** the server is unreachable
> **WHEN** the duet screen opens
> **THEN** it offers to sing solo instead.

---

### DM-L2-14 — In‑song opponent overlay
- **Traces to:** DM-L1-5 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** `sing.ts` (opponent chip; `onOpponentTick` shows sparkles and streak when ≥3; `onOpponentFinished` toast)
- **Requirement:** During a duet, the singing screen shall show the opponent's avatar and name with a
  live sparkle count (and a streak indicator when the streak reaches 3), and shall announce when the
  opponent finishes.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** an active duet
> **WHEN** an opponent tick with streak 4 and 60 sparkles arrives
> **THEN** the opponent chip shows 60 sparkles and a streak indicator.

> **WHEN** the opponent finishes
> **THEN** a celebratory toast announces it.

---

### DM-L2-15 — Cooperative combined‑result panel
- **Traces to:** DM-L1-6 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `results.ts` (`paintDuet`: local singer first; heading "Together you earned <combined> ✨"; per‑player stars and notes; waiting card until result arrives)
- **Requirement:** The duet results panel shall lead with the combined family sparkle total, show each
  player's stars and notes side by side (local singer first), and show a friendly "waiting" state until
  the combined result arrives; win/lose shall not be emphasised.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a completed duet with a combined total of 260 sparkles
> **WHEN** the results panel paints
> **THEN** the headline celebrates "Together you earned 260 ✨", both players are shown, and no winner/loser is declared.

> **GIVEN** the local singer finished but the opponent has not
> **THEN** a "waiting for your duet buddy" state is shown until the combined result arrives.

---

### DM-L2-16 — Duet error handling
- **Traces to:** DM-L1-7 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** `duet.ts` (toasts: can't reach server; join errors; "Need both singers in the room!")
- **Requirement:** Transient failures (connection, join, start) shall surface as friendly, non‑blocking
  messages while keeping the singer on the duet screen.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a start attempted before both singers are present
> **THEN** a "Need both singers in the room!" message is shown and the lobby remains.

---

### DM-L2-17 — Network minimisation and privacy
- **Traces to:** DM-L1-3 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `DuetHub` (only score/summary DTOs relayed); ADR‑0001 (no audio; scores not stored)
- **Requirement:** Only score ticks and finish summaries (small JSON) shall cross the network during a
  duet; no audio shall be transmitted, and duet score events shall not be persisted.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** an active duet
> **WHEN** network traffic is inspected
> **THEN** only small score/summary messages are exchanged — no audio — and nothing about the live duet is written to storage.
