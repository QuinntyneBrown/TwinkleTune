# Singing Gameplay — L2 Detailed Requirements

**Subsystem code:** SG
**Specification level:** L2 (detailed / verifiable)
**Baseline:** v1.0 · **Status:** Approved
**Traces up to:** [L1.md](./L1.md)

---

## 1. Purpose & reading guide

Verifiable requirements for the live singing screen. Constants are the shipped baseline. Primary
source: `frontend/src/screens/sing.ts`, with scoring primitives from `frontend/src/state/scoring.ts`
and playback from `frontend/src/audio/player.ts`.

---

## 2. Detailed requirements

### SG-L2-1 — Entry guards and window selection
- **Traces to:** SG-L1-1 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `sing.ts` (song lookup; `practicePhrase`; `windowNotes` = notes in `[fromBeat, untilBeat)`)
- **Requirement:** The screen shall resolve the requested song and redirect to the song list if it is
  missing; in practice mode it shall bound play to the requested phrase; the set of pills and scored
  notes shall be exactly the notes within the active window.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a `#/sing?song=<id>` request whose song does not exist
> **WHEN** the screen mounts
> **THEN** it redirects to `#/songs`.

> **GIVEN** a `#/sing?song=<id>&practice=2&slow=1` request
> **WHEN** the screen mounts
> **THEN** only phrase 2's notes are rendered and scored, and the header shows a practice title.

---

### SG-L2-2 — Note‑pill layout
- **Traces to:** SG-L1-1 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `sing.ts` (`PX_PER_BEAT = 90`; pill `left = start·90`, `width = max(34, dur·90 − 8)`, `top = yFor(midi) − 17`)
- **Requirement:** Each in‑window note shall render as a pill positioned horizontally by its start beat
  (90 px/beat) and sized by its duration, positioned vertically by its pitch, and labelled with its
  syllable.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a note starting at beat 4 with duration 2 beats
> **WHEN** its pill renders
> **THEN** its left edge is at 360 px, its width is ≈172 px, and it shows the note's syllable.

---

### SG-L2-3 — Beat‑synchronised scrolling with "now" marker
- **Traces to:** SG-L1-1 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `sing.ts` (loop: `track.transform = translateX(markerX − beat·90)`; `markerX = stageWidth·0.21`)
- **Requirement:** Each frame the note track shall scroll so that the pill for the current beat aligns
  with the "now" marker at 21% of the stage width.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** the song is playing at beat *b*
> **WHEN** the frame renders
> **THEN** the track is translated so the note at beat *b* sits under the "now" marker, and pills flow toward it as the song progresses.

---

### SG-L2-4 — Count‑in overlay and "Sing!" cue
- **Traces to:** SG-L1-1 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `sing.ts` (countdown = `ceil(fromBeat − beat)` while `beat < fromBeat`; "Sing! 🎤" for the first 0.5 beat)
- **Requirement:** During the count‑in (negative/pre‑start beats) the screen shall show a descending
  numeric countdown; at the segment start it shall briefly show a "Sing!" cue, then clear the overlay.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a 4‑beat count‑in
> **WHEN** the count‑in is 2.4 beats from the start
> **THEN** the overlay shows "3", counting down each beat.

> **WHEN** the segment reaches its start beat
> **THEN** a "Sing! 🎤" cue shows briefly and then the overlay hides.

---

### SG-L2-5 — Progress indicator
- **Traces to:** SG-L1-1 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** `sing.ts` (progress width = `clamp((beat − fromBeat)/(untilBeat − fromBeat), 0, 1)·100%`)
- **Requirement:** The screen shall show a progress bar that fills from 0% to 100% across the active
  window as the song plays.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a whole‑song play
> **WHEN** the song is halfway through in beats
> **THEN** the progress bar is ≈50% filled.

---

### SG-L2-6 — Pitch‑driven avatar with octave folding
- **Traces to:** SG-L1-2 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `sing.ts` (`yFor`; `sungBase = frame.midi − shift`; fold toward `midiCenter`; ease `+= (target − y)·0.3`; quiet drift `·0.02`)
- **Requirement:** When a pitch frame is present, the star shall move toward the vertical position of
  the sung pitch (after removing the song's shift and folding to the octave nearest the song's centre),
  eased for smoothness; when no pitch is present, the star shall drift gently toward centre and the
  stage shall show a "quiet" state.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** the song is transposed by shift *s* and the child sings the target note in a different octave
> **WHEN** the frame is processed
> **THEN** the star moves to the target note's vertical position (octave‑folded), not to a wrong extreme.

> **GIVEN** silence (no frame)
> **THEN** the star drifts slowly toward the vertical centre and the stage indicates quiet.

---

### SG-L2-7 — Karaoke syllable highlighting
- **Traces to:** SG-L1-3 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `sing.ts` (`updateKaraoke`: `word-now` while inside a note, `word-done` after; prev/next lyric lines; 1‑beat lead‑in, 0.5‑beat tail)
- **Requirement:** The "now" lyric line shall mark each syllable as upcoming, active (while the beat is
  inside its note), or done (after its note ends); the previous and next phrase lyrics shall be shown
  as context.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** the song is mid‑phrase
> **WHEN** the beat is inside a syllable's note
> **THEN** that syllable is styled active, already‑sung syllables are styled done, and the previous/next lines are shown.

---

### SG-L2-8 — Phrase‑change coaching cues
- **Traces to:** SG-L1-3, SG-L1-5 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** `sing.ts` (`PHRASE_CHEERS[pi % 5]` on phrase change)
- **Requirement:** On advancing to a new phrase, the coach bubble shall show an encouraging cue drawn
  from a fixed rotation.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** the song crosses from one phrase to the next
> **WHEN** the new phrase becomes active
> **THEN** the coach bubble updates to an encouraging line (e.g. "Big breath — here comes the next part!").

### SG-L2-9 — Live per‑note frame accumulation
- **Traces to:** SG-L1-4 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `sing.ts` (active note = first `windowNotes[i≥finalizedUpTo]` with `scoreBeat ∈ [start, start+dur)`; increments `totalFrames`; on‑pitch when `|foldCents(frame.midi, note.midi + shift)| ≤ 50`; records `firstHitFrac`)
- **Requirement:** For the currently active note (outside no‑mic mode), each frame shall increment the
  observed‑frame count; when the sung pitch is within ±50 cents of the note (octave‑folded, shift
  applied) the on‑pitch‑frame count shall increment and, on the first such frame, the fractional
  position within the note shall be recorded.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** the active note is MIDI 67 and the song shift is +2
> **WHEN** the child sings within ±50 cents of MIDI 69 (folded)
> **THEN** the note's on‑pitch frame count increases and the star shows an on‑note state.

> **GIVEN** the first on‑pitch frame occurs 30% into the note
> **THEN** the note's first‑hit fraction is recorded as ≈0.30.

---

### SG-L2-10 — Note finalisation and reward events
- **Traces to:** SG-L1-4, SG-L1-5 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `sing.ts` (`finalizeNotesUpTo`; `noteLanded`; landed → `landedCount++`, streak +1, `.hit`, cheer pop, sparkles = `landedCount·10`; missed → streak reset)
- **Requirement:** As each note's end passes, the system shall finalise it: if it is landed
  (SR criteria), increment the landed count and streak, mark the pill, show a random cheer, and update
  the live sparkle count to `landedCount·10`; if it is missed, reset the streak to 0. No missed note
  shall produce a negative message.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a note that was on‑pitch for at least half of ≥3 observed frames
> **WHEN** its end passes
> **THEN** it is marked landed, the streak increments, a cheer appears, and the sparkle count rises by 10.

> **GIVEN** a note that was not landed
> **WHEN** its end passes
> **THEN** the streak resets to 0 and no failure message is shown.

---

### SG-L2-11 — Streak indicator and milestones
- **Traces to:** SG-L1-5 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** `sing.ts` (`setStreak`: chip shown only at streak ≥ 3; toast at each multiple of 5)
- **Requirement:** The streak chip shall appear only once the current streak reaches 3, and a
  celebratory toast shall fire at every multiple of 5.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** the current streak is 2
> **THEN** the streak chip is hidden.

> **GIVEN** the streak reaches 5 (then 10, 15, …)
> **THEN** a "🔥 N in a row!" toast fires at each such milestone.

---

### SG-L2-12 — Live sparkle count
- **Traces to:** SG-L1-5 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** `sing.ts` (sparkles pill = `landedCount·10`)
- **Requirement:** The on‑screen sparkle count shall equal ten times the number of landed notes so far,
  updating as notes finalise.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** 7 notes have been landed
> **WHEN** the sparkle pill renders
> **THEN** it shows 70.

---

### SG-L2-13 — "Tuned just for you" reveal (once per song)
- **Traces to:** SG-L1-5 · **Priority:** Could
- **Source:** `sing.ts` (`maybeTunedDialogThenStart`; `tunedDialogShown` set; only when non‑duet, range set, shift ≠ 0)
- **Requirement:** The first time a personalized (non‑zero shift) song is started in a session — outside
  duet mode — the system shall show a one‑time, dismissible "Tuned just for YOU!" message explaining the
  shift in friendly terms before starting.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a stored range, a song whose shift is −3, and this song not yet shown this session
> **WHEN** the child taps start
> **THEN** a message says Twinkle moved the song 3 notes lower so it fits her voice, and continuing starts the song; the message does not reappear for that song in the same session.

---

### SG-L2-14 — No‑microphone "just for fun" mode
- **Traces to:** SG-L1-6 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `sing.ts` (`noMic` path: no pitch read, no frame accumulation, star drifts to centre, sparkles stay 0; `summarize(..., noMic=true)`)
- **Requirement:** In no‑mic mode the song shall play fully with pills and karaoke, but no pitch shall be
  read, no scoring frames accumulated, the star shall drift to centre, and the live sparkle count shall
  remain 0; the resulting summary shall be flagged as a fun (non‑scored) run.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** no‑mic mode is active
> **WHEN** the song plays to the end
> **THEN** the song completes, the sparkle count stays 0, no notes are scored, and the summary is flagged no‑mic.

---

### SG-L2-15 — Count‑in defaults for start, resume, and practice
- **Traces to:** SG-L1-7 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** `sing.ts` (`begin(from, countIn)`: 4 on fresh start/restart, 2 on resume/practice repeat)
- **Requirement:** A fresh start or restart shall use a 4‑beat count‑in; resuming from pause and a
  practice repeat shall use a 2‑beat count‑in.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a fresh start
> **THEN** the count‑in is 4 beats.

> **GIVEN** a resume from pause
> **THEN** the count‑in is 2 beats.

---

### SG-L2-16 — Finish, record, and navigate
- **Traces to:** SG-L1-7 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `sing.ts` (`finish`: finalise remaining, `summarize`, `recordPlay(store, song, summary, todayISO())`, navigate to `#/results` after ≈600 ms)
- **Requirement:** When a non‑practice performance ends, the system shall finalise any remaining notes,
  summarise the performance, record the play (rewards/progress), and navigate to the results screen
  after a brief celebratory pause.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a normal performance reaches its end
> **WHEN** playback completes
> **THEN** a summary is computed, the play is recorded, and the results screen opens shortly after.

---

### SG-L2-17 — Single‑phrase practice loop
- **Traces to:** SG-L1-7 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** `sing.ts` (practice mode: rate 0.7, phrase bounds, loops with a 1200 ms gap and 2‑beat count‑in; "Back to my score" exit)
- **Requirement:** In practice mode the system shall loop the chosen phrase at 0.7× tempo indefinitely
  (with an encouraging "one more time" between repeats), never recording a score, until the child
  chooses to leave; leaving via the pause menu shall return to the score.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a practice session on phrase 2
> **WHEN** the phrase finishes
> **THEN** it restarts at 0.7× tempo after a short encouraging pause, without recording a result.

> **GIVEN** a practice session
> **WHEN** the child chooses "Back to my score"
> **THEN** play ends and the results screen opens.

---

### SG-L2-18 — Pause, resume, restart, and exit
- **Traces to:** SG-L1-7 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** `sing.ts` (`pauseDialog`: Keep singing / Start over / Back to my score / Pick another song / Leave the duet)
- **Requirement:** During play the child shall be able to pause, then resume from where she left off
  (2‑beat count‑in), start the song over (resetting all captured note data, streak, and sparkles), or
  exit; a paused song shall not advance.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a paused song
> **WHEN** "Keep singing" is chosen
> **THEN** play resumes from the paused position with a 2‑beat count‑in.

> **GIVEN** a paused song
> **WHEN** "Start over" is chosen
> **THEN** all captured note data, the landed count, the streak, and the sparkle count reset, and the song restarts from the beginning.

### SG-L2-19 — Microphone‑latency compensation
- **Traces to:** SG-L1-8 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** `sing.ts` (`latencyBeats = (profile.latencyMs/1000)/secondsPerBeat`; `scoreBeat = beat − latencyBeats`)
- **Requirement:** Scoring shall use a latency‑compensated beat position derived from the profile's mic
  latency offset, while visuals use the raw beat, so that a device with input lag still scores the note
  the child is actually singing.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a profile latency offset of +120 ms and a song at some tempo
> **WHEN** frames are scored
> **THEN** the active‑note selection uses a beat position shifted earlier by 120 ms worth of beats, while pills scroll on the raw beat.

---

### SG-L2-20 — Microphone‑permission handling at start
- **Traces to:** SG-L1-9 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `sing.ts` (`startFlow` → `micDialog`: "Yes, let's sing!" retry / "Sing just for fun" no‑mic / "Back to songs")
- **Requirement:** If starting the microphone fails, the system shall present a non‑dismissible choice
  to retry, to play without scoring, or to return to the song list.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** the microphone is denied when the child taps start
> **WHEN** the failure is detected
> **THEN** a dialog offers retry, a "just for fun" no‑mic option, and a way back to songs — and the child is never left on a blank or stuck screen.

---

### SG-L2-21 — Cleanup on leaving gameplay
- **Traces to:** SG-L1-9 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `sing.ts` (cleanup: cancel RAF, `player.stop()`, `tracker.stop()`, close any paused modal)
- **Requirement:** On navigating away from the singing screen, the system shall cancel the render loop,
  stop playback, release the microphone, and dismiss any open dialog.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** an active performance
> **WHEN** the child navigates away
> **THEN** the render loop is cancelled, audio stops, the microphone is released, and no dialog remains.

---

### SG-L2-22 — Duet score‑tick emission
- **Traces to:** SG-L1-4 · **Priority:** Should · **See also:** DM
- **Source:** `sing.ts` (`duet?.client.sendTick({ landed, streak, sparkles, noteIdx })` once per finalised note)
- **Requirement:** In duet mode, the system shall emit a score tick once per finalised note (not per
  frame), carrying the landed count, streak, sparkles, and note index; tick delivery failures shall be
  swallowed so play is never interrupted. (Relay semantics: DM.)

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a duet performance
> **WHEN** a note finalises
> **THEN** exactly one score tick is emitted for that note with the current landed/streak/sparkles/index, and a delivery error does not disrupt play.
