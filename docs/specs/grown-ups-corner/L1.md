# Grown-Ups Corner — L1 High-Level Requirements

**Subsystem code:** GC
**Specification level:** L1 (capability / high‑level)
**Baseline:** v1.0 · **Status:** Approved
**Detailed requirements:** [L2.md](./L2.md)
**Primary dependencies:** Song Library (SL), Player Profiles (PP), Audio Engine (AE, preview), Singing Gameplay (SG, latency), Family Server Platform (FS)

---

## 1. Purpose

The Grown‑Ups Corner is where a parent or guardian tunes the app, curates its content, manages who
sings, and understands exactly what data the product handles. It sits behind a **parent gate** — a
simple challenge a young child is unlikely to pass — and gathers device calibration (microphone
latency), content management (a song editor and avatar manager), profile management, a full reset, and
an honest privacy disclosure. Because the family server has no authentication by design, this
client‑side gate is the product's only guard, and its scope and limits are stated plainly.

## 2. Scope

### In scope

- The parent gate challenge and the actions it guards.
- The grown‑ups settings hub and its layout.
- Microphone‑latency calibration (a device‑local property).
- The song manager: create/edit/delete songs with live preview and validation.
- The avatar manager: create/edit/delete avatars.
- A full "start over" reset with confirmation.
- Honest disclosure of the product's data and privacy posture to the grown‑up.

### Out of scope

- Song invariants and CRUD semantics — **SL**; avatar/singer CRUD semantics — **PP**.
- Melody preview synthesis — **AE**; latency's effect on scoring — **SG**.
- The server's security posture (no‑auth, LAN‑only) — **FS** (GC states it to the grown‑up).

## 3. Context & dependencies

The parent gate is invoked before opening the settings hub (from the welcome and progress screens) and
before destructive actions such as deleting a singer. The hub reads and writes the active profile's mic
latency, launches the song and avatar managers (which call the server), links to profile and voice
re‑setup, and offers a device reset. Its intro copy discloses that on‑device rewards stay on the device
while profiles, songs, and family scores live on the home server, and that only live scores are shared
during duets.

## 4. High-level requirements

| ID | Requirement | Priority | Rationale |
|----|-------------|----------|-----------|
| **GC-L1-1** | The system shall guard grown‑up‑only functions behind a challenge that a young child is unlikely to pass, and shall not reveal the guarded area on a wrong answer. | Must | Keeps settings, content editing, and destructive actions out of a child's reach; the only client guard given the no‑auth server. |
| **GC-L1-2** | The system shall provide a grown‑ups settings hub gathering device tuning, content management, profile management, and reset. | Should | A single, discoverable place for everything a parent needs. |
| **GC-L1-3** | The system shall let a grown‑up calibrate the microphone latency offset, treating it as a device property that stays on the device. | Should | Corrects input lag on budget tablets so feedback lands in time (SG); latency belongs to the device, not the person. |
| **GC-L1-4** | The system shall let a grown‑up create, edit, and delete songs, with a live preview and the same validation the server enforces. | Should | Extending the songbook is a headline grown‑up feature; preview and validation prevent unsingable songs. |
| **GC-L1-5** | The system shall let a grown‑up create, edit, and delete avatars. | Could | Curating the family's identity set. |
| **GC-L1-6** | The system shall provide a full "start over" reset, protected by explicit confirmation, that erases the device profile and progress. | Should | A clean slate for handing the device on or starting fresh, with a guard against accidental loss. |
| **GC-L1-7** | The system shall disclose, in plain language, what data is stored where and what crosses the network, so a grown‑up can make an informed choice. | Must | Honesty about children's data is both an ethical obligation and part of the product's trust story (P3, P4). |

## 5. Assumptions & constraints

- The parent gate is a single‑digit arithmetic question with one correct and two incorrect options; it
  is child‑resistant, not adversary‑resistant, and is the only client‑side guard.
- Mic latency ranges −300…+300 ms in 10 ms steps and is stored per device in the profile.
- Song and avatar management require the family server; validation mirrors SL/PP.
- Reset clears the active profile's on‑device state and returns to onboarding.
