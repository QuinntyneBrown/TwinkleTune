## 2. Detailed requirements

### GC-L2-1 — Parent gate challenge
- **Traces to:** GC-L1-1 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `modal.ts` (`parentGate`: `a,b ∈ [2,8]`; options `[answer, answer−1, answer+2]` shuffled; wrong answer shakes for 700 ms and keeps the gate open; "Back to singing" cancels)
- **Requirement:** The parent gate shall pose a single‑digit addition question with three answer
  options (one correct, two incorrect); a correct choice shall proceed to the guarded action, a wrong
  choice shall give gentle feedback and keep the gate closed, and a cancel option shall return to play.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** the parent gate for "a + b"
> **WHEN** the correct sum is chosen
> **THEN** the gate closes and the guarded action proceeds.

> **GIVEN** the parent gate
> **WHEN** an incorrect option is chosen
> **THEN** the option shakes, the gate stays open, and the guarded action does not proceed.

### GC-L2-2 — Gate placement on guarded actions
- **Traces to:** GC-L1-1 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `welcome.ts` & `me.ts` (`parentGate(showSettings)`); `profiles.ts` (delete behind `parentGate`)
- **Requirement:** Opening the settings hub and deleting a singer shall each require passing the parent
  gate first.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** the child taps the grown‑ups entry (link or gear)
> **WHEN** the gate is not passed
> **THEN** the settings hub does not open.

> **GIVEN** a singer deletion is requested
> **WHEN** the gate is not passed
> **THEN** deletion does not proceed.

### GC-L2-3 — Settings hub layout
- **Traces to:** GC-L1-2, GC-L1-9 · **Priority:** Should
- **Revised:** v1.8 (lists the pitch strictness choice, GC-L2-11); v1.9 (the microphone picker joins the hub)
- **Source:** `settings.ts` (`showSettings`: latency, microphone, pitch strictness, voice re‑setup, song/avatar managers, singers/profiles, danger zone)
- **Requirement:** The settings hub shall present, in one place: the microphone timing offset, the
  microphone picker (GC-L2-12), the pitch strictness choice, a voice re‑setup shortcut, the song and avatar managers, a
  profiles‑management shortcut, and a danger‑zone reset, alongside the privacy disclosure.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** the gate is passed
> **WHEN** the settings hub opens
> **THEN** controls for latency, the microphone, pitch strictness, voice re‑setup, songs, avatars, profiles, and reset are all present.

### GC-L2-4 — Microphone‑latency calibration
- **Traces to:** GC-L1-3 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** `settings.ts` (range input −300…300, step 10; live `store.update` of `profile.latencyMs`; label shows " ms")
- **Requirement:** The hub shall provide a −300…+300 ms slider (10 ms steps) that updates the profile's
  mic latency live, with a numeric read‑out; the value shall stay device‑local (not synced as a person
  property) and shall feed scoring compensation, where it is added to the automatic pipeline latency
  estimate (SG-L2-19) as a manual correction.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** the latency slider at 0 ms
> **WHEN** it is moved to +120 ms
> **THEN** the read‑out shows "120 ms" and the profile's latency is updated immediately and persisted.

> **WHEN** "Re‑do" is chosen

### GC-L2-6 — Song manager and editor
- **Traces to:** GC-L1-4 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** `managers.ts` (`showSongManager`, `songEditor`: title/emoji/bpm/difficulty/art fields + phrases JSON; `Preview ▶` plays with a 1‑beat count‑in; validation via `validateSong`; save via `api.songs.create/update`; seed songs labelled "built‑in")
- **Requirement:** The song manager shall list the songbook (marking built‑in songs), allow creating a
  new song or editing an existing one via a structured form plus a phrases/notes JSON field, offer a
  live preview, validate before saving (identically to the server), and persist via the API.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** the song editor with a valid song
> **WHEN** "Preview" is tapped
> **THEN** the song plays with a short count‑in.

> **GIVEN** the song editor with an invalid phrases JSON or an invariant violation
> **WHEN** "Save" is tapped
> **THEN** the specific validation messages are shown and nothing is saved.

> **GIVEN** a valid new song
> **WHEN** it is saved
> **THEN** it is created on the server and the manager list refreshes to include it.

### GC-L2-7 — Song deletion with context‑aware confirmation
- **Traces to:** GC-L1-4 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** `managers.ts` (delete confirm; seed vs non‑seed copy; `api.songs.remove`; toast)
- **Requirement:** Deleting a song shall require confirmation; the confirmation shall explain the
  consequence, differing for a built‑in song (removed from the songbook) versus a custom song (its
  family high scores go with it).

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a custom song with family scores
> **WHEN** deletion is requested
> **THEN** the confirmation warns its family high scores will be removed too, and only on confirm is it deleted.

> **GIVEN** a built‑in (seed) song
> **THEN** the confirmation explains it is a built‑in song being removed from the songbook.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** the avatar manager
> **WHEN** a new avatar with an emoji and name is saved
> **THEN** it is created on the server and the list refreshes.

### GC-L2-9 — Reset danger zone
- **Traces to:** GC-L1-6 · **Priority:** Should
- **Revised:** v1.9 (the reset also forgets the device's microphone choice)
- **Source:** `settings.ts` (reset → confirm modal → `store.reset()` and `deviceSettings.setMicrophone(null)` → `#/welcome`)
- **Requirement:** The hub shall offer a "start over" reset that, only after an explicit confirmation,
  erases the active profile and progress on the device, forgets the device's microphone choice (GC-L2-12)
  so the app listens on the system default again, and returns to onboarding; declining shall keep
  everything. The confirmation shall name what is erased.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** the reset action
> **WHEN** the grown‑up confirms
> **THEN** the device profile, sparkles, badges, and range are erased and the app returns to the welcome screen.

> **GIVEN** a microphone choice on the device
> **WHEN** the grown‑up confirms the reset
> **THEN** the microphone choice is gone and the picker shows "System default" the next time the hub opens.

> **GIVEN** the reset confirmation
> **WHEN** the grown‑up declines
> **THEN** nothing is erased, and the microphone choice is kept.

### GC-L2-10 — Privacy disclosure
- **Traces to:** GC-L1-7 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `settings.ts` (intro copy: badges/streaks stay on the device; profiles, songs, family scores live on the home server only; during duets only live scores are shared)
- **Requirement:** The settings hub shall state, in plain language, that on‑device rewards stay on the
  device, that profiles/songs/family scores live only on the home server, and that during duets only
  live scores are shared.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** the settings hub
> **WHEN** it opens
> **THEN** its introduction discloses the on‑device vs home‑server data split and the duet data‑sharing scope.

### GC-L2-11 — Pitch strictness choice
- **Traces to:** GC-L1-8 · **Priority:** Should · **See also:** SR‑L2‑20
- **Source:** `settings.ts` (the "Pitch matching" row: a radio group of three choices; `store.update` of `profile.strictness` on change); `store.ts` (`Profile.strictness`)
- **Requirement:** The hub shall offer a one‑of‑three choice of pitch strictness for the active singer,
  labelled "Gentle", "Just right", and "Precise", with exactly one level chosen at a time. The control
  shall open on the singer's stored level, and on Precise when she has none or the stored value is not
  one of the three. Choosing a level shall save it at once, with no separate save step. The level shall
  belong to the active singer on this device: another singer on the same device keeps her own, and the
  level shall not be sent to the family server. "Start everything over" (GC-L2-9) shall return it to
  Precise. What each level means for judging is defined by SR-L2-20.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a singer who has never had a level chosen
> **WHEN** the settings hub opens
> **THEN** the pitch strictness choice shows "Gentle", "Just right", and "Precise", with "Precise" chosen.

> **GIVEN** the settings hub with "Precise" chosen
> **WHEN** the grown‑up chooses "Gentle"
> **THEN** "Gentle" is chosen, "Precise" is not, and the singer's stored level is Gentle immediately, with no save action.

> **GIVEN** a singer whose stored level is Gentle
> **WHEN** the app is reloaded and the settings hub opens
> **THEN** "Gentle" is chosen.

> **GIVEN** two singers on one device, one stored as Gentle and one with no stored level
> **WHEN** each is the active singer in turn
> **THEN** the hub shows "Gentle" for the first and "Precise" for the second.

> **GIVEN** a stored level that is not one of the three
> **WHEN** the settings hub opens
> **THEN** "Precise" is chosen.

> **GIVEN** a singer stored as Gentle and linked to the family server
> **WHEN** her profile is pushed to, or refreshed from, the server
> **THEN** the level is not part of what is sent, and it is still Gentle on this device afterwards.

> **GIVEN** a singer stored as Gentle
> **WHEN** the grown‑up confirms "Start everything over" and a new profile is created
> **THEN** the new profile's level is Precise.
### GC-L2-12 — Microphone picker
- **Traces to:** GC-L1-9 · **Priority:** Should · **See also:** AE-L2-14, AE-L2-22, SG-L2-28, FS-L2-16
- **Source:** `settings.ts` (`[data-microphone]` select, `[data-microphone-names]` button, `[data-microphone-hint]`; `devicechange` refresh), `audio/microphone.ts` (`describePicker`), `audio/microphone-access.ts` (`listMicrophones`, `revealMicrophoneNames`), `state/device.ts` (`deviceSettings`, key `twinkletune:microphone`)
- **Requirement:** The hub shall provide a microphone picker: a control named "Microphone" listing
  **System default** first and then each microphone the device reports, by name, with a hint telling
  the grown‑up that a headset works best because it stops the tablet hearing its own melody.
  - *Starting value.* With no choice made the picker shall show "System default", and the app shall
    listen exactly as it did before the picker existed.
  - *Saving.* Picking an entry shall save the **microphone choice** at once, with no separate save
    step; picking "System default" shall forget the choice.
  - *Ownership.* There shall be one microphone choice per device, shared by every singer on it and
    unchanged when the active singer changes. It shall not be part of a singer's data, and neither the
    choice nor any microphone name or identifier shall be sent to the family server or anywhere else.
  - *Taking effect.* The choice shall apply the next time the app starts listening — on the Sing
    screen, in Find My Voice, and in the tuner — not to a microphone that is already open.
  - *Names not yet shared.* When the browser lists microphones with their names withheld, the picker
    shall instead offer a "Show microphones" action. Choosing it shall ask for microphone permission
    solely to read the names; the stream it opens shall be stopped at once and nothing analysed
    (AE-L2-22). Once permission is given the picker shall list the microphones.
  - *Permission declined in the picker.* If that request is declined, the picker shall say in plain
    words that the names stay hidden until the microphone is allowed, keep the "Show microphones"
    action, and change nothing that was saved.
  - *Remembered microphone not plugged in.* When the saved choice matches no listed microphone, the
    picker shall still show it as the selected entry, marked in words as not plugged in, and shall say
    that the system default is listening for now. The choice shall be kept, so the microphone is used
    again once it is back. The child shall see no error and no new dialog (AE-L2-22).
  - *A browser that cannot list microphones.* The picker shall show "System default" alone, not
    changeable, and say that this browser picks the microphone itself.
  - *Plugging in while open.* When a microphone is plugged in or removed while the hub is open, the
    picker shall refresh its list.
  - *Reset.* "Start everything over" shall forget the choice (GC-L2-9).

  No state shall be carried by colour alone.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a tablet reporting a built‑in microphone and a headset, and no choice made
> **WHEN** the hub opens
> **THEN** the picker lists "System default", the built‑in microphone and the headset, with "System default" selected.

> **GIVEN** the same tablet
> **WHEN** the grown‑up picks the headset
> **THEN** the choice is saved at once, is still selected after the app is reloaded, and the next song listens on the headset.

> **GIVEN** a saved choice
> **WHEN** the grown‑up picks "System default"
> **THEN** the choice is forgotten and the next song listens on the system default.

> **GIVEN** a saved choice and two singers on the device
> **WHEN** the active singer changes
> **THEN** the picker shows the same choice, and no request to the family server carries it.

> **GIVEN** a tablet reporting only one microphone
> **WHEN** the hub opens
> **THEN** the picker lists "System default" and that microphone, and either can be picked.

> **GIVEN** a browser that has not yet shared microphone names
> **WHEN** the hub opens
> **THEN** the picker offers "Show microphones" in place of the list.

> **GIVEN** that state
> **WHEN** the grown‑up chooses "Show microphones" and allows the microphone
> **THEN** the stream is stopped at once and the picker lists the microphones by name.

> **GIVEN** that state
> **WHEN** the grown‑up chooses "Show microphones" and declines the microphone
> **THEN** the picker says the names stay hidden until the microphone is allowed, still offers "Show microphones", and nothing saved has changed.

> **GIVEN** a saved choice of a headset that is not plugged in
> **WHEN** the hub opens
> **THEN** the picker shows the headset selected and marked "not plugged in", says the system default is listening for now, and the choice is still saved.

> **GIVEN** that saved choice
> **WHEN** the child starts a song
> **THEN** the song starts on the system default with no error and no extra dialog.

> **GIVEN** a browser that cannot list microphones
> **WHEN** the hub opens
> **THEN** the picker shows "System default" alone, cannot be changed, and says this browser picks the microphone itself.

> **GIVEN** the hub open on the list
> **WHEN** a headset is plugged in
> **THEN** it appears in the picker without reopening the hub.
