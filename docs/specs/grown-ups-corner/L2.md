## 1. Purpose & reading guide

Verifiable requirements for the parent gate, settings hub, and content managers. Sources:
`frontend/apps/game/src/ui/modal.ts` (`parentGate`), `frontend/apps/game/src/ui/settings.ts`,
`frontend/apps/game/src/ui/managers.ts`, with entry points in `frontend/apps/game/src/screens/welcome.ts`,
`frontend/apps/game/src/screens/me.ts`, and `frontend/apps/game/src/screens/profiles.ts`.

## 2. Detailed requirements

### GC-L2-1 — Parent gate challenge
- **Traces to:** GC-L1-1 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `modal.ts` (`parentGate`: `a,b ∈ [2,8]`; options `[answer, answer−1, answer+2]` shuffled; wrong answer shakes for 700 ms and keeps the gate open; "Back to singing" cancels)
- **Requirement:** The parent gate shall pose a single‑digit addition question with three answer
  options (one correct, two incorrect); a correct choice shall proceed to the guarded action, a wrong
  choice shall give gentle feedback and keep the gate closed, and a cancel option shall return to play.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** the parent gate for "a + b"
> **WHEN** the correct sum is chosen
> **THEN** the gate closes and the guarded action proceeds.

> **GIVEN** the parent gate
> **WHEN** an incorrect option is chosen
> **THEN** the option shakes, the gate stays open, and the guarded action does not proceed.

---

### GC-L2-2 — Gate placement on guarded actions
- **Traces to:** GC-L1-1 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `welcome.ts` & `me.ts` (`parentGate(showSettings)`); `profiles.ts` (delete behind `parentGate`)
- **Requirement:** Opening the settings hub and deleting a singer shall each require passing the parent
  gate first.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** the child taps the grown‑ups entry (link or gear)
> **WHEN** the gate is not passed
> **THEN** the settings hub does not open.

> **GIVEN** a singer deletion is requested
> **WHEN** the gate is not passed
> **THEN** deletion does not proceed.

---

### GC-L2-3 — Settings hub layout
- **Traces to:** GC-L1-2 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** `settings.ts` (`showSettings`: latency, voice re‑setup, song/avatar managers, singers/profiles, danger zone)
- **Requirement:** The settings hub shall present, in one place: the microphone timing offset, a voice
  re‑setup shortcut, the song and avatar managers, a profiles‑management shortcut, and a danger‑zone
  reset, alongside the privacy disclosure.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** the gate is passed
> **WHEN** the settings hub opens
> **THEN** controls for latency, voice re‑setup, songs, avatars, profiles, and reset are all present.

---

### GC-L2-4 — Microphone‑latency calibration
- **Traces to:** GC-L1-3 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** `settings.ts` (range input −300…300, step 10; live `store.update` of `profile.latencyMs`; label shows " ms")
- **Requirement:** The hub shall provide a −300…+300 ms slider (10 ms steps) that updates the profile's
  mic latency live, with a numeric read‑out; the value shall stay device‑local (not synced as a person
  property) and shall feed scoring compensation.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** the latency slider at 0 ms
> **WHEN** it is moved to +120 ms
> **THEN** the read‑out shows "120 ms" and the profile's latency is updated immediately and persisted.

---

### GC-L2-5 — Voice re‑setup shortcut
- **Traces to:** GC-L1-2 · **Priority:** Could · **See also:** VP‑L2‑13
- **Source:** `settings.ts` ("Re‑do" link → `#/voice`, closes the modal)
- **Requirement:** The hub shall offer a shortcut to re‑run "Find My Voice", showing the current range.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** the settings hub with a stored range
> **WHEN** "Re‑do" is chosen
> **THEN** the hub closes and the voice‑setup screen opens.

---

### GC-L2-6 — Song manager and editor
- **Traces to:** GC-L1-4 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** `managers.ts` (`showSongManager`, `songEditor`: title/emoji/bpm/difficulty/art fields + phrases JSON; `Preview ▶` plays with a 1‑beat count‑in; validation via `validateSong`; save via `api.songs.create/update`; seed songs labelled "built‑in")
- **Requirement:** The song manager shall list the songbook (marking built‑in songs), allow creating a
  new song or editing an existing one via a structured form plus a phrases/notes JSON field, offer a
  live preview, validate before saving (identically to the server), and persist via the API.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** the song editor with a valid song
> **WHEN** "Preview" is tapped
> **THEN** the song plays with a short count‑in.

> **GIVEN** the song editor with an invalid phrases JSON or an invariant violation
> **WHEN** "Save" is tapped
> **THEN** the specific validation messages are shown and nothing is saved.

> **GIVEN** a valid new song
> **WHEN** it is saved
> **THEN** it is created on the server and the manager list refreshes to include it.

---

### GC-L2-7 — Song deletion with context‑aware confirmation
- **Traces to:** GC-L1-4 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** `managers.ts` (delete confirm; seed vs non‑seed copy; `api.songs.remove`; toast)
- **Requirement:** Deleting a song shall require confirmation; the confirmation shall explain the
  consequence, differing for a built‑in song (removed from the songbook) versus a custom song (its
  family high scores go with it).

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a custom song with family scores
> **WHEN** deletion is requested
> **THEN** the confirmation warns its family high scores will be removed too, and only on confirm is it deleted.

> **GIVEN** a built‑in (seed) song
> **THEN** the confirmation explains it is a built‑in song being removed from the songbook.

---

### GC-L2-8 — Avatar manager
- **Traces to:** GC-L1-5 · **Priority:** Could
- **Source:** `managers.ts` (`showAvatarManager`, `avatarEditor`: emoji + name; delete without a confirm dialog; `api.avatars.*`)
- **Requirement:** The avatar manager shall list avatars and allow creating, editing, and deleting them
  (emoji + name), persisting via the API.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** the avatar manager
> **WHEN** a new avatar with an emoji and name is saved
> **THEN** it is created on the server and the list refreshes.

---

### GC-L2-9 — Reset danger zone
- **Traces to:** GC-L1-6 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** `settings.ts` (reset → confirm modal → `store.reset()` → `#/welcome`)
- **Requirement:** The hub shall offer a "start over" reset that, only after an explicit confirmation,
  erases the active profile and progress on the device and returns to onboarding; declining shall keep
  everything.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** the reset action
> **WHEN** the grown‑up confirms
> **THEN** the device profile, sparkles, badges, and range are erased and the app returns to the welcome screen.

> **GIVEN** the reset confirmation
> **WHEN** the grown‑up declines
> **THEN** nothing is erased.

---

### GC-L2-10 — Privacy disclosure
- **Traces to:** GC-L1-7 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `settings.ts` (intro copy: badges/streaks stay on the device; profiles, songs, family scores live on the home server only; during duets only live scores are shared)
- **Requirement:** The settings hub shall state, in plain language, that on‑device rewards stay on the
  device, that profiles/songs/family scores live only on the home server, and that during duets only
  live scores are shared.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** the settings hub
> **WHEN** it opens
> **THEN** its introduction discloses the on‑device vs home‑server data split and the duet data‑sharing scope.
