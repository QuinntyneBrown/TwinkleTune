# Voice Personalization — L1 High-Level Requirements

**Subsystem code:** VP
**Specification level:** L1 (capability / high‑level)
**Baseline:** v1.0 · **Status:** Approved
**Detailed requirements:** [L2.md](./L2.md)
**Primary dependencies:** Audio Engine (AE), Player Profiles (PP), Family Server Platform (FS), Song Library (SL)

---

## 1. Purpose

Voice Personalization is the capability that makes TwinkleTune's central promise real: **every song is
tuned to the singer's own voice.** It captures a child's comfortable vocal range through a short,
playful "Find My Voice" game, stores that range against the singer, and computes the semitone shift
that re‑keys any song so it never sits too high or too low for her.

## 2. Scope

### In scope

- The guided "Find My Voice" experience (capture of a comfortable low note and a comfortable high note).
- Stable‑pitch detection used for capture, including tolerance of momentary microphone dropouts.
- Quality guards that reject an implausible or unstable capture.
- Computation of the per‑song transposition ("shift") from a stored range.
- Persistence of the range to the singer's profile and its synchronisation to the family server so it
  follows the singer across devices.
- Re‑capturing the range at any time, and surfacing range growth to the child.
- Kid‑facing communication of the personalization ("tuned just for you", "in your key").

### Out of scope

- The synthesis that renders a transposed melody, and the raw pitch‑tracking primitive — owned by
  **Audio Engine (AE)**; VP consumes them.
- Live in‑song scoring and octave folding — owned by **Scoring & Results (SR)**.
- Microphone latency compensation, which is a *device* property, not a *person* property — owned by
  **Grown‑Ups Corner (GC)**.
- Storage and switching of the profile record itself — owned by **Player Profiles (PP)**.

## 3. Context & dependencies

The captured range is a property of the *person* and therefore lives on the singer's profile
(client `Profile.range`) and, when a family server is present, on the `Singer` entity (`RangeLow`,
`RangeHigh`) so it follows her from tablet to tablet. Transposition is a pure function of the song's
note span and the singer's range; because songs are stored as note data (SL) and synthesised on device
(AE), re‑keying is a frequency shift with **no audio artefacts**.

## 4. High-level requirements

| ID | Requirement | Priority | Rationale |
|----|-------------|----------|-----------|
| **VP-L1-1** | The system shall capture a singer's comfortable vocal range (a low note and a high note) through a short, guided, game‑like flow that a child can complete unaided. | Must | The range is the input to every transposition; capture must be effortless and non‑intimidating for a nine‑year‑old. |
| **VP-L1-2** | The system shall transpose every song so its note span sits centred within the captured range, bounded so the result stays natural, and shall do so without audio artefacts. | Must | "In your key" is the core differentiator; a bounded, artefact‑free shift keeps songs comfortable and pleasant. |
| **VP-L1-3** | The system shall persist the captured range against the singer and, when a family server is reachable, synchronise it so the range follows the singer across devices. | Must | A child should tune once and have every device know her voice. |
| **VP-L1-4** | The system shall guard capture quality, accepting only a sustained, stable pitch and rejecting a high‑note capture that is not clearly above the low note. | Must | A bad range would mis‑key every song; the guard protects the whole downstream experience. |
| **VP-L1-5** | The system shall allow the range to be re‑captured at any time and shall reflect a changed range in subsequent play and in the child's progress view. | Should | Children's voices grow; re‑tuning is both practical and a celebratory "your voice grew" moment. |
| **VP-L1-6** | The system shall function without a captured range and without a microphone, falling back to a standard key, so that solo play is never blocked. | Must | Offline‑first (P2): personalization enhances play but must never gate it. |
| **VP-L1-7** | The system shall communicate personalization to the child in warm, non‑technical language, never exposing raw musical or technical detail as a requirement to proceed. | Should | Kid‑first interaction (P5) and encouragement‑only (P1). |

## 5. Assumptions & constraints

- A working microphone and the browser's `getUserMedia` permission are required to *capture* a range;
  neither is required to *play*.
- The kid‑singable base range for content is C3–C6 (MIDI 48–84); capture and the ladder operate within
  this band (see SL for content invariants).
- Transposition is bounded to ±6 semitones; beyond that, octave‑folded scoring (SR) means a different
  octave already fits better, so a larger shift adds no value.
- Range values are integers (MIDI note numbers).
