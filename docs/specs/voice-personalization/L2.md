# Voice Personalization — L2 Detailed Requirements

**Subsystem code:** VP
**Specification level:** L2 (detailed / verifiable)
**Baseline:** v1.0 · **Status:** Approved
**Traces up to:** [L1.md](./L1.md)

## 1. Purpose & reading guide

Each requirement below is a verifiable statement that traces to one or more VP L1 requirements and is
accompanied by GIVEN/WHEN/THEN acceptance criteria. Numeric constants are the shipped baseline values.
Primary sources: `frontend/src/screens/voice-setup.ts`, `frontend/src/audio/range.ts`,
`frontend/src/state/profile.ts`, `backend/src/TwinkleTune.Application/Services/SingerService.cs`.

## 2. Detailed requirements

### VP-L2-1 — Three‑step guided capture flow
- **Traces to:** VP-L1-1 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `frontend/src/screens/voice-setup.ts` (`phase: 'intro' | 'low' | 'high'`, `setSteps`)
- **Requirement:** The "Find My Voice" screen shall present exactly three visible steps — an
  introduction, a low‑note capture, and a high‑note capture — and shall advance a three‑dot progress
  indicator as the singer moves through them.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** the singer opens the "Find My Voice" screen
> **WHEN** it first renders
> **THEN** the phase is *intro*, step 1 of 3 is marked current, and the primary action reads "I'm ready — listen! 🎤".

> **GIVEN** the singer is in the *low* phase (step 2)
> **WHEN** her low note is captured
> **THEN** the flow advances to the *high* phase and marks step 3 as current.

### VP-L2-2 — Visual pitch ladder spanning the singable band
- **Traces to:** VP-L1-1 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** `voice-setup.ts` (`LADDER_LOW = 48`, `LADDER_RUNGS = 12`, `RUNG_SPAN = 3`, `rungFor`)
- **Requirement:** The screen shall display a ladder of 12 rungs spanning C3 to C6 (3 semitones per
  rung) and shall highlight the rung corresponding to the singer's currently detected pitch, filling
  in lower rungs to visualise how high she is singing.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** the microphone is live and the singer sustains a note at MIDI *m*
> **WHEN** each animation frame paints the ladder
> **THEN** the rung `clamp(floor((m − 48) / 3), 0, 11)` is marked current, rungs below it are lit, and the singer's avatar marker sits on the current rung.

> **GIVEN** no pitch is detected in a frame
> **THEN** the ladder shows no current rung and the level meter is not in its live state.

### VP-L2-3 — Stable sustained‑pitch lock
- **Traces to:** VP-L1-1, VP-L1-4 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `frontend/src/audio/range.ts` (`StablePitchCapture`, window 40 frames, spread 1.0 semitone); `frontend/src/audio/range.test.ts`
- **Requirement:** Capture shall lock onto a note only after the singer holds a steady pitch for a full
  detection window (40 frames, ≈0.7 s at 60 fps) during which the spread between the 10th and 90th
  percentile of detected pitches is at most 1.0 semitone; on lock it shall return the median pitch and
  reset for the next capture.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a capture window of 40 frames
> **WHEN** the singer holds a note near C4 with only a tiny wobble (spread ≤ 1.0 semitone) for the full window
> **THEN** the capture locks and returns the median (≈C4).

> **GIVEN** the singer's pitch is sliding continuously (a glissando)
> **WHEN** frames are fed for longer than one window
> **THEN** no lock occurs, because the percentile spread never falls within 1.0 semitone.

### VP-L2-4 — Tolerance of momentary dropouts
- **Traces to:** VP-L1-4 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** `range.ts` (`StablePitchCapture.feed(null)`, resets after >8 silent frames)
- **Requirement:** A capture in progress shall survive brief detector dropouts (silent frames) but
  shall reset its buffer once silence exceeds 8 consecutive frames, so that a genuinely abandoned
  attempt does not produce a stale reading.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a partially filled capture buffer
> **WHEN** a single silent frame is fed
> **THEN** the accumulated progress is retained (progress > 0).

> **GIVEN** a partially filled capture buffer
> **WHEN** 9 consecutive silent frames are fed
> **THEN** the buffer resets and progress returns to 0.

### VP-L2-5 — Low‑note capture with audible confirmation
- **Traces to:** VP-L1-1 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `voice-setup.ts` (`captured` for low phase; `playNote`; toast)
- **Requirement:** When the low note locks, the system shall store it as the range low, play the
  captured note back so the child hears it, mark the ladder rung as captured, confirm with an
  encouraging message, and advance to the high phase.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** the *low* phase is active
> **WHEN** a stable pitch at MIDI *m* locks
> **THEN** the range low is set to `round(m)`, that note is played back, a "Got it! Your cozy low note: <name> 🌊" confirmation is shown, and the flow advances to the *high* phase.

---

### VP-L2-6 — High‑note separation guard
- **Traces to:** VP-L1-4 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `voice-setup.ts` (`captured` for high phase: rejects when `midi < lowMidi + 4`)
- **Requirement:** In the high phase, a captured pitch that is fewer than 4 semitones above the stored
  low note shall be rejected with a gentle "reach a little higher" prompt and shall not be accepted as
  the high note; only a pitch at least 4 semitones above the low note shall complete capture.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** the low note was captured at MIDI *L*
> **WHEN** the high phase locks a pitch at MIDI *H* with `H < L + 4`
> **THEN** the capture is rejected, a "reach a little higher! 🐦" prompt is shown, and the high phase remains active.

> **GIVEN** the low note was captured at MIDI *L*
> **WHEN** the high phase locks a pitch at MIDI *H* with `H ≥ L + 4`
> **THEN** the high note is accepted and capture completes.

### VP-L2-7 — Encouraging, non‑technical guidance
- **Traces to:** VP-L1-1, VP-L1-7 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** `voice-setup.ts` (coach‑bubble copy for intro/low/high; "why" note)
- **Requirement:** Each phase shall guide the child with playful, imagery‑based instructions ("a big
  cozy laaa", "squeak up high like a baby bird") and shall explain the benefit ("tunes every song to
  fit YOUR voice — never too high, never too low") without requiring the child to understand notes,
  octaves, or MIDI.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** the *low* phase begins
> **THEN** the coach bubble instructs the child to sing her lowest comfortable "laaa" and hold it, in child‑friendly language, with no musical notation shown as an instruction.

### VP-L2-8 — Transposition (shift) computation
- **Traces to:** VP-L1-2, VP-L1-6 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `range.ts` (`computeShift`); `frontend/src/audio/range.test.ts`
- **Requirement:** For a given song and range, the system shall compute the shift as the rounded
  difference between the range centre and the song's note‑span centre — `round(((low+high)/2) −
  ((min+max)/2))` — and shall return 0 when no range is known.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a song whose note span is centred at pitch *C*
> **WHEN** a range centred on *C* is supplied
> **THEN** the computed shift is 0 ("Already a perfect fit!").

> **GIVEN** a song and a voice range lower than the song's centre
> **WHEN** the shift is computed
> **THEN** the shift is negative (the song moves down).

> **GIVEN** no range is stored (`null`)
> **WHEN** the shift is computed
> **THEN** the shift is 0.

---

### VP-L2-9 — Shift clamped to ±6 semitones
- **Traces to:** VP-L1-2 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `range.ts` (`clamp(..., -6, 6)`); `range.test.ts`
- **Requirement:** The computed shift shall be clamped to the inclusive range −6..+6 semitones.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a very high voice range far above the song
> **WHEN** the shift is computed
> **THEN** the result is exactly +6.

> **GIVEN** a very low voice range far below the song
> **WHEN** the shift is computed
> **THEN** the result is exactly −6.

### VP-L2-10 — Range persisted to the singer's profile
- **Traces to:** VP-L1-3 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `voice-setup.ts` (`finish` → `store.update` sets `profile.range`)
- **Requirement:** On completing capture, the system shall persist `{ low, high }` to the active
  singer's local profile so that subsequent play is tuned without re‑capture.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** capture completes with low *L* and high *H*
> **WHEN** the completion step runs
> **THEN** the active profile's `range` is `{ low: L, high: H }` and persists across a reload of the app.

---

### VP-L2-11 — Range synchronised to the family server
- **Traces to:** VP-L1-3 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** `state/profile.ts` (`pushProfileToServer`); `SingerService` (`RangeLow`/`RangeHigh`); `SingerService.Validate` (high must exceed low)
- **Requirement:** When the active profile is linked to a family server, the captured range shall be
  pushed to the singer record (fire‑and‑forget) so it follows the singer across devices; the server
  shall reject a range whose high note is not above its low note. A failed push (offline) shall not
  block capture and shall reconcile on a later successful update.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a server‑linked singer completes capture and the server is reachable
> **WHEN** the range push runs
> **THEN** the singer's `rangeLow`/`rangeHigh` on the server reflect the captured values.

> **GIVEN** a singer update whose `rangeHigh ≤ rangeLow`
> **WHEN** the server validates it
> **THEN** it is rejected with the message "The high note must be above the low note." and the record is unchanged.

> **GIVEN** the server is unreachable at capture time
> **WHEN** the push fails
> **THEN** capture still completes and the local range is stored; no error is surfaced to the child.

### VP-L2-12 — "In your key" indication and completion summary
- **Traces to:** VP-L1-2, VP-L1-6, VP-L1-7 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** `voice-setup.ts` (completion modal); `frontend/src/screens/songs.ts` (`✓ In your key` / `Find my voice →`); `range.ts` (`describeShift`)
- **Requirement:** After capture, the system shall confirm the discovered range in words ("You sing
  from <low> up to <high>"). Song cards shall indicate, per song, that it is tuned to the singer ("✓ In
  your key", with a tooltip describing the shift) when a range exists, or invite capture ("Find my
  voice →") when it does not.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a stored range and a song requiring a −2 shift
> **WHEN** the song card renders
> **THEN** it shows "✓ In your key" with a tooltip reading "Moved 2 notes lower for you 💙".

> **GIVEN** no stored range
> **WHEN** a song card renders
> **THEN** it shows a "Find my voice →" call to action linking to the capture screen.

### VP-L2-13 — Re‑capture entry points
- **Traces to:** VP-L1-5 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** `frontend/src/ui/settings.ts` ("Re‑do" → `#/voice`); `screens/home.ts` & `screens/me.ts` (voice card / "Find your voice"); `main.ts` route `voice`
- **Requirement:** The system shall allow re‑running "Find My Voice" at any time from the Grown‑Ups
  Corner ("Re‑do"), from the home screen, and from the progress ("Me") screen; re‑running shall
  overwrite the stored range with the new capture.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a singer with an existing range
> **WHEN** she re‑runs capture and completes it with a new low/high
> **THEN** the stored range is replaced by the new values and later play uses the new shift.

---

### VP-L2-14 — Range growth surfaced in progress
- **Traces to:** VP-L1-5, VP-L1-7 · **Priority:** Could
- **Source:** `screens/me.ts` (growth block: "Your voice goes from <low> to <high> — that's <high−low+1> notes!")
- **Requirement:** The progress ("Me") screen shall express the singer's range as a friendly note‑count
  span so a re‑captured, wider range is visibly celebrated.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a stored range of low *L* and high *H*
> **WHEN** the "Me" screen renders the growth block
> **THEN** it states the span in words and reports the note count as `H − L + 1`.

---

### VP-L2-15 — Standard‑key fallback and mic‑denied handling
- **Traces to:** VP-L1-6 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `voice-setup.ts` (`micDeniedDialog`); `computeShift` returns 0 with no range; `songs.ts` ("Standard key")
- **Requirement:** If the microphone is denied during capture, the system shall offer to try again or
  defer, without blocking the app; with no range stored, songs shall play in a standard key (shift 0)
  so solo play continues unimpeded.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** the singer starts capture and the microphone permission is denied
> **WHEN** the denial is detected
> **THEN** a gentle dialog offers "Try again 🎤" and "Do this later" (returning home), and no range is stored.

> **GIVEN** no range is stored
> **WHEN** the singer plays a song
> **THEN** the song plays untransposed (shift 0) and play is not blocked.
