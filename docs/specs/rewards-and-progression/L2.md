## 1. Purpose & reading guide

## 2. Detailed requirements

### RP-L2-1 — Sparkle accumulation
- **Traces to:** RP-L1-1 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `store.ts` (`recordPlay`: `sparkles += summary.sparkles`); `screens/tips.ts` (`sparkles += 5`)
- **Requirement:** The system shall add the play's earned sparkles (10 × landed notes) to the running
  total on each completed play, and 5 sparkles on each completed tip; sparkles shall never decrease
  except on a full reset.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a total of 100 sparkles and a play earning 80
> **WHEN** the play is recorded
> **THEN** the total becomes 180.

> **GIVEN** any completed tip
> **THEN** the total increases by exactly 5.

---

### RP-L2-2 — Level and title derivation
- **Traces to:** RP-L1-1 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** `store.ts` (`level`, `sparklesToNextLevel`, `LEVEL_TITLES`, `levelTitle`)
- **Requirement:** Level shall be ⌊sparkles ÷ 300⌋ + 1; sparkles‑to‑next‑level shall be 300 −
  (sparkles mod 300); the title shall come from the fixed list Hatchling, Chick, Fledgling, Songbird,
  Skylark, Nightingale, Superstar, clamped at the last for high levels.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** 0 sparkles
> **THEN** level is 1 ("Hatchling") and 300 sparkles remain to level 2.

> **GIVEN** 650 sparkles
> **THEN** level is 3 and 250 sparkles remain to level 4.

> **GIVEN** a level of 8 or more
> **THEN** the title is "Superstar".

---

### RP-L2-3 — Daily goal and ring
- **Traces to:** RP-L1-2 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** `home.ts` (`DAILY_GOAL = 3`; `sungToday = songsSungOn(state, today)`; `goalDeg = min(1, sungToday/3)·360`)
- **Requirement:** The home screen shall track songs sung today against a goal of 3 and render a ring
  that fills proportionally, capped at complete, with a status message for none/partial/complete.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** 0 songs sung today
> **THEN** the ring is empty and the status invites singing 3 songs today.

> **GIVEN** 2 songs sung today
> **THEN** the ring is two‑thirds filled and the status says one more to go.

> **GIVEN** 3 or more songs sung today
> **THEN** the ring is full and the status celebrates completing the goal.

---

### RP-L2-4 — Daily streak (forgiving)
- **Traces to:** RP-L1-2 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** `store.ts` (`streakCount`: consecutive sing‑days ending today, or yesterday if today has none)
- **Requirement:** The daily streak shall count consecutive sing‑days ending at today, or at yesterday
  when today has not yet been sung, so a streak is not shown as lost partway through the day.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** sing‑days for the last three consecutive days including today
> **THEN** the streak is 3.

> **GIVEN** sing‑days for yesterday and the day before, and nothing yet today
> **THEN** the streak is 2 (not 0).

> **GIVEN** the most recent sing‑day was two days ago
> **THEN** the streak is 0.

---

### RP-L2-5 — Per‑song personal best
- **Traces to:** RP-L1-3 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** `store.ts` (`recordPlay` bests: `stars = max(prev, noMic?0:stars)`, `accuracy = max(prev, noMic?0:accuracy)`, `plays += 1`)
- **Requirement:** Each song's best shall keep the maximum stars and accuracy ever achieved and a total
  play count; a no‑mic run shall increment the play count but shall contribute 0 to best stars and
  accuracy.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a song with best 2 stars / 0.7 accuracy
> **WHEN** a new scored play gets 3 stars / 0.9
> **THEN** the best becomes 3 stars / 0.9 and plays increments.

> **GIVEN** the same song
> **WHEN** a no‑mic run is recorded
> **THEN** best stars and accuracy are unchanged and the play count still increments.

---

### RP-L2-6 — Badge catalogue and award rules
- **Traces to:** RP-L1-4 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** `badges.ts` (`BADGES`, `evaluateBadges`); `badges.test.ts`
- **Requirement:** The system shall award badges by these rules: **First Song** at ≥1 total play;
  **3‑Day Streak** at streak ≥ 3; **High Note Hero** when scored and braveness stars = 3 and accuracy
  ≥ 0.5; **Show Stopper** when scored and overall stars = 3; **Brave Bird** on finishing a
  difficulty‑3 song; **Perfect Ten** when scored and the max streak ≥ 10.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** the first ever play
> **THEN** "First Song" is awarded.

> **GIVEN** a scored play with 3 overall stars
> **THEN** "Show Stopper" is awarded.

> **GIVEN** finishing a Brave (difficulty‑3) song, even in no‑mic mode
> **THEN** "Brave Bird" is awarded.

---

### RP-L2-7 — Badge idempotency and scored gating
- **Traces to:** RP-L1-4 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** `badges.ts` (`scored = !noMic && total > 0`; already‑earned excluded); `badges.test.ts`
- **Requirement:** No badge shall be awarded twice, and performance badges (High Note Hero, Show
  Stopper, Perfect Ten) shall never be awarded from a no‑mic run.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** "First Song" already earned
> **WHEN** another play completes
> **THEN** "First Song" is not in the newly earned list.

> **GIVEN** a no‑mic run with 3 stars, a 10‑streak, and 3 braveness stars
> **THEN** none of Show Stopper, Perfect Ten, or High Note Hero is awarded, though "First Song" (playing counts) may still be.

---

### RP-L2-8 — Record‑play orchestration
- **Traces to:** RP-L1-6 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `store.ts` (`recordPlay`: sparkles, singDays dedup, plays++, bests, badges, lastResult/lastSongId/lastNewBadges)
- **Requirement:** Recording a completed play shall, in one persisted operation, add sparkles, mark
  today as a sing‑day (without duplicates), increment today's play count, update the song best,
  evaluate and store newly earned badges with today's date, and stash the last result, song id, and
  new‑badge list for the results screen.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a completed play on a new day
> **WHEN** it is recorded
> **THEN** today appears once in the sing‑days, the play count for today is incremented, and the last result/song/new‑badges are available to the results screen.

---

### RP-L2-9 — "Star Journey" progress view
- **Traces to:** RP-L1-5 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** `me.ts` (XP card; weekly ring `DAY_LETTERS` Monday‑first; badges grid of all 6; "songs I shine at"; growth block)
- **Requirement:** The progress ("Me") screen shall show the level and sparkles with an XP bar to the
  next level, a seven‑day week strip marking sung days and today, all six badges as earned or locked
  with hints, the songs mastered (best stars > 0) sorted by stars, and the voice‑range growth block.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a singer at 650 sparkles who sang Mon/Tue/today
> **WHEN** the "Me" screen renders
> **THEN** the XP bar shows progress within level 3, the week strip marks Mon/Tue/today, earned badges show their emoji and locked ones a lock, and mastered songs are listed by stars.

> **GIVEN** a singer who has never landed a note on any song
> **THEN** the "songs I shine at" list shows an encouraging empty state.

---

### RP-L2-10 — Mastered count on home
- **Traces to:** RP-L1-5 · **Priority:** Could
- **Source:** `home.ts` (`masteredCount = bests with stars > 0`)
- **Requirement:** The home dashboard shall report the number of songs the singer has mastered (best
  stars > 0), with correct singular/plural wording.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** one song with best stars > 0
> **THEN** the home tile reads "1 song mastered".

> **GIVEN** three such songs
> **THEN** it reads "3 songs mastered".

---

### RP-L2-11 — On‑device, per‑profile persistence
- **Traces to:** RP-L1-6 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `store.ts` (`localStorage`‑backed store; per‑singer key via PP); corrupted state falls back to empty
- **Requirement:** All rewards (sparkles, sing‑days, plays, bests, badges) shall persist in device
  storage under the active profile's namespace and survive reloads; corrupted stored state shall fall
  back to an empty state rather than crashing. No reward data shall require the server.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a singer with recorded rewards
> **WHEN** the app is reloaded
> **THEN** her sparkles, streak, bests, and badges are restored from device storage.

> **GIVEN** corrupted stored state
> **WHEN** the store loads
> **THEN** it starts from an empty state without error.
