# Rewards & Progression — L1 High-Level Requirements

**Subsystem code:** RP
**Specification level:** L1 (capability / high‑level)
**Baseline:** v1.0 · **Status:** Approved
**Detailed requirements:** [L2.md](./L2.md)
**Primary dependencies:** Scoring & Results (SR), Player Profiles (PP, storage namespacing), Coaching & Encouragement (CE, tip sparkles), Application Shell (AS)

---

## 1. Purpose

Rewards & Progression is the game layer that makes practice feel like play and keeps a child coming
back — kindly. It awards **sparkles** as a universal, never‑subtracted currency; grows a **level** and a
**title** from them; tracks a gentle **daily goal** and a forgiving **daily streak**; remembers a
personal **best** per song; and awards a small set of **badges** by clear, fair rules. All of it lives
on the device, per profile, and needs no server.

## 2. Scope

### In scope

- Sparkle accumulation (from landed notes and from completing tips) and the level/title derivation.
- The daily singing goal and the daily‑streak calculation.
- Per‑song personal bests (stars, accuracy, play count).
- The badge catalogue and its award rules, including idempotency and scored‑only gating.
- The orchestration that records a completed play (sparkles, streak day, bests, badges).
- The "Star Journey" progress view (level/XP, weekly ring, badges, mastered songs, voice growth).
- The mastered‑song count and daily‑goal ring on the home dashboard.
- On‑device, per‑profile persistence of all rewards.

### Out of scope

- Computing the per‑play summary (accuracy, stars, sparkles‑earned, streak) — **SR**.
- Revealing newly earned badges on the results screen — **SR** (RP decides which are new).
- Family (cross‑device) high scores — **HS**; those are server‑owned and distinct from local rewards.
- Storage keying and profile switching — **PP** (RP writes into whatever profile store is active).

## 3. Context & dependencies

When a play completes, gameplay calls `recordPlay()`, which folds the SR summary into the on‑device
state: it adds sparkles, marks today as a sing‑day, increments today's play count, updates the song's
best, evaluates badges, and stashes the last result for the results screen. Levels, streaks, and goals
are pure functions of this state. All state is namespaced per singer by PP, so each child's progress is
her own.

## 4. High-level requirements

| ID | Requirement | Priority | Rationale |
|----|-------------|----------|-----------|
| **RP-L1-1** | The system shall award sparkles for singing and for completing tips, accumulate them without ever subtracting, and derive a level and an encouraging title from the total. | Must | A single, always‑growing currency is motivating and never punishing (P1). |
| **RP-L1-2** | The system shall track a daily singing goal and a daily streak that counts consecutive days sung, treating a streak as intact until a whole day is missed. | Should | Gentle habit‑building without the anxiety of losing a streak mid‑day. |
| **RP-L1-3** | The system shall remember a personal best per song — best stars, best accuracy, and play count — updating only upward and never crediting a no‑mic run with stars or accuracy. | Should | Personal bests reward mastery; excluding unscored runs keeps them meaningful. |
| **RP-L1-4** | The system shall award a fixed catalogue of achievement badges by clear rules, each at most once, and shall gate performance badges to scored play only. | Should | Badges are milestone celebrations; fairness requires one‑time, scored‑only awarding. |
| **RP-L1-5** | The system shall present a progress view that celebrates level and sparkles, the week's singing, earned and yet‑to‑earn badges, mastered songs, and the singer's voice growth. | Should | A visible "Star Journey" turns invisible progress into pride. |
| **RP-L1-6** | The system shall persist all rewards on the device, per profile, and function fully without a server. | Must | Offline‑first (P2) and privacy (P3): on‑device rewards never leave the device. |

## 5. Assumptions & constraints

- One level per 300 sparkles; level = ⌊sparkles ÷ 300⌋ + 1; titles cap at "Superstar".
- Daily goal is 3 songs/day.
- A landed note is worth 10 sparkles (SR); completing a tip is worth 5 sparkles (CE).
- Badge catalogue: First Song, 3‑Day Streak, High Note Hero, Show Stopper, Brave Bird, Perfect Ten.
