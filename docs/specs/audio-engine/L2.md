# Audio Engine — L2 Detailed Requirements

**Subsystem code:** AE
**Specification level:** L2 (detailed / verifiable)
**Baseline:** v1.0 · **Status:** Approved
**Traces up to:** [L1.md](./L1.md)

---

## 1. Purpose & reading guide

Verifiable requirements for on‑device synthesis and pitch detection. Constants are the shipped
baseline. Primary sources: `frontend/src/audio/player.ts`, `frontend/src/audio/pitch.ts`,
`frontend/src/audio/range.ts`, `frontend/src/screens/tuner.ts`.

---

## 2. Detailed requirements

### AE-L2-1 — Melody voice synthesis
- **Traces to:** AE-L1-1 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `player.ts` (`SongPlayer.tone`: triangle oscillator + quiet sine an octave below; ADSR‑style gain envelope; master gain 0.5)
- **Requirement:** The engine shall synthesise each note as a triangle oscillator at the note's
  frequency plus a quieter sine one octave below, shaped by an attack/sustain/release gain envelope,
  mixed through a master gain, so the voice is soft and toy‑piano‑like.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a song with at least one note
> **WHEN** the song is started
> **THEN** for each in‑window note an oscillator pair is scheduled with an amplitude envelope that ramps up, holds, and ramps down, and no note is heard as a click or with a hard edge.

---

### AE-L2-2 — Semitone transposition
- **Traces to:** AE-L1-2 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `player.ts` (`midiToHz(note.midi + transpose)`); `range.ts` (`midiToHz`)
- **Requirement:** The engine shall shift every note's pitch by the caller‑supplied `transpose`
  semitones by converting `note.midi + transpose` to frequency, with no change to timing and no
  artefacts.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a song played with `transpose = +3`
> **WHEN** a note whose base is MIDI 60 is scheduled
> **THEN** it sounds at the frequency of MIDI 63 (`440·2^((63−69)/12)` Hz), and its start/duration are unchanged.

> **GIVEN** the same song played with `transpose = 0`
> **THEN** every note sounds at its base pitch.

---

### AE-L2-3 — Tempo / rate scaling (incl. slow practice)
- **Traces to:** AE-L1-2 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `player.ts` (`spb = 60 / song.bpm / rate`)
- **Requirement:** The engine shall scale playback speed by the caller‑supplied `rate`, computing
  seconds‑per‑beat as `60 / bpm / rate`; a `rate` of 0.7 shall lengthen every beat for slow practice
  while preserving pitch.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a 100 BPM song
> **WHEN** it is played at `rate = 1`
> **THEN** one beat lasts 0.6 s.

> **GIVEN** the same song played at `rate = 0.7`
> **THEN** one beat lasts ≈0.857 s and every note keeps its original pitch.

---

### AE-L2-4 — Segment playback bounds
- **Traces to:** AE-L1-2 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `player.ts` (`fromBeat`, `untilBeat`; `end = min(untilBeat, songBeats(song))`; notes with `start < fromBeat || start >= end` skipped)
- **Requirement:** The engine shall play only notes whose start lies in `[fromBeat, end)` where
  `end = min(untilBeat, songLengthInBeats)`, enabling a single phrase (or the whole song) to be
  scheduled.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a song and a request with `fromBeat = 16`, `untilBeat = 24`
> **WHEN** it is started
> **THEN** only notes starting at or after beat 16 and before beat 24 are scheduled.

> **GIVEN** a request with `untilBeat = Infinity`
> **THEN** playback ends at the song's final note (`end = songLengthInBeats`).

---

### AE-L2-5 — Count‑in
- **Traces to:** AE-L1-2 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `player.ts` (`countInBeats` default 4; `click` at 1568 Hz for beat 0, 1046 Hz otherwise)
- **Requirement:** Before the melody, the engine shall play `countInBeats` metronome clicks (default 4)
  at one per beat, with the first click higher in pitch than the rest, to prepare the singer.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a song started with the default count‑in
> **WHEN** playback begins
> **THEN** 4 clicks sound one per beat before the first note, the first click being higher (1568 Hz) than the remaining clicks (1046 Hz).

> **GIVEN** a segment started with `countInBeats = 2`
> **THEN** exactly 2 count‑in clicks precede the segment.

---

### AE-L2-6 — Musical position clock
- **Traces to:** AE-L1-3 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `player.ts` (`currentBeat()`, `secondsPerBeat()`)
- **Requirement:** The engine shall report the current position in beats relative to the segment start,
  returning a negative value during the count‑in and increasing through the segment; it shall also
  report the current seconds‑per‑beat.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a segment started with a 4‑beat count‑in from `fromBeat = 0`
> **WHEN** the clock is read during the count‑in
> **THEN** `currentBeat()` is negative and rises toward 0 as the first note approaches.

> **WHEN** the clock is read after the count‑in, one beat into the melody
> **THEN** `currentBeat()` is approximately 1.

---

### AE-L2-7 — End‑of‑segment signal
- **Traces to:** AE-L1-3 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `player.ts` (`onDone` fired via `setTimeout` at `(countInBeats + end − fromBeat)·spb + 0.6` s)
- **Requirement:** When an `onDone` callback is supplied, the engine shall invoke it once, after the
  scheduled segment (plus a short tail) has elapsed, and shall mark playback as no longer playing.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a segment played with an `onDone` callback
> **WHEN** the segment (count‑in + notes + ≈0.6 s tail) elapses
> **THEN** `onDone` is invoked exactly once and `playing` is false.

> **GIVEN** playback is stopped before completion
> **THEN** the pending `onDone` timer is cancelled and `onDone` does not fire.

---

### AE-L2-8 — Playback stop and resource release
- **Traces to:** AE-L1-6 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `player.ts` (`stop()` stops oscillators, disconnects nodes, clears the node list and done‑timer)
- **Requirement:** Stopping playback shall halt and disconnect all scheduled audio nodes, clear any
  pending completion timer, and set the not‑playing state, leaving no audible tail or leaked node.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a song is playing
> **WHEN** `stop()` is called
> **THEN** all oscillators are stopped and disconnected, the node list is emptied, any done‑timer is cleared, and `playing` is false.

> **GIVEN** a new song is started while one is playing
> **THEN** the engine stops the previous playback before scheduling the new one (no overlap).

---

### AE-L2-9 — Single‑note preview
- **Traces to:** AE-L1-1 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** `player.ts` (`playNote(midi, durSec = 0.5)`)
- **Requirement:** The engine shall play a single note of a given MIDI value for a short default
  duration, for use by the voice ladder and the "tuned for you" preview.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a MIDI value *m*
> **WHEN** `playNote(m)` is called
> **THEN** a single ≈0.5 s tone at the frequency of *m* is heard.

---

### AE-L2-10 — Pitch detection pipeline
- **Traces to:** AE-L1-4 · **Priority:** Must
- **Revised:** v1.3 (the detector reads the band‑limited window, AE-L2-19)
- **Source:** `pitch.ts` (`PitchAnalyzer.analyze`, `PitchDetector.forFloat32Array`, `FFT_SIZE = 2048`; `hzToMidi`)
- **Requirement:** The engine shall analyse 2048‑sample time‑domain windows, band‑limited per AE-L2-19,
  with the McLeod pitch method and, for an accepted frame, report `{ hz, midi, clarity, rms }` where
  `midi` is a float (`69 + 12·log2(hz/440)`) and `rms` is the level of the unfiltered window.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** the tracker is running and a steady 440 Hz tone is present
> **WHEN** a frame is read
> **THEN** the frame reports `hz ≈ 440` and `midi ≈ 69.0` with a clarity value in [0, 1].

---

### AE-L2-11 — RMS noise gate
- **Traces to:** AE-L1-5 · **Priority:** Must
- **Revised:** v1.3 (the gate measures the band‑limited level)
- **Source:** `pitch.ts` (`MIN_BAND_RMS = 0.006`; returns `null` and clears `latest` below threshold)
- **Requirement:** The engine shall reject (report no pitch for) any frame whose band‑limited RMS level
  (AE-L2-19) is below 0.006, so quiet background noise does not register as singing. Measuring the
  level inside the singing band lets the gate sit lower for a quiet voice while hum, rumble, and hiss
  outside the band no longer open it.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** the tracker is running in a quiet room (band‑limited RMS < 0.006)
> **WHEN** a frame is read
> **THEN** the frame is `null` and no pitch is reported.

> **GIVEN** a loud 60 Hz hum (amplitude 0.1) and nobody singing
> **THEN** the frame is `null`: the hum lies outside the band and does not open the gate.

---

### AE-L2-12 — Clarity threshold and frequency bounds
- **Traces to:** AE-L1-5 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `pitch.ts` (`MIN_CLARITY = 0.8`, `MIN_HZ = 70`, `MAX_HZ = 1500`)
- **Requirement:** The engine shall reject a frame whose detector clarity is below 0.8 or whose
  frequency falls outside 70–1500 Hz, so uncertain or out‑of‑band detections are discarded.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a loud but inharmonic noise producing clarity < 0.8
> **WHEN** a frame is read
> **THEN** no pitch is reported.

> **GIVEN** a detected frequency below 70 Hz or above 1500 Hz
> **THEN** no pitch is reported.

---

### AE-L2-13 — Octave‑blip smoothing (median filter)
- **Traces to:** AE-L1-4, AE-L1-5 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `pitch.ts` (`MEDIAN_WINDOW = 5`; median of the last 5 MIDI values)
- **Requirement:** The reported MIDI value shall be the median of the most recent 5 accepted frames, so
  a single octave‑error blip cannot swing the reported pitch.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** four consecutive frames near MIDI 60 and one spurious frame near MIDI 72
> **WHEN** the fifth frame is read
> **THEN** the reported MIDI value remains near 60 (the median), not 72.

---

### AE-L2-14 — Microphone acquisition and denial propagation
- **Traces to:** AE-L1-6 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `pitch.ts` (`start()`: `getUserMedia` with `echoCancellation/noiseSuppression/autoGainControl: false`; throws `NotAllowedError` on denial)
- **Requirement:** The engine shall request the microphone with echo cancellation, noise suppression,
  and auto‑gain **disabled** (to preserve pitch fidelity), and shall propagate a permission‑denied
  error to the caller rather than swallowing it.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** the microphone permission is granted
> **WHEN** the tracker starts
> **THEN** the stream is acquired with EC/NS/AGC off and the tracker becomes running.

> **GIVEN** the microphone permission is denied
> **WHEN** the tracker starts
> **THEN** the call rejects with a `NotAllowedError` so the caller can present a fallback.

---

### AE-L2-15 — Audio‑context lifecycle and policy compliance
- **Traces to:** AE-L1-6 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `player.ts` (`audioCtx()` lazily creates and resumes a suspended context)
- **Requirement:** The engine shall create a single shared `AudioContext` lazily and resume it when
  suspended, so audio starts reliably after the first user gesture on mobile browsers.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** no audio has played yet
> **WHEN** the first playback or preview is triggered from a user gesture
> **THEN** the shared context is created and, if suspended, resumed, and sound is produced.

---

### AE-L2-16 — Tracker stop and history reset
- **Traces to:** AE-L1-6 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `pitch.ts` (`stop()`: stops tracks, disconnects nodes, clears history and `latest`)
- **Requirement:** Stopping the tracker shall stop the media tracks, disconnect the audio nodes, and
  clear the smoothing history and last frame, so no microphone remains active and no stale pitch
  survives into a later session.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a running tracker
> **WHEN** `stop()` is called
> **THEN** the microphone tracks are stopped, the tracker is no longer running, and a subsequent `read()` returns `null`.

---

### AE-L2-17 — Diagnostic tuner
- **Traces to:** AE-L1-7 · **Priority:** Could
- **Source:** `frontend/src/screens/tuner.ts` (route `#/tuner`)
- **Requirement:** The hidden tuner screen shall display, per detected frame, the nearest note name,
  the cents deviation on a needle (±50 cents mapped across the display), the clarity as a percentage,
  the level (RMS), and the frequency; it shall toggle listening on/off and, if the microphone is
  unavailable, indicate this inline without navigating away.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** the tuner is listening and a note ~25 cents sharp of A4 is sung
> **WHEN** a frame is read
> **THEN** the display shows "A4", the needle sits right of centre proportional to +25 cents, and clarity/level/frequency update.

> **GIVEN** no pitch is detected in a frame
> **THEN** the note display shows a neutral placeholder rather than a stale note.

> **GIVEN** the microphone is unavailable when listening is toggled on
> **THEN** the screen indicates the problem inline (e.g. "mic?") and does not crash or redirect.

### AE-L2-18 — Pipeline latency estimate
- **Traces to:** AE-L1-3, AE-L1-4 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** `audio/latency.ts` (`pipelineLatencyMs`, `MAX_DEVICE_LATENCY_MS = 500`); `pitch.ts` (`PitchTracker.pipelineLatencyMs`, input latency read from the microphone track's settings at start)
- **Requirement:** The engine shall estimate, in milliseconds, how long after the playback clock
  reaches a beat the singing for that beat is seen by the pitch detector, as the sum of:
  1. the **output delay** the browser reports for the audio context (`baseLatency + outputLatency`) —
     the child sings with what she hears, and she hears it this much after the clock;
  2. the **input delay** the browser reports for the microphone track (`latency` in its settings);
  3. **half the analysis window** (`windowSize / 2 / sampleRate`) — a detected pitch describes the
     middle of the window it was measured over.

  A figure the browser does not report, or reports as non‑finite or negative, shall count as 0. The
  device part (1 + 2) shall be capped at 500 ms so a bogus report cannot push scoring off the song.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a context reporting 20 ms base and 230 ms output latency, a microphone track reporting 40 ms, a 48 kHz sample rate and a 2048‑sample window
> **WHEN** the estimate is computed
> **THEN** it is ≈311.3 ms (290 ms device + 21.3 ms analysis).

> **GIVEN** a browser that reports none of the three latencies
> **THEN** the estimate is the analysis term alone (≈21.3 ms at 48 kHz, ≈23.2 ms at 44.1 kHz).

> **GIVEN** a reported latency that is `NaN`, infinite, or negative
> **THEN** that figure counts as 0.

> **GIVEN** reported output and input latencies totalling 2 s
> **THEN** the device part of the estimate is 500 ms.

### AE-L2-19 — Band‑limited detector input
- **Traces to:** AE-L1-5 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `audio/band-limit.ts` (`BandLimiter`, 4th‑order Butterworth high‑pass and low‑pass as cascaded biquads); `pitch.ts` (`BAND_LOW_HZ = 90`, `BAND_HIGH_HZ = 1500`)
- **Requirement:** Before the level gate and the pitch estimate, each analysis window shall be
  band‑limited to 90–1500 Hz by a 4th‑order Butterworth high‑pass at 90 Hz followed by a 4th‑order
  Butterworth low‑pass at 1500 Hz, computed for the context's sample rate. Sung fundamentals and their
  first harmonics lie inside that band; mains hum, handling rumble, breath, and hiss lie mostly outside
  it, so removing them keeps the detector confident about a voice it would otherwise lose in the noise.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a steady 440 Hz tone
> **WHEN** a window is band‑limited
> **THEN** its level changes by less than 0.5 dB.

> **GIVEN** a steady 60 Hz hum
> **THEN** its level is reduced by at least 12 dB.

> **GIVEN** a steady 6 kHz tone
> **THEN** its level over the whole window is reduced by at least 35 dB, and by at least 45 dB over the second half of the window, once the filter has settled from rest.

> **GIVEN** the same window band‑limited at 44.1 kHz and at 48 kHz
> **THEN** the three outcomes above hold at both sample rates.

### AE-L2-20 — Detection holds up in a noisy home
- **Traces to:** AE-L1-4, AE-L1-5 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `frontend/src/testing/noisy-home-acceptance.test.ts` (reference singer of SR-L2-18 through `PitchAnalyzer`, `NoteJudge`, and `noteLanded`)
- **Requirement:** Taken end to end, the engine shall keep hearing an in‑tune child through the
  ordinary interference of a home — a breathy voice, broadband hiss, mains hum, a quiet voice — and
  shall report no pitch at all when the same interference is present and nobody sings.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** the reference singer sings every bundled song with breath noise louder than her voice's fundamental (amplitude 0.1 against 0.08)
> **WHEN** the performances are judged
> **THEN** at least 90% of notes are landed.

> **GIVEN** she sings over broadband hiss of the same amplitude as her voice (0.08)
> **THEN** at least 90% of notes are landed.

> **GIVEN** she sings over a 60 Hz hum louder than her voice (amplitude 0.1)
> **THEN** at least 90% of notes are landed.

> **GIVEN** she sings very quietly (amplitude 0.012) in the reference room
> **THEN** at least 90% of notes are landed.

> **GIVEN** nobody sings while the hiss (0.08) or the hum (0.1) is present
> **THEN** no pitch frame is reported and no note is landed.

### AE-L2-21 — Fixed‑step analysis, independent of polling
- **Traces to:** AE-L1-4 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `pitch.ts` (`PitchAnalyzer.analyzeHops`, `PitchTracker.readHops`, `HOP_MS = 1000/60`, `HISTORY_SIZE = 8192`)
- **Requirement:** The engine shall analyse the microphone signal on a fixed grid of audio time — one
  2048‑sample window every 1/60 s (a *hop*) — however often or evenly it is polled. Each poll shall
  return, oldest first, one *observation* for every hop that has come due since the previous poll: a
  pitch frame or none, together with the age of the end of its window. The engine shall retain 8192
  samples of history for this (at least 128 ms beyond one window). A poll that arrives before the next
  hop is due shall return no observation; if more hops have come due than the history holds, only the
  most recent that fit shall be returned and the grid shall restart from there. A slow or stuttering
  display thereby samples the voice exactly as densely as a fast one.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** the engine is polled every 16.7 ms
> **THEN** every poll returns one observation of age ≈0.

> **GIVEN** the engine is polled 50 ms after the previous poll
> **THEN** it returns three observations, oldest first, with ages ≈33, ≈17 and ≈0 ms.

> **GIVEN** the engine is polled every 8.3 ms (a 120 Hz display)
> **THEN** every other poll returns one observation and the rest return none.

> **GIVEN** the engine is polled after a one‑second stall
> **THEN** it returns only the most recent hops its history holds (8 at 48 kHz), and the next poll 16.7 ms later returns one observation.

> **GIVEN** a tone that began 20 ms before a poll made 50 ms after the previous one
> **THEN** the oldest observation, whose window is mostly silence, reports no pitch and the newest reports the tone.
