# Audio Engine — L1 High-Level Requirements

**Subsystem code:** AE
**Specification level:** L1 (capability / high‑level)
**Baseline:** v1.0 · **Status:** Approved
**Detailed requirements:** [L2.md](./L2.md)
**Primary dependencies:** Song Library (SL, note data); consumed by Voice Personalization (VP), Singing Gameplay (SG), Scoring & Results (SR)

---

## 1. Purpose

The Audio Engine is TwinkleTune's on‑device sound and hearing layer. It **synthesises** a song's
melody from note data (never from recordings) and it **detects** the singer's pitch from the
microphone in real time. Because playback is synthesised, the engine can transpose a song into any key
and slow it down for practice with no audio artefacts and no additional assets — properties that make
"in your key", slow practice mode, and offline play possible.

## 2. Scope

### In scope

- Web Audio synthesis of a song's melody with a soft, toy‑piano‑like voice.
- Parameterised playback: semitone transposition, tempo/rate scaling, segment (from/until beat)
  playback, and a metronome count‑in.
- A musical position clock and an end‑of‑playback signal for gameplay synchronisation.
- One‑off single‑note preview tones.
- Real‑time microphone pitch detection (frequency → MIDI), with noise gating, clarity thresholding,
  and octave‑error smoothing.
- Microphone and `AudioContext` lifecycle, resource cleanup, and browser audio‑policy compliance.
- A hidden diagnostic tuner for verifying pitch accuracy.

### Out of scope

- Capturing the voice range (the *use* of pitch detection to find a comfortable low/high) — **VP**.
- Live per‑note scoring and octave folding — **SR**; the engine reports raw pitch, not correctness.
- The gameplay render loop that positions pills and the avatar — **SG**; it consumes the clock and
  pitch frames.
- Song content and its invariants — **SL**.

## 3. Context & dependencies

Songs are stored as note data (`{ midi, start, dur, syll }`, times in beats). The engine turns a note
into a frequency via equal‑temperament (`440·2^((m−69)/12)`) and schedules Web Audio oscillators. On
the input side, it uses the McLeod pitch method (via the `pitchy` library) over 2048‑sample windows
and converts detected frequency back to a floating‑point MIDI value. The same `AudioContext` is shared
between playback and analysis and must be resumed from a user gesture to satisfy mobile browser policy.

## 4. High-level requirements

| ID | Requirement | Priority | Rationale |
|----|-------------|----------|-----------|
| **AE-L1-1** | The system shall synthesise a song's melody on the device from note data alone, with no audio recordings, and shall support one‑off single‑note previews. | Must | No‑audio‑assets synthesis is what enables artefact‑free re‑keying, offline play, and the privacy story. |
| **AE-L1-2** | The system shall play a song with a caller‑specified semitone transposition, playback rate, start/stop beat bounds, and count‑in, producing no artefacts across the supported ranges. | Must | Powers "in your key", slow practice mode, and practise‑the‑tricky‑part segment loops. |
| **AE-L1-3** | The system shall expose a musical position clock (in beats, negative during the count‑in) and signal when a scheduled segment has finished. | Must | Gameplay visuals and scoring must stay frame‑accurately synchronised to the audio. |
| **AE-L1-4** | The system shall detect the singer's pitch from the microphone in real time, reporting frequency, a floating‑point MIDI value, a confidence, and a level for each polled frame. | Must | Live feedback and scoring depend on a stable per‑frame pitch estimate. |
| **AE-L1-5** | The system shall be robust to background noise and momentary octave errors, suppressing frames that are too quiet or too uncertain and smoothing brief pitch blips. | Must | Budget tablets in noisy homes must not register noise as singing or flip octaves mid‑note. |
| **AE-L1-6** | The system shall manage microphone and audio‑context resources cleanly, comply with browser audio‑activation policy, and surface a microphone‑denied condition to callers. | Must | Resource leaks, silent‑audio bugs, and unhandled permission denials would break core play. |
| **AE-L1-7** | The system shall provide a diagnostic tuner that displays the detected note, cents deviation, clarity, and level for verifying pitch accuracy. | Could | Enables field verification against a reference instrument without external tooling. |

## 5. Assumptions & constraints

- Detection window is 2048 samples; pitch is accepted only for 70 Hz ≤ f ≤ 1500 Hz.
- Equal temperament, A4 = 440 Hz, MIDI 60 = C4.
- The count‑in default is 4 beats; slow practice rate is 0.7×.
- A single shared `AudioContext` is created lazily and resumed on demand.
