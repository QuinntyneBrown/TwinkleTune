# Scoring & Results — L1 High-Level Requirements

**Subsystem code:** SR
**Specification level:** L1 (capability / high‑level)
**Baseline:** v1.0 · **Status:** Approved
**Detailed requirements:** [L2.md](./L2.md)
**Primary dependencies:** Singing Gameplay (SG, frame capture), Rewards & Progression (RP), Family High Scores (HS), Coaching & Encouragement (CE), Duet Multiplayer (DM)

---

## 1. Purpose

Scoring & Results turns a performance into meaning — forgivingly, and in the service of confidence. It
judges singing by **pitch class** (octave‑forgivingly), decides which notes were "landed" by a generous
majority‑of‑frames rule, and summarises a run into an accuracy, a star rating, and warm, dimensioned
feedback (pitch, timing, braveness). It finds the one phrase worth practising — only when a child was
genuinely shaky — and offers it as an *invitation*, never a penalty. Finally it presents the results
screen and, when eligible, submits the family high score.

## 2. Scope

### In scope

- Octave‑folded pitch comparison (cents, pitch class).
- The "landed note" decision rule and its minimum‑sample guard.
- Summarisation: accuracy, overall stars, longest in‑song streak, sparkles, and the pitch/timing/
  braveness dimensions.
- "Tricky part" detection and its genuine‑shakiness threshold.
- The results screen: headline, stars, score card, badge reveal, coach message, and practise‑the‑tricky‑
  part invitation.
- Eligibility and triggering of a family high‑score submission and the "new family record" moment.
- No‑mic (unscored) handling of results.

### Out of scope

- Live per‑note frame capture during play — **SG** (SR consumes the captured `NoteResult` list).
- Persisting sparkles/bests/badges/streaks — **RP**; awarding badge *rules* — **RP** (SR reveals them).
- The high‑score store, upsert, and boards — **HS** (SR triggers submission and reads the "improved" flag).
- The duet combined‑result panel — **DM** (SR hosts it on the results screen).

## 3. Context & dependencies

Gameplay accumulates, per note, the frames observed, the frames on‑pitch, and the first‑hit fraction.
SR's `summarize()` reduces that list into a `SongSummary` that the results screen renders and that the
store records. Scoring is deliberately forgiving: children naturally sing in their own octave, so
comparison is by pitch class, and a note need only be on‑pitch for half of the frames it was heard.

## 4. High-level requirements

| ID | Requirement | Priority | Rationale |
|----|-------------|----------|-----------|
| **SR-L1-1** | The system shall judge sung pitch against target pitch octave‑forgivingly, comparing pitch class so that the correct note sung in any octave counts as correct. | Must | Children naturally sing an octave off; octave folding is central to fairness and confidence. |
| **SR-L1-2** | The system shall decide a note "landed" by a forgiving rule: on‑pitch for at least half of the frames it was heard, with a minimum number of observed frames. | Must | A generous, sample‑guarded rule avoids both harsh scoring and noise‑driven false positives. |
| **SR-L1-3** | The system shall summarise a performance into an accuracy, an overall star rating, a longest‑streak, a sparkle total, and separate pitch, timing, and braveness assessments. | Must | Multi‑dimensional, positive feedback celebrates effort along several axes, not one pass/fail. |
| **SR-L1-4** | The system shall identify the single weakest phrase as a "tricky part" only when it is genuinely shaky, and shall offer it as an optional low‑stakes practice, never as a penalty. | Must | Turning difficulty into a friendly invitation is the product's pedagogy (P1). |
| **SR-L1-5** | The system shall present results in an encouraging, celebratory way tailored to the outcome, with no negative framing. | Must | The results moment is where confidence is reinforced or undermined (P1). |
| **SR-L1-6** | The system shall submit the performance as a family high score when — and only when — it is a scored run by a server‑linked singer on a server song, and shall celebrate a new family record. | Should | Family scores are a motivating, private, opt‑in‑by‑connectivity feature. |
| **SR-L1-7** | The system shall treat a no‑microphone run as unscored — awarding no stars and no high score — while still celebrating that the child sang. | Must | Fairness: a fun run must not inflate records; kindness: it still deserves celebration. |

## 5. Assumptions & constraints

- Hit tolerance is ±50 cents after octave folding.
- Overall/pitch stars: ≥0.85 accuracy → 3, ≥0.6 → 2, else 1.
- A landed note requires ≥3 observed frames and ≥50% on‑pitch frames.
- Sparkles earned = landed notes × 10.
- The tricky part is nulled unless the weakest qualifying phrase (≥3 notes) landed under 75%.
