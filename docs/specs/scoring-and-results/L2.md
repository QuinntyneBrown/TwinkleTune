## 2. Detailed requirements

### SR-L2-1 — Octave‑folded pitch comparison
- **Traces to:** SR-L1-1 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `scoring.ts` (`foldCents`); `scoring.test.ts`
- **Requirement:** The system shall compare sung and target pitch by pitch class, returning the
  signed cents difference folded into (−600, +600], so an exact pitch class in any octave reads as 0.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a target of MIDI 60
> **WHEN** the child sings MIDI 72 (C5) or MIDI 48 (C3)
> **THEN** the folded difference is 0 cents.

> **GIVEN** a target of MIDI 60
> **WHEN** the child sings MIDI 61
> **THEN** the folded difference is +100 cents; MIDI 67 folds to −500 cents (a fifth up reads as a fourth down).

---

### SR-L2-2 — "Landed note" decision
- **Traces to:** SR-L1-2 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `scoring.ts` (`noteLanded`, `HIT_TOLERANCE_CENTS = 50`, min 3 frames, ≥50%); `scoring.test.ts`
- **Requirement:** A note shall be counted as landed only if it was observed for at least 3 frames and
  was on‑pitch (within ±50 cents, octave‑folded) for at least half of those frames.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a note observed for 10 frames, on‑pitch for 5
> **THEN** it is landed.

> **GIVEN** a note observed for 10 frames, on‑pitch for 4
> **THEN** it is not landed.

> **GIVEN** a note observed for only 2 frames (both on‑pitch)
> **THEN** it is not landed (minimum‑sample guard).

---

### SR-L2-3 — Accuracy and overall stars
- **Traces to:** SR-L1-3 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `scoring.ts` (`accuracy = landed/total`, 0 when no notes; `starsFromRatio(accuracy, 0.85, 0.6)`)
- **Requirement:** The system shall compute accuracy as landed ÷ total notes and award overall stars:
  3 at ≥0.85, 2 at ≥0.6, otherwise 1; pitch stars shall equal overall stars.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** every note landed
> **THEN** accuracy is 1.0 and stars is 3.

> **GIVEN** 60% of notes landed
> **THEN** stars is 2.

> **GIVEN** 40% of notes landed
> **THEN** stars is 1.

---

### SR-L2-4 — Longest in‑song streak
- **Traces to:** SR-L1-3 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** `scoring.ts` (`maxStreak` = longest consecutive landed run); `scoring.test.ts`
- **Requirement:** The summary shall report the longest run of consecutive landed notes.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a run that misses every 5th note
> **WHEN** summarised
> **THEN** the max streak is 4.

---

### SR-L2-5 — Timing assessment
- **Traces to:** SR-L1-3 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** `scoring.ts` (of notes ever hit, fraction with `firstHitFrac ≤ 0.4`, `starsFromRatio(_, 0.8, 0.5)`; 1 if none hit)
- **Requirement:** Timing stars shall reflect how often a hit note was caught early (first hit within
  the first 40% of the note): 3 at ≥0.8, 2 at ≥0.5, else 1; if no note was ever hit, timing is 1 star.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** every hit note was caught within the first 40%
> **THEN** timing stars is 3.

> **GIVEN** no note was ever hit
> **THEN** timing stars is 1.

---

### SR-L2-6 — Braveness (high‑note) assessment
- **Traces to:** SR-L1-3 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** `scoring.ts` (high notes = MIDI ≥ songMax − 2; fraction landed via `starsFromRatio(_, 0.9, 0.6)`; 3 if no high notes)
- **Requirement:** Braveness stars shall reflect how the song's highest notes (within 2 semitones of
  the top) went: 3 at ≥0.9 landed, 2 at ≥0.6, else 1; a song with no qualifying high notes yields 3.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** the highest notes of the song were all missed
> **THEN** braveness stars is 1.

> **GIVEN** the highest notes were nearly all landed
> **THEN** braveness stars is 3.

---

### SR-L2-7 — Tricky‑part detection
- **Traces to:** SR-L1-4 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `scoring.ts` (weakest phrase with ≥3 notes by landed ratio; nulled if worst ratio ≥ 0.75); `scoring.test.ts`
- **Requirement:** The system shall select, among phrases with at least 3 notes, the one with the
  lowest landed ratio as the tricky part — but only if that ratio is under 0.75; otherwise there is no
  tricky part.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** one phrase was entirely missed while others were strong
> **WHEN** summarised
> **THEN** that phrase is the tricky part and its lyric is reported.

> **GIVEN** every phrase landed at least 75%
> **THEN** there is no tricky part (null).

---

### SR-L2-8 — Sparkles earned
- **Traces to:** SR-L1-3 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `scoring.ts` (`sparkles = landed · 10`)
- **Requirement:** The summary shall award ten sparkles per landed note.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** 12 notes landed
> **THEN** the summary reports 120 sparkles.

---

### SR-L2-9 — Outcome‑tailored headline
- **Traces to:** SR-L1-5 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** `results.ts` (headline: no‑mic → "What a fun sing‑along"; 3★ "Super Singing"; 2★ "Beautiful Singing"; else "Brave Singing")
- **Requirement:** The results headline shall be selected by outcome and personalised with the singer's
  name, always positive (even at one star: "Brave Singing").

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a 1‑star scored result for "Ada"
> **THEN** the headline reads "Brave Singing, Ada! 🎉" with the song title beneath.

---

### SR-L2-10 — Star row and scored score card
- **Traces to:** SR-L1-5 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** `results.ts` (three star slots filled by `stars`; score card "You landed X of Y notes!", accuracy %, pitch/timing/braveness marks)
- **Requirement:** For a scored run, the results screen shall show a three‑star row filled to the earned
  star count and a score card reporting notes landed of total, the accuracy percentage, and the three
  dimension marks.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a scored result of 2 stars, 14 of 20 notes, 70% accuracy
> **THEN** two stars are filled, and the card shows "You landed 14 of 20 notes!", a 70% fill, and the pitch/timing/braveness marks.

---

### SR-L2-11 — No‑mic results variant
- **Traces to:** SR-L1-7 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `results.ts` (no‑mic: star row suppressed; card "You sang the whole song!" with an invitation to let Twinkle listen)
- **Requirement:** For a no‑mic run, the results screen shall suppress stars and the scored card,
  showing instead a celebration of completing the song and a gentle invitation to sing with the
  microphone next time.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a no‑mic result
> **THEN** no stars are shown, and the card celebrates singing the whole song and invites a scored run next time.

---

### SR-L2-12 — Badge reveal on results
- **Traces to:** SR-L1-5 · **Priority:** Should · **See also:** RP
- **Source:** `results.ts` (iterates `state.lastNewBadges` → badge name/emoji)
- **Requirement:** Newly earned badges from this play shall be revealed on the results screen with their
  emoji and name.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** this play earned the "Show Stopper" badge
> **THEN** the results screen reveals "🎭 New badge: Show Stopper!".

---

### SR-L2-13 — Coach message selection
- **Traces to:** SR-L1-5 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** `results.ts` (no‑mic / tricky‑lyric / 3★ / default / duet variants)
- **Requirement:** The coach message shall be chosen by outcome: invite a scored run (no‑mic); name the
  tricky phrase and invite practice (when a tricky part exists); celebrate a clean run at 3 stars;
  otherwise offer warm encouragement; in duet mode, celebrate singing together.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a scored result with a tricky phrase "Up above the world so high"
> **THEN** the coach says the notes sparkled and invites practising just that bit.

> **GIVEN** a 3‑star result with no tricky part
> **THEN** the coach celebrates the clean run and suggests a brave new song.

---

### SR-L2-14 — Practise‑the‑tricky‑part invitation
- **Traces to:** SR-L1-4 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** `results.ts` (CTA shown when `!noMic && trickyPhrase !== null && !duet` → `#/sing?song=<id>&practice=<phrase>&slow=1`)
- **Requirement:** When a scored, non‑duet run has a tricky part, the results screen shall offer a
  prominent, optional "Practice the tricky part" action that opens that phrase in slow practice mode.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a scored result with tricky phrase index 2
> **WHEN** the invitation is shown and tapped
> **THEN** it navigates to `#/sing?song=<id>&practice=2&slow=1`.

> **GIVEN** a result with no tricky part
> **THEN** no practice invitation is shown.

---

### SR-L2-15 — High‑score submission eligibility and record moment
- **Traces to:** SR-L1-6, SR-L1-7 · **Priority:** Should · **See also:** HS
- **Source:** `results.ts` (submit only when `!noMic && profile.singerId && isServerSongId(songId)`; on `improved` → toast "🏆 New family record!")
- **Requirement:** The results screen shall submit a family high score only for a scored run by a
  server‑linked singer on a server (GUID) song; if the submission improves the family record, it shall
  celebrate "New family record!". A no‑mic run, a local‑only profile, or a bundled song shall not
  submit.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a scored run by a server‑linked singer on a server song that beats the prior best
> **WHEN** results render
> **THEN** the score is submitted and a "🏆 New family record!" celebration appears.

> **GIVEN** a no‑mic run, or a local‑only profile, or a bundled song id
> **THEN** no high‑score submission is attempted.

---

### SR-L2-16 — Result actions and navigation
- **Traces to:** SR-L1-5 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** `results.ts` (Sing again / New song / Home; duet: Another duet / New song / Home)
- **Requirement:** The results screen shall offer to sing the same song again, pick a new song, or go
  home; in duet mode it shall offer another duet (ending the current session) instead of a solo replay.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a solo result
> **WHEN** "Sing again" is chosen
> **THEN** the same song reopens for a fresh performance.

---

### SR-L2-17 — Celebratory visuals
- **Traces to:** SR-L1-5 · **Priority:** Could
- **Source:** `results.ts`, `rendering/reactive-scene.ts`
- **Requirement:** The results screen shall present star-count-sensitive celebratory particles and
  animated stars appropriate to a joyful moment. Particles shall prefer WebGPU, fall back to Canvas2D,
  remain decorative to assistive technology, and become a still scene under reduced motion.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** any results screen
> **WHEN** it renders
> **THEN** celebratory motion accompanies the outcome when motion is allowed, while the result content remains semantic DOM.

> **GIVEN** reduced motion is requested or WebGPU is unavailable
> **THEN** the same result remains complete using the still or Canvas2D presentation without an error.
