## 2. Detailed requirements

### CE-L2-1 — Mascot rendering with expressive state
- **Traces to:** CE-L1-1 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** `parts.ts` (`mascotSVG(cls, happy)`; a star face with eyes, cheeks, and a mouth that widens when happy)
- **Requirement:** The system shall render Twinkle as a star‑shaped mascot with a friendly face, and
  shall offer a happier expression variant for celebratory contexts; the mascot shall be decorative to
  assistive technology.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a screen that shows Twinkle
> **WHEN** the mascot renders in its happy state
> **THEN** a wider smile is shown, and the mascot is marked decorative (aria‑hidden).

---

### CE-L2-2 — Coaching voice across screens
- **Traces to:** CE-L1-1 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** coach bubbles in `welcome.ts`, `home.ts`, `voice-setup.ts`, `sing.ts`, `tips.ts`, `results.ts`
- **Requirement:** A context‑appropriate coach line ("bubble") shall be present on the welcome, home,
  voice‑setup, singing, tips, and results screens, always in Twinkle's warm first‑person voice.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** the home screen for a singer with no voice range
> **WHEN** it renders
> **THEN** the coach bubble invites her to find her voice first, in an encouraging tone.

---

### CE-L2-3 — In‑song cheers and phrase cues
- **Traces to:** CE-L1-2 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** `sing.ts` (`CHEERS` shown per landed note; `PHRASE_CHEERS[pi % 5]` on phrase change)
- **Requirement:** On each landed note the system shall show a short, varied, positive cheer; on each
  new phrase the coach bubble shall show an encouraging cue. No missed note shall produce a negative
  message.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a note is landed
> **THEN** a positive cheer (e.g. "Perfect! ✨", "Great! 🌟") appears near the star.

> **GIVEN** the song advances to a new phrase
> **THEN** an encouraging cue appears in the coach bubble.

---

### CE-L2-4 — Tips library structure
- **Traces to:** CE-L1-3 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** `tips.ts` (`CATEGORIES`: All, Breathing, Brave Voice, Warm‑ups, Show Time; hero tip; tip cards); `tips-data.ts`
- **Requirement:** The tips screen shall present a featured hero tip and a filterable set of tip cards
  organised by category (Breathing, Brave Voice, Warm‑ups, Show Time), each card showing an icon,
  title, short line, and estimated time.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** the tips screen with the "All" filter active
> **WHEN** a category (e.g. "Brave Voice") is selected
> **THEN** only that category's tips are shown, and the hero tip and quote remain.

---

### CE-L2-5 — Tip walk‑through and unconditional reward
- **Traces to:** CE-L1-3 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** `tips.ts` (`openTip`: step list; "I did it! ✨" → `sparkles += 5`, toast "+5 sparkles ✨"; no correctness check)
- **Requirement:** Opening a tip shall show its ordered steps and an "I did it!" action; completing a
  tip shall grant exactly 5 sparkles unconditionally (no performance check) and celebrate it.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** an open tip
> **WHEN** the child taps "I did it!"
> **THEN** 5 sparkles are added, a "+5 sparkles ✨" celebration appears, and the tip closes — with no pass/fail evaluation.

---

### CE-L2-6 — Daily tip rotation
- **Traces to:** CE-L1-4 · **Priority:** Could
- **Source:** `tips-data.ts` (`tipOfTheDay`: `[HERO_TIP, ...TIPS]` indexed by day‑of‑year mod 7); `home.ts` (tip‑of‑the‑day card)
- **Requirement:** The system shall select a "tip of the day" deterministically from the set of 7 tips
  by calendar day, and surface it on the home dashboard.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a fixed calendar date
> **WHEN** the tip of the day is computed
> **THEN** it is the same tip every time for that date, cycling through all 7 over 7 days.

---

### CE-L2-7 — Tip content set
- **Traces to:** CE-L1-3 · **Priority:** Could
- **Source:** `tips-data.ts` (HERO_TIP "Breathe like a balloon" + 6 tips: Siren, Stand like a star, Bumblebee hum, "Mistakes are sparkle dust", Tiny mouse/big lion, Sing to your teddy)
- **Requirement:** The product shall ship a hero breathing tip and six additional tips spanning
  breathing, warm‑up, bravery, and performance themes, each with playful steps.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** the shipped tips
> **WHEN** they are enumerated
> **THEN** there are 7 in total (1 hero + 6), covering breathing, warm‑ups, brave voice, and show‑time themes.

---

### CE-L2-8 — Encouragement‑only guarantee
- **Traces to:** CE-L1-5 · **Priority:** Must
- **Source:** product‑wide copy; `results.ts` (lowest outcome is "Brave Singing"); `sing.ts` (misses reset streak silently); tips (no fail state)
- **Requirement:** No screen, message, toast, headline, or reward interaction shall tell the child she
  failed, got something wrong, or lost; the lowest scored outcome shall still be framed positively
  ("Brave Singing"), missed notes shall pass without comment, and difficulty shall be reframed as an
  invitation to practise.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** the worst possible scored result
> **WHEN** the results screen renders
> **THEN** the headline is still positive ("Brave Singing") and the coach offers warm next steps, with no failure language.

> **GIVEN** a series of missed notes during a song
> **THEN** the streak simply resets and no "wrong"/"miss"/"fail" message is shown.

---

### CE-L2-9 — Positive celebration language
- **Traces to:** CE-L1-2, CE-L1-5 · **Priority:** Should
- **Source:** `results.ts` (headlines, coach lines), `sing.ts` (streak toasts), tips (quote "your voice is one of a kind — that's your superpower")
- **Requirement:** Celebration copy (headlines, streak milestones, coach lines, quotes) shall be warm,
  affirming, and child‑appropriate.

**Acceptance criteria**
> **GIVEN** a streak milestone or a completed song
> **WHEN** the celebration appears
> **THEN** it uses affirming, positive language appropriate for a young child.
