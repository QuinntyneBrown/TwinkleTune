# Coaching & Encouragement — L1 High-Level Requirements

**Subsystem code:** CE
**Specification level:** L1 (capability / high‑level)
**Baseline:** v1.0 · **Status:** Approved
**Detailed requirements:** [L2.md](./L2.md)
**Primary dependencies:** Singing Gameplay (SG), Scoring & Results (SR), Rewards & Progression (RP), Application Shell (AS)

---

## 1. Purpose

Coaching & Encouragement is the product's soul: the star coach **Twinkle** and the guarantee that **the
only feedback that exists is encouragement.** It gives the app a consistent, warm voice; delivers
in‑the‑moment cheer while a child sings; offers a small library of warm‑ups and confidence tips framed
as games; and enforces, as a cross‑cutting rule, that nothing in the product ever tells a child she
failed. Difficulty is always reframed as an invitation ("mistakes are sparkle dust").

## 2. Scope

### In scope

- The Twinkle mascot persona and its presence as a coaching voice across screens.
- In‑the‑moment encouragement during singing (per‑note cheers, phrase cues) — content ownership (SG
  triggers them).
- The tips library: categories, a featured hero tip, tip cards, and a step‑by‑step tip walk‑through.
- Unconditional, participation‑based rewarding of tips.
- A daily rotating tip.
- The encouragement‑only guarantee: positive celebration language and reframing of difficulty, applied
  product‑wide.

### Out of scope

- The gameplay loop that fires cheers and the results screen that shows coach messages — **SG / SR**
  (CE owns the *content and tone*; those subsystems own the *timing and placement*).
- The sparkle economy the tip reward feeds into — **RP**.
- Practise‑the‑tricky‑part mechanics — **SR / SG** (CE owns its non‑punitive framing).

## 3. Context & dependencies

Twinkle appears as an SVG mascot and as a "coach bubble" that speaks context‑appropriate lines on the
welcome, home, voice‑setup, singing, tips, and results screens. Cheers, phrase cues, celebration
headlines, and toasts are all authored to be unconditionally positive. Tips are static content with a
deterministic daily rotation; completing one grants a small, fixed sparkle reward with no correctness
check.

## 4. High-level requirements

| ID | Requirement | Priority | Rationale |
|----|-------------|----------|-----------|
| **CE-L1-1** | The system shall present a consistent, friendly mascot (Twinkle) as the app's coaching persona across screens, including an expressive "happy" state. | Should | A single warm character gives the app identity and makes guidance feel like a friend, not a grader. |
| **CE-L1-2** | The system shall deliver in‑the‑moment encouragement while singing — varied per‑note cheers and phrase cues — and never a negative or failure message. | Must | Continuous positive feedback is the core of the confidence‑building loop (P1). |
| **CE-L1-3** | The system shall provide a library of warm‑up and confidence tips, framed as playful games and rewarded unconditionally on completion. | Should | Skill and confidence building outside the scored loop; participation, not performance, is rewarded. |
| **CE-L1-4** | The system shall surface a daily tip that rotates deterministically by date. | Could | A fresh, predictable "tip of the day" gives a gentle reason to return. |
| **CE-L1-5** | The system shall guarantee that all feedback and copy presented to the child is positive and reframes difficulty as opportunity, never as failure. | Must | The encouragement‑only ethos (P1) is a defining, sellable product promise and a safety property for a child audience. |

## 5. Assumptions & constraints

- Completing a tip grants exactly 5 sparkles, with no correctness check.
- The daily tip rotates across a fixed set of 7 tips (a hero tip plus 6 others) by day‑of‑year.
- Cheers and phrase cues are drawn from fixed, all‑positive rotations.
